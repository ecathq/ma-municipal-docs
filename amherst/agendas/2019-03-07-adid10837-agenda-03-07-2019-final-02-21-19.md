1 SECTION 5.3: PUBLIC FORUM – Not later than March 15, but before the Town Manager submits a proposed
budget to the Town Council, the President of the Town Council with the cooperation of the Town Manager shall
call at least 1 public forum on the topic of the proposed budget. This forum is intended for the Town Council and
the Town Manager to present priorities, context based on prior years’ budgets, revenue and expenditure forecasts,
and other relevant information, and to solicit feedback from the public.
