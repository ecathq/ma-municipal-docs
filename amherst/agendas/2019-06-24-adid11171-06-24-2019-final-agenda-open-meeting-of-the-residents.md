Town of Amherst
Open Meeting of the Residents
Regarding Studio Apartment Supportive Housing – 132 Northampton Road
Monday, June 24, 2019 6:30 p.m.
Large Activity Room
Bangs Center
70 Boltwood Walk
Agenda
1. Call to Order
The purpose of the meeting is to hear presentation and discussion on the proposed Studio Apartment
Supportive Housing – 132 Northampton Road
2. Presentations
a. Recommendation of the Community Preservation Act Committee – Nate
Budington, Chair, Community Preservation Act Committee (5 Minutes)
b. Project description and response to known questions – Valley CDC (20 minutes)
c. Statement from the Amherst Affordable Housing Trust – John Hornik, Chair (5
minutes)
d. Abutters and Neighbors – TBD (30 minutes)
3. Councilor Questions (Clarifying)
4. Public Comments and Questions
Residents are welcome to express their views for up to 3 minutes. If questions arise that are able to be
answered during the meeting, the Town Council President will recognize the person(s) who will
respond. All questions will be recorded and, to the extent possible, answers will be provided on the
website by the end of the week. Otherwise, the Town Council will not engage in a dialogue or comment
on a matter raised during Public Comment.

6. Topics Not Reasonably Anticipated by the Chair 48 Hours in Advance of the Meeting
7. Adjourn
