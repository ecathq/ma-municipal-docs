1. Call to Order – The Town Council will convene at 5:00 p.m. to begin review of the
Town Manager’s evaluation documents. Regular business will commence at 6:30
p.m. and the Council will return to the Town Manager’s evaluation after regular
business is completed.

4. General Public Comment - General public comments on matters other than those
under agenda items 6a(3) will occur during the period of Regular Business starting at
6:30 PM. Public comments on that Agenda item will be taken at the relevant time
during the meeting. Residents are welcome to express their views for 1 to 3 minutes,
at the discretion of the Town Council President, based upon the number of people
who want to speak. The Council will not engage in a dialogue or comment on a
matter raised during General Public Comments.
