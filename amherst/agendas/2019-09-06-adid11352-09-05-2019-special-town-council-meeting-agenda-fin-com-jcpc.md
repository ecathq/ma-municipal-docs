Town of Amherst
Special Meeting of the Town Council
Thursday, September 5, 2019
7:00 p.m.
Town Room, Town Hall
4 Boltwood Avenue
Agenda
1. Call to Order
2. General Public Comment
General public comments on matters other than those listed on the agenda. Residents are welcome to
express their views for up to 3 minutes, at the discretion of the Town Council President, based upson the
number of people who wish to speak. The Council will not engage in a dialogue or comment on a matter
raised during General Public Comment.
3. Presentations and Discussion
a. Joint meeting of the Finance Committee and Joint Capital Planning Committee:
(1) Presentation and discussion of current status of the following possible
building projects:
 Elementary School
 Jones Library addition or renovations
 Fire Station
 Department of Public Works Facility
(2) Consideration of a process to provide guidance to the Council when it will
have to make decisions regarding these possible projects
(3) Demonstration and discussion of the current version of the financial analysis
tool for major building projects
(4) Financial Considerations of proposed “Percent for Art” Bylaw
(5) Consequences of these proposals for funds for other capital needs, including
roads and sidewalks
(6) Development of a 5-year capital plan
(7) Other business not aniticipated by the Chair 48 hours in advance of the
meeting
4. Topics Not Reasonably Anticipated by the President 48 Hours in Advance of the
meeting
5. Adjourn
