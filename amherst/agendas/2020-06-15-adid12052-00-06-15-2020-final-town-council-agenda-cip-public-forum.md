2. Capital Improvement Program Public Forum – Charter Sec. 5.7(d)
Per Charter Sec. 1.7, a Public Forum shall mean a meeting during which more than one-half
of the meeting time on the agenda is devoted to public comment.
Public Participation: Residents are welcome to express their views for up to 3
minutes, at the discretion of the Town Council President, based on the number of
people who wish to speak. The Council President, or others recognized by the
President, may answer questions during the hearing.
