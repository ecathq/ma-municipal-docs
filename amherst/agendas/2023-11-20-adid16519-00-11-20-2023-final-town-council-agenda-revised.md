Note: Members of the public may attend virtually via Zoom, by phone by calling phone (301)
715-8592, enter webinar ID: 879 5845 4126, or in person. The meeting will also be live
broadcast on Amherst Media channel 17 and https://amherstmedia.org/live-stream
The regular Town Council meeting will convene at 5:00 p.m.
From 5:00-6:30 p.m. the Town Council will hold a reading period to review the Town
Manager’s evaluation documents.
At 6:30 p.m. the Town Council will recess the regular meeting for the 6:30 p.m. Public
Forum on the FY25 Budget and 7:00 p.m. Public Forum on Supplemental Budget
Appropriations
The regular meeting will resume following the public forums.
