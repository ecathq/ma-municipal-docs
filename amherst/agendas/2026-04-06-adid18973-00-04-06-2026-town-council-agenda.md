4. General Public Comment – Residents are welcome to express their views for up to 3
minutes, at the discretion of the Town Council President, based upon the number of
people who wish to speak. If there are more than 10 people wishing to speak, the
public comment will be 2 minutes for each speaker. The Council will not engage in
dialogue or comment on a matter raised during General Public Comment. Public
comments are not reflective of the opinions of the Town Council.
