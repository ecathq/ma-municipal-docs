Amherst Town Council — Activity by Year

How much the council took up each year, and how often it split. Counts cover formal motions in the Unofficial Record of Votes; 2026 is a partial year (records through mid-2026).

Motions voted on per year

A “motion” is one formal vote — adopting an order, making an appointment, amending a bylaw, and so on. Taller bars mean a busier council.

How often the council split

Share of each year's motions that were contested — i.e. not unanimous (at least one No or abstention). Higher means more disagreement, independent of how much they voted on.

Year by year

Meetings counts distinct meeting dates with a recorded vote. “Per meeting” is motions ÷ meetings.
