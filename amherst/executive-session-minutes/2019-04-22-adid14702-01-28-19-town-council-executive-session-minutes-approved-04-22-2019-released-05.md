Town of Amherst
Town Council Meeting
Monday, January 28, 2019
Town Room, Town Hall
4 Boltwood Avenue
Executive Session Minutes
President Griesemer called the executive session to order at 10:34 p.m. pursuant to the provisions of G.L.
c. 30A, sec. 21(a)(1) to discuss the purchase, exchange, lease or value of real property if an open meeting
will have a detrimental effect on the negotiating position of this public body.
Councilors present: Councilors Bahl-Milne, Brewer, DeAngelis, DuMont, Griesemer, Hanneke, Pam,
Ross, Ryan, Schoen, Schreiber, Steinberg, Swartz.
Councilors absent: None
Councilors participating remotely: None
Others present: Town Manager Paul Bockelman, Assistant Town Manager David Ziomek, Clerk of the
Council Margaret Nartowicz, Economic Development Director Geoff Kravitz.
Assistant Town Manager David Ziomek and Economic Development Director Geoff Kravitz gave a
presentation regarding the Hickory Ridge property.
Main points discussed included:
• Overview of uses, mix of conservation & recreation
• Connection between residential developments
• Multiple stakeholders coming together with vision for the property
• Achieving multiple municipal goals
• Potential discussion with partners, public & private entities
• Rare & endangered species & riverfront buffer
• Zoning
Councilors discussed executive session rules.
Minutes approved by Town Council on April 22, 2019. Minutes released on May 16, 2022.
