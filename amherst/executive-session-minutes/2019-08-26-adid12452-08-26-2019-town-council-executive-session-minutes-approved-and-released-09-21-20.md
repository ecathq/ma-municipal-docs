Town of Amherst
Town Council Meeting
August 26, 2019
Town Room, Town Hall
4 Boltwood Avenue
Executive Session Minutes
President Griesemer called the executive session to order at 11:00 p.m. pursuant to the provisions of
MGL c. 30A, sec. 21(a)(3) to discuss strategy with respect to collective bargaining, units SEIU, DPW,
DPW Supervisors, Police Patrol, and Police Supervisors, as the chair declares that an open meeting may
have a detrimental effect on the bargaining position of the public body, and (2) to conduct a strategy
session in preparation for negotiations with nonunion personnel, Town Manager Paul Bockelman, and (2)
to conduct contract negotiations with nonunion personnel, Town Manager Paul Bockelman.
Councilors Present: Bahl-Milne, Brewer, De Angelis, DuMont, Griesemer, Hanneke, Pam, Ross, Ryan,
Schoen, Schreiber, Swartz
Councilors Absent: Steinberg
Councilors participating remotely: none
Staff Present: Town Manager Paul Bockelman, Human Resources Director Evelyn Riffenburg-Rivera,
Clerk of the Council Athena O’Keeffe
Update on union collective bargaining: Police Patrol, Police Supervisors, DPW, DPW Supervisors,
and SEIU
Human Resources Director Evelyn Rivera-Riffenburg spoke about the negotiating process, and about
where negotiations stand with the five bargaining units. E. Riffenburg-Rivera spoke about plans to reach
$15/hour for all positions by 2023.
Town Manager Contract Negotiations
Town Manager P. Bockelman recused himself for this portion of the meeting.
President Griesemer spoke about changes to the Town Manager’s contract, and extending the contract to
2023. President Griesemer spoke about the difficulty in comparing salaries for town managers and
administrators; Amherst competes with larger towns in eastern Massachusetts and Connecticut.
Councilors discussed a 2% cost of living increase and a 3% performance-based adjustment to the Town
Manager’s salary.
VOTED 12-0 by roll call (Councilor Steinberg was absent) adjourn at 12:10 a.m.
Respectfully submitted,
Athena O’Keeffe, Clerk of the Council
Minutes approved and released by Town Council on September 21, 2020
