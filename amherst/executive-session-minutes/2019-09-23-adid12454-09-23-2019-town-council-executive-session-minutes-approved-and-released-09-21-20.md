Town of Amherst
Town Council Meeting
Monday, September 23, 2019
Town Room, Town Hall
4 Boltwood Avenue
Executive Session Minutes
President Griesemer called the executive session to order at 9:54 p.m. pursuant to the provisions of MGL
c. 30A, sec. 21(a)(7) to comply with, or act under the authority of, any general or special law or federal
grant-in-aid requirements: to approve Town Council executive session minutes of June 17, 2019, August
19, 2019, and August 26, 2019, and to release the executive session minutes of January 28, 2019, April
22, 2019, June 17, 2019, August 19, 2019 and August 26, 2019.
Members present: Bahl-Milne, Brewer, De Angelis, DuMont, Griesemer, Hanneke, Pam, Ross, Ryan,
Schoen, Schreiber, Steinberg, and Swartz.
Members absent: None
Staff present: Town Manager Paul Bockelman, Clerk of the Council Athena O’Keeffe
Executive Session Minutes
Councilors reviewed approved Town Council executive session minutes of January 28, 2019 and April
22, 2019.
VOTED unanimously 13-0 to release the Town Council executive minutes of January 28, 2019 and April
22, 2019, as presented.
Councilors reviewed draft minutes of June 17, 2019, August 19, 2019 and August 26, 2019.
Councilor Brewer commented that the minutes lack the MGL section included in previous drafts and
asked that those changes be made before the Council votes to approve the minutes.
VOTED unanimously 13-0 by roll call to adjourn the meeting of the Town Council.
Respectfully submitted,
Athena O’Keeffe, Clerk of the Council
Minutes approved & released by Town Council on September 21, 2020
