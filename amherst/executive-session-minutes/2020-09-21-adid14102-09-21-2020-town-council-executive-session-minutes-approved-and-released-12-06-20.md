Town of Amherst
Town Council Meeting
September 21, 2020
Virtual Meeting
Executive Session Minutes
Councilors Participating Remotely: Bahl-Milne, Brewer, De Angelis, Griesemer, Hanneke, Pam, Ross,
Ryan, Schoen, Schreiber, Steinberg, and Swartz
Councilors Absent: DuMont
Others Participating Remotely: Town Manager Paul Bockelman, Clerk of the Council Athena O’Keeffe
Griesemer called the executive session to order pursuant to MGL c.30A, s.21(a)7: to approve the Town
Council executive session minutes of June 17, 2019, August 19, 2019, August 26, 2019, September 23,
2019, and August 31, 2020; to retain for future Town Council review the approved executive session
minutes of August 19, 2019; and to release the minutes of June 17, 2019, August 26, 2019, September 23,
2019, and August 31, 2020;, and confirmed members can hear and be heard, and that no other person is
present or able to hear the discussion at the remote location.
MOTION: Grieesmer moved, second by De Angelis, to approve the Town Council executive session
minutes of June 17, 2019, August 19, 2019, August 26, 2019, September 23, 2019, and August 31, 2020
and to retain for future Town Council review the approved executive session minutes of August 19, 2019.
VOTED 12-0 by roll call, DuMont was absent, to approve the Town Council executive session minutes
of June 17, 2019, August 19, 2019, August 26, 2019, September 23, 2019, and August 31, 2020 and to
retain for future Town Council review the approved executive session minutes of August 19, 2019.
MOTION: Griesemer moved, second by Hanneke, to release the Town Council executive session
minutes of June 17, 2019, August 26, 2019, September 23, 2019, and August 31, 2020.
VOTED 12-0 by roll call, DuMont was absent, to release the Town Council executive session minutes of
June 17, 2019, August 26, 2019, September 23, 2019, and August 31, 2020.
MOTION: Hanneke moved, second by De Angelis, to adjourn the meeting.
VOTED 12-0 by roll, call DuMont was absent, to adjourn the meeting.
The Town Council adjourned in executive session at 9:35 pm.
Respectfully submitted,
Athena O’Keeffe, Clerk of the Council
Minutes approved and released by Town Council on December 6, 2021
