Amherst-Pelham Regional Middle School Library

Minutes
Call to order and declaration of a quorum
President Griesemer declared the presence of a quorum and called the meeting to order at 9:00 a.m.
Councilors Present: Councilors Bahl-Milne, Brewer, DuMont, Griesemer, Pam, Ross, Schoen, Steinberg.
Councilors absent: Councilors De Angelis, Hanneke, Ryan, Schreiber, Swartz
Councilors participating remotely: none
Others present: Town Manager Paul Bockelman, Comptroller Sonia Aldrich
Four Towns Meeting
Eric Nakajima, chair of the Amherst-Pelham Regional School District welcomed elected and appointed
members from the four towns that comprise the Amherst-Pelham Regional School District: Amherst,
Leverett, Pelham, and Shutesbury. He noted that each of the committees present had called their
respective meetings to order.
Mr. Nakajima thanked the four towns for their continued support for the highest quality education being
offered by the Regional School District.
Superintendent of Schools Michael Morris and School Finance Director Sean Mangano presented
information on the Regional School District that included a review of FY19 to date, a vision of the
School District, FY20 Budget Projections for both expenses and revenues, and FY20 Assessment
Method Options.
After the presentation, the four communities broke-out into small groups organized by community.
The Town of Amherst grouping included all members of the Town Council who were present plus the
Town Manager and Comptroller. At a prior Town Council meeting, Mr. Steinberg had been designated

the spokesperson for the Town of Amherst. After discussion in the small group, there was consensus
that the Town of Amherst was fully committed to the regional schools and was appreciative of the
quality education being offered in both the Middle and High Schools. The Town’s position should be to
hold the Town’s contribution to the Regional School District to 2.5% with the goals being that the
Regional School District not have significant cuts to its budget and that each of the four communities
was protected financially. It was agreed that a working group should gather outside of the Four Towns
Meeting to determine the best methodology to achieve these goals.
The four communities gathered back together and Mr. Steinberg summarized the discussion from the
Town of Amherst small group.
The three other towns shared their feedback.
No actions were taken.
Mr. Mangano then presented the District’s FY20 Capital Plan.
The meeting adjourned at 11:00 a.m.
Respectfully submitted,
Paul Bockelman, Town Manager
