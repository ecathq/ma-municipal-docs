1. Call to order and declaration of a quorum
President Griesemer declared the presence of a quorum and called the meeting to order at 4:00 p.m.
Councilors present: Councilors Bahl-Milne, Brewer, DeAngelis, DuMont, Griesemer, Hanneke, Pam,
Ross, Ryan, Schoen, Schreiber, Steinberg, Swartz.
Councilors absent: None
Councilors participating remotely: None
Town staff present: Town Manager Paul Bockelman, Town Clerk and Clerk of the Council Margaret
Nartowicz, Assistant Town Manager and Director of Conservation and Development David Ziomek,
Sustainability Coordinator Stephanie Ciccarello, Building Commissioner and Assistant Director of
Conservation and Development Rob Morra, Planning Director Christine Brestrup, Senior Planner
Nathaniel Malloy, Police Chief Scott Livingstone and Fire Chief Tim Nelson.
President Griesemer announced audio and video recording by Amherst Media.
2. Announcements - None
3. Presentations and Discussion

Ciccarello
b. Planning Department Overview – Planning Director Christine Brestrup and Senior Planner
Nathaniel Malloy
c. Inspection Services – Building Commissioner and Assistant Director of Conservation and
Development Rob Morra

5. Councilor Comments
Councilor Brewer commented that given the department presentations were not available ahead of the
meeting, and no hardcopies were printed for use at the meeting, it would be helpful to have the
department presentations uploaded to the Town Council meeting packet at the beginning of each
presentation so there was a place to make notes during the department presentation.

7. Adjourn
