1. Call to order and declaration of a quorum
President Griesemer declared the presence of a quorum and called the meeting to order at 9:00 a.m.
Councilors present: Councilors Bahl-Milne, Brewer, DeAngelis, DuMont, Griesemer, Hanneke, Pam,
Ross, Ryan, Schoen, Schreiber, Steinberg, Swartz.
Councilors absent: None
Councilors participating remotely: None
Town staff present: Town Manager Paul Bockelman, Town Clerk and Clerk of the Council Margaret
Nartowicz, Human Resources Director Deb Radway, Interim Finance Director and Comptroller Sonia
Aldrich, Principal Assessor David Burgess, Assistant Comptroller Holly Bowser, Acting Collector Jen
LaFountain, Acting Treasurer Cheryl Boucher, and Library Director Sharon Sharry.
President Griesemer announced audio and video recording by Amherst Media.
2. Announcements
President Griesemer and the Town Manager thanked the staff responsible for renovating the Town Room
in preparation for the Town Council.
3. Proclamations
4. Presentations and Discussion
a) Human Resources Department Overview – Director of Human Resources Deb Radway and
Personnel Board Chair Dr. Tony Butterfield.

b) Finance Department Overview – Interim Finance Director and Comptroller Sonia Aldrich,

5. Public Comment
Kent Faerber, a former Library Trustee, made positive remarks about the “browsing” rather than
“research” nature of the Amherst libraries, and applauded the work of the Library Director.
6. Councilor Comments
Councilor Steinberg pointed out that the budget booklets councilors received at an earlier meeting contain
additional information about departments, and encouraged councilors to review their booklets.
7. Topics Not Reasonably Anticipated by the President 48 Hours in Advance – None
8. Adjourn
MOVED: Councilor Schreiber moved, second by Councilor Hanneke to adjourn.
VOTED unanimously to adjourn at 6:00 p.m. on January 16, 2019.
