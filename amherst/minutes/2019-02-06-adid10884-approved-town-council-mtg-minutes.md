February 6, 2019 Town Council Meeting
Discussion: Councilor Steinberg gave a summary of the Finance Committee’s recommended motions,
explained that the funding of some previous projects will be paid off soon and that will free up some funds
that may be allocated to future projects; the Town had been building the Free Cash and Stabilization Fund;
a grant will not fund the entire permanent bridge project.
President Griesemer commented that the Town Council will focus on capital projects this month, Library,
DPW, and Fire will make presentations about their respective capital projects and a discussion about
funding for those projects will take place at a following meeting.
Councilor Hanneke commented that the process for this project has shed light on other infrastructure
issues, and that these issues need to be prioritized.
Councilor Swartz commented that the process for this project has not allowed for an appraisal of other
infrastructure projects that may be considered urgent, particularly the Meadow and Pine Street intersection.
VOTED 10-1 (Councilors Bahl-Milne, De Angelis, DuMont, Griesemer, Hanneke, Pam, Ross, Ryan,
Schoen, and Steinberg voted Yes; Councilor Swartz voted No) to appropriate $212,500, for the purchase
and installation of a temporary bridge for the Station Road, Hop Brook Bridge, and to meet such
appropriation, transfer $212,500 from Free Cash.
MOTION: Councilor Steinberg moved, second by Councilor Pam to appropriate $227,500, to reimburse
the road repair account for the engineering service costs incurred for the Station Road, Hop Brook Bridge
replacement, and to meet such appropriation, transfer $227,500 from Free Cash.
VOTED 10-1 (Councilors Bahl-Milne, De Angelis, DuMont, Griesemer, Hanneke, Pam, Ross, Ryan,
Schoen, and Steinberg voted Yes; Councilor Swartz voted No) to appropriate $227,500, to reimburse the
road repair account for the engineering service costs incurred for the Station Road, Hop Brook Bridge
replacement, and to meet such appropriation, transfer $227,500 from Free Cash.
