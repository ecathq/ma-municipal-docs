1. Call to order and declaration of a quorum
President Griesemer declared the presence of a quorum and called the meeting to order at 9:01 a.m.
Councilors present: Councilors Bahl-Milne, De Angelis, DuMont, Griesemer, Hanneke, Pam, Ross, Ryan,
Schoen, Schreiber, Steinberg, Swartz.
Councilors absent: Councilor Brewer
Councilors participating remotely: None
Others present: Town Manager Paul Bockelman, Clerk of the Council Margaret Nartowicz
President Griesemer announced audio and video recording by Amherst Media.
2. Announcements - None
3. Presentations and Discussion
a) Station Road Bridge Replacement Plan and Funding – Superintendent of Public Works
Guilford Mooring
Superintendent of Public Works Guilford Mooring gave a brief update on the current state of the Station
Road bridge project.
4. Committee Reports – Finance Committee
Councilor Steinberg gave an update from the Finance Committee on the funding for the Station Road
bridge project, and explained the two recommendations from the Finance Committee; one to appropriate
funds for a temporary bridge, and the other to replace funds from the road repair account that were used
for engineering service costs incurred for the Station Road bridge.

February 6, 2018 Town Council Public Forum
5. Public Comment
b) Public Forum [Charter Sec 5.6] – Station Road Bridge Funding
Public comment began at 9:17 a.m.
Sigurd Nilsen, 27 Teaberry Ln and President of the Amherst Woods Homeowners Association
summarized comments made at previous meetings, including safety concerns, increased traffic on
alternate routes, and urged the Town Council to approve funding for the project.
Barbara Rothenberg, 27 Teaberry Ln. spoke about the impact of the bridge closure on residents and
businesses, the stress on alternate routes used to avoid the closure, and urged the Town Council to
approve funding for the temporary bridge.
Martha Hanner, 18 Alyssum Dr. estimated two million extra miles have been driven by residents to avoid
the bridge closure, and commented that the temporary bridge would be a fair compromise until a grant is
awarded to help fund a permanent bridge.
Dr. Mary Ann Lowen, 104 Larkspur Dr. spoke about the need for doctors in her neighborhood responding
to emergencies to reach the hospital quickly.
Kenneth Cohen, an Amherst resident, echoed previous comments, and urged the Town Council to focus
on infrastructure to avoid a similar situation in the future.
Rich Morse of Precinct 7 asked about projects that may not be funded in the future if funds are moved
from free cash now.
Randy Needham of Acrow Bridge Corporation asked about the possibility of a temporary bridge that can
be used in the permanent bridge.
Councilor De Angelis asked how infrastructure projects are prioritized.
Superintendent Mooring compared the Station Road bridge project to the Mill River bridge project, and
explained that the Department of Public Works does not set project priorities.
6. Topics not reasonably anticipated by the President 48 hours in advance – None
7. Adjourn
MOVED: President Griesemer moved, second by Councilor De Angelis to adjourn.
VOTED unanimously to adjourn at 9:39 a.m. on February 6, 2019.
Respectfully submitted,
Margaret Z. Nartowicz
