Minutes
1. Call to order and declaration of a quorum
President Griesemer declared the presence of a quorum and called the meeting to order at 9:28 a.m.
[Note: the Four Towns Meeting started at 9:00 a.m. without the presence of a quorum of the Town
Council.]
Councilors present: Councilors Brewer, Griesemer, Pam, Ross, Schreiber, Steinberg, Swartz.
Councilors absent: Councilors Bahl-Milne, DeAngelis, DuMont, Hanneke, Ryan, Schoen.
Councilors participating remotely: None
Others present: Town Manager Paul Bockelman, Comptroller Sonia Aldrich, Assistant Town Manager
David Ziomek; representatives from the Amherst-Pelham Regional School Committee, Amherst School
Committee, and three other Towns (Leverett, Pelham, Shutesbury)
2. Presentations and Discussion
 Presentation on School Finance
Superintendent Morris welcomed the representatives from the four communities and presented an
overview and status of the regional school district assessment method. School Finance Director Sean
Mangano presented information on the proposed budget, capital and debt, and options for the
assessment method.
The four towns separated into individual Town caucuses. Members representing the Town of Amherst
discussed the presentation and agreed that the Town should be seeking an assessment option that was
fair, predictable, and provided adequate financing to the regional school district. The group agreed that
Option A met these criteria. The group decided that Option B was acceptable but did not provide the
predictability that Option A provided.
Minutes approved by Town Council on March 18, 2019

March 2, 2019 Town Council Meeting – Four Towns’ Meeting
After further discussion, all four communities agreed that Option A was the preferred option and
encouraged the Regional School Committee to give that option the most serious consideration.
Several in attendance encourage those assembled to seek assistance from the State government in
addressing the financial condition of regional school districts.
