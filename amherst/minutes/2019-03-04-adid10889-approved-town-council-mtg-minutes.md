March 4, 2019 Town Council Meeting
President Griesemer announced that some agenda items will be taken out of order.
Town Manager Paul Bockelman introduced the Residents Advisory Committee: Connie Kruger, Jim
Pistrang, and Keisha Dennis. Ms. Kruger and Mr. Pistrang made brief comments; Ms. Dennis was not in
attendance.
Agenda item 9. a) Tibet Day Proclamation
Thondup Tsering, president of the Regional Tibetan Association of Massachusetts, spoke to the
proclamation. The presenters noted there will be a flag-raising ceremony and symbolic walk from
Amherst to Northampton on March 10, 2019 to commemorate the Tibetan national uprising, and they
called on the Town Council and the public to support the peaceful resolution of the issue of Tibet.
Councilor Brewer noted a formatting error on the Proclamation and thanked the presenters.
MOTION: Councilor De Angelis moved, second by Councilor Pam, to adopt the Tibet Day 2019
Proclamation as amended.
VOTED 12-0-1 (Councilors Bahl-Milne, Brewer, DeAngelis, DuMont, Griesemer, Pam, Ross, Ryan,
Schoen, Schreiber, Steinberg, Swartz voted Yes; Councilor Hanneke Abstained) to adopt the Tibet Day
2019 Proclamation as amended.
Agenda item 9. b) Resolution in Support of Changing the Commonwealth Flag and Seal of
Massachusetts, as presented.
Elaine Kenseth, an Amherst resident, and David Detmold, a Montague resident, spoke to the resolution,
and asked for the Town Council’s adoption of the resolution, and support of HD.2968 and SD.1495, “a
Resolve Providing for the Creation of a Special Commission Relative to the Seal and Motto of the
Commonwealth.”
Councilor Hanneke noted a spelling error in the proposed resolution.
Councilor Brewer asked if councilors felt that this was an appropriate action for the Town Council.
Councilors De Angelis and DuMont spoke in favor of the resolution.
Councilor Ryan commented that he has not heard from enough of his constituents to vote in favor of the
resolution.
MOTION: Councilor Pam moved, second by Councilor Bahl-Milne, to adopt the Resolution in Support
of Changing the Commonwealth Flag and Seal of Massachusetts, as amended.
VOTED 10-0-3 (Councilors Bahl-Milne, DeAngelis, DuMont, Griesemer, Pam, Ross, Schoen, Schreiber,
Steinberg, and Swartz voted Yes, Councilors Brewer, Hanneke, and Ryan Abstained) to adopt the
Resolution in Support of Changing the Commonwealth Flag and Seal of Massachusetts, as amended.
Agenda item 9. c) Resolution in Support of First Congregational Church in Amherst, United
Church of Christ, Request to Maintain a Temporary Shelter
Reverend Vicki Kemper spoke to the resolution; Reverend Kemper explained that the State has classified
the church as a permanent shelter, which requires the church to make significant modifications to the
building to bring it to compliance as a permanent shelter. The Church does not intend to become a
permanent shelter, and is therefore asking the Town Council to support the resolution, which is a request
to remain a temporary shelter.
Town Manager Paul Bockelman spoke about the Town’s previous and ongoing support of the variance as
a temporary shelter, and explained that in the view of the Town, the Church complies with all health and
safety regulations as a temporary shelter.
State Representative Mindy Domb spoke in favor of the resolution.

March 4, 2019 Town Council Meeting
The Town Manager stated that the Town is looking into alternative ways of securing the Station Road
Bridge; the pavement plan will be prepared soon, typically, paved roads in need of repair are a higher
priority than unpaved roads.
Councilor Schoen asked about the prioritization of paving and road repairs.
The Town Manager stated that the pavement plan will be available to the public, and that the plan is
subject to change.
Councilor DuMont commented that the Emergency Preparedness workshop was well run, effective, and
well attended.
Councilor Swartz commented that Meadow Street was remarkably quiet through the weekend, and
applauded those who organized events for students.

process; the RAC will not review applications and will be subject to Open Meeting Law.
The Town Manager stated that RAC could be involved individually in the interview process, and/or
advise the Town Manager and ensure appointments comply with the Charter.
