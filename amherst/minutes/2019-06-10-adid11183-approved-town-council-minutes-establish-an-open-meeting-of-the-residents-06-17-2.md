June 10, 2019 Town Council Meeting – To Establish an Open Meeting of the Residents
Councilor Brewer asked if any of the presentations will address the zoning use that is allowed specifically
by approval from Town Meeting. President Griesemer asked that the Town Manager confer with the
Planning Department.
Councilor Ross clarified that the vote will be to set the meeting, not approve the agenda.
Councilor DuMont asked if the public will expect to hold a similar meeting any time an issue like this
comes up. President Griesemer responded that due to an abundance of comments to the Council from the
public, there was a need to use the Open Meeting of the Residents provision in the charter.
Councilor Ross asked about how abutters and neighbors would be defined, as they are listed specifically
rather than general public comment. President Griesemer responded that she has not been provided with a
list for the group but have been asked to give abutters and neighbors time to speak.
Councilor Schreiber asked about questions sent from neighbors regarding public safety; will anyone be
present who can address public safety issues. President Griesemer stated that Mr. Bockelman will have
information.
Councilor Ryan asked about the change in format. President Griesemer stated that she received an email
that some members of the public were not satisfied with the format of the previously scheduled meeting.
Councilor Brewer stated that there was intent to have project website with information, and asked how to
make correspondence available on the Town website.
Councilor Schreiber commented that there may be some who are not comfortable speaking in public.
Councilor Pam commented that abutters is limiting phrase. Town Council is experimenting with a
different form of democracy and this is an opportunity to let people be heard.
President Griesemer stated that residents who wish to make a presentation should contact her.
VOTED 12-0-1 (Councilors Bahl-Milne, Brewer, De Angelis, Griesemer, Hanneke, Pam, Ross, Ryan,
Schoen, Schreiber, Steinberg, and Swartz voted Yes; Councilor DuMont Abstained) in accordance with
Charter Section 8.1, to establish an Open Meeting of the Residents on June 24, 2019 at 6:30 p.m.
in the Town Room for the purpose of hearing presentations and discussing the Community
Preservation Act Committee’s recommendation regarding the proposed SRO project at 132
Northampton Road.
4. Topics Not Reasonably Anticipated by the Chair 48 Hours in Advance of the
Meeting - None
5. Adjourn
MOTION: Councilor Hanneke moved, second by Councilor Pam to adjourn.
VOTED unanimously to adjourn the meeting at 6:46 p.m. on June 10, 2019.
