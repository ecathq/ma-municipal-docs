June 10, 2019 Town Council CIP Public Forum
4. Public Comment
David Clooney of 984 East Pleasant Street, and full time firefighter and paramedic for the Town. Asked
about the ladder truck maintenance and the value of shifting priorities from maintenance to new
equipment. Assistant Fire Chief Lindsay Stromgren responded that it is known that the ladder truck and
ambulance have exceeded their lives, ideally ambulances would be on an 8-year replacement cycle; the
new vehicles did not make it on the JCPC request for this budget. Councilor Steinberg added that the
JCPC did give very serious consideration to the ladder truck and has been discussed for a number
of years; the question became weighing that against the Town’s other largest need, which is road repair.
Ultimately, the committee decided that the $16 plus million backlog on roads and sidewalks would be
recommended to the Town Manager. Councilor De Angelis commented that we are not doing the needed
maintenance to buildings and equipment; understanding that the roads are an issue but there is a need to
maintain the equipment that the Town owns. Mr. Bockelman responded that the Chief and Assistant Chief
were clear about the department’s needs. There is hesitation to invest too much in maintenance of
buildings that are planned to be replaced. Councilor Pam asked about fire trucks fitting inside the station.
Chief Nelson responded that a new truck will fit.
Sarah Marshall of 64 Eames Avenue asked if this plan is the same as the “One Town One
Plan.” Mr. Mangano responded that as the One Town One Plan is developed, it will feed into this Capital
Improvement Program.
Councilor Schoen the more detailed tables show a deficit for new debt cost for buildings. Mr. Mangano
explained the costs reflected in the tables.
