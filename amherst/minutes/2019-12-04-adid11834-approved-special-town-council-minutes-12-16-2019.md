December 4, 2019 Special Town Council Meeting
3. Hearings - None
4. Proclamations and Commemorations
a. Human Rights Day Proclamation
Councilor Ryan stated that Governance, Organization, and Legislation Committee voted the proclamation
clear, consistent, and actionable, and notes changes to the proclamation from the previous year.
MOTION: Councilor Ross moved, second by Councilor De Angelis, to adopt the Human Rights Day
Proclamation, as amended.
VOTED 9-0-0 (Councilors Pam, Schoen, and Schreiber were absent) to adopt the Human Rights Day
Proclamation, as amended.
b. Resolution in Support of First Congregational, Amherst, United Church of Christ’s
Request to Maintain a Temporary Shelter
Councilor Hanneke explained the differences between the current resolution and the resolution provided in
the packet to Town Council.
Reverend Vicki Kemper thanked the Council for their ongoing support and reiterated that the Church
hopes to resolve the temporary shelter and not become a permanent residence.
MOTION: Councilor Hanneke moved, second by Councilor De Angelis, to adopt the Resolution in
Support of First Congregational, Amherst, United Church of Christ’s Request to Maintain a Temporary
Shelter, as amended.
VOTED 9-0-0 (Councilors Pam, Schoen, and Schreiber were absent) to adopt the Resolution in Support of
First Congregational, Amherst, United Church of Christ’s Request to Maintain a Temporary Shelter, as
amended.
5. Annual Charter Requirements
a. Receipt of Annual Reports [Charter Sec. 4.2(d), 4.3(d), 4.4(d), 4.5, 6.3]
b. Receipt of Town Manager Memo Regarding Topics for Study [Charter Sec. 10.12]
Councilor Brewer stated that the Council should have a discussion about what the reports contain at a
future meeting.
6. Receipt of Town Manager Memo Regarding Topics for Study
MOTION: Councilor Hanneke moved, second by Councilor De Angelis, to accept the receipt of the Town
Manager memo regarding topics for study, per Charter Sec. 10.12, as completing the topics for study.
Councilor Brewer sought to clarify if the Council intends to study the topics further, or if the Town
Manager’s memo concludes the Council’s research into the topics. Councilor Hanneke responded that the
Charter requires the investigation of those specific items; the motion is intended to state that the Council
has met the Charter requirement.
Councilor Steinberg requested inclusion of the Charter reference in the motion.
VOTED: 9-0-0 (Councilors Pam, Schoen, and Schreiber were absent) to accept the receipt of the Town
Manager memo regarding topics for study, per Charter Sec. 10.12, as completing the topics for study.

December 4, 2019 Special Town Council Meeting
11. Topics Not Reasonably Anticipated by the President 48 Hours in Advance - None
12. Executive Session - None
13. Adjourn
MOTION: Councilor De Angelis moved, second by Councilor Swartz to adjourn the December 4, 2019
meeting of the Town Council.
MOTION: Councilor Hanneke moved, second by Councilor Ryan, to reconsider the Human Rights Day
Proclamation.
VOTED 9-0-0 (Councilors Pam, Schoen, and Schreiber were absent) to reconsider the Human Rights Day
Proclamation.
Councilor Brewer noted that the statement in the Proclamation “since 2018” is erroneous.
MOTION: Councilor Brewer moved, second by Councilor Hanneke, to adopt the Human Rights Day
Proclamation, as amended by removing the words “since 2018.”
VOTED 9-0-0 (Councilors Pam, Schoen, and Schreiber were absent) to adopt the Human Rights Day
Proclamation, as amended by removing the words “since 2018.”
VOTED 9-0-0 to adjourn at 12:56 pm on December 4, 2019.
Respectfully submitted,
Athena O’Keeffe
