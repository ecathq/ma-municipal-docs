Call to Order
President Griesemer declared the presence of a quorum and called the meeting to order at 6:10 p.m.
Councilors present: Councilors Brewer, De Angelis, DuMont, Griesemer, Hanneke, Pam, Ross, Ryan,
Schreiber, and Steinberg.
Councilors Absent: Councilors Bahl-Milne, Schoen, and Swartz.
Councilors Participating Remotely: none
Others present: Town Manager Paul Bockelman, School Committee Chair Anastasia Ordonez, Library
Trustee President Austin Sarat, Library Director Sharon Sharry, Town Clerk Shavena Martin,
Superintendent of Schools Michael Morris, Fire Chief Tim Nelson, Fire Chaplain Bruce Arbour, Police
Chief Scott Livingstone, State Senator for the Hampshire, Franklin, and Worcester District Jo Comerford,
State Representative for the 3rd Hampshire District Mindy Domb, and Clerk of the Council Athena
O’Keeffe

Recognition of Amherst High School football team.
Performance by Amherst Regional High School Students
Ceremonial Swearing-In of New and Promoted Firefighters and Police Officers – Fire Chaplain
Bruce Arbour, Police Chief Scott Livingstone, Fire Chief Tim Nelson, Town Clerk Shavena Martin
State of the Town Address – President Griesemer and Town Manager Paul Bockelman [Charter Sec.
2.2(c)] The President’s remarks begin at 17:45, and the Town Manager’s remarks begin at 37:35 of the
video. The full text of the President’s remarks is available here, the full text of the Town Manager’s
remarks are available here.
President Griesemer spoke about the Charter requirements fulfilled by the Council in its first year,
including required hearings, public forums, district meetings, formation of standing committees, adoption
of rules, completed review of zoning bylaws and the near-completion of review of general bylaws.
