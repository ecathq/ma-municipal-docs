1. Call to Order
President Griesemer declared the presence of a quorum and called the meeting to order at 6:31 p.m.
Councilors present: Councilors Bahl-Milne, Brewer, De Angelis, DuMont, Griesemer, Hanneke, Pam,
Ross, Ryan, Schoen, Schreiber, Steinberg, Swartz

Finance Committee members absent: non-voting resident members Robert Hegner, and Sharon Povinelli.
Councilors absent: None
Councilors participating remotely: None
Others present: Town Manager Paul Bockelman, Assistant Town Manager David Ziomek, and Clerk of
the Council Athena O’Keeffe.
President Griesemer announced audio and video recording by Amherst Media.
Councilor Steinberg called the Finance Committee meeting to order at 6:31 p.m.
President Griesemer noted the purpose of the Public Forum, which is to consider a request for an
appropriation outside the annual budget, and required per Charter Sec. 5.6. Notice of the Public Forum
has been posted for more than 10 days on the Town bulletin board in compliance with Charter Sec. 5.6(b).
2. Presentations and Discussion
a. Kendrick Park
Assistant Town Manager D. Ziomek provided brief presentation, explaining that the Town received a
$400,000 PARC grant for improvements at Kendrick Park; the Town is required to provide an additional
$259,000 for the same purposed and to provide funding for the entire project until the grant is received.
The Community Preservation Act Committee recommended that the Town’s share of the project cost be
funded from CPA funds, to be borrowed. The proposed appropriation of funds from the Community
Preservation Act requires this public forum. The project is in early design phases, Town staff and LSSE

3. Public Comment
a. Public Forum [Charter Sec. 5.6] – Kendrick Park
Amherst resident Laura MacLeod asked if an environmental impact study has been conducted. D. Ziomek
responded that the official design process has not begun, the next public meeting is scheduled for January
9, and additional input will be gathered at that time. The Tan Brook runs under the park, but it will be
avoided. The Town will also work with the Tree Warden to determine which trees must be trimmed or
moved.
No further public comment.
Councilor Bahl-Milne arrived at 6:33 p.m.
Councilor Steinberg stated that the Finance Committee would typically hear the feedback during the
public forum before making a recommendation to the Council. There was not time before the end of the
month to hold a regular Finance Committee meeting, and the deadline for the grant is approaching. The
Finance Committee previously unanimously voted in favor of recommending the appropriation.
There were no further Councilor or Finance Committee member comments.
4. Adjourn
MOTION: Councilor Pam moved, second by President Griesemer to adjourn the December 16, 2019
meeting of the Finance Committee.
VOTED unanimously to adjourn the December 16, 2019 meeting of the Finance Committee at 6:44 p.m.
