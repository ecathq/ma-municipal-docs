5. Presentations and Discussion
a. Massachusetts School Building Authority Process Overview
Town Manager Paul Bockelman and Superintendent of School Michael Morris provided an update
regarding the MSBA application process, including the creation and selection of members of the building
committee, the building process, application timeline, and next steps. M. Morris asked the Town Council
to consider clarifying the implications of the Council’s vote to adopt Climate Action Goals in terms of the
building project. M. Morris noted the upcoming deadline to fund a feasibility study by January 26, 2021.
Public comment:
Kate Troast, an Amherst resident of Dana Street, spoke in support of investment in the schools for the
short, middle, and long-term, and about the support of the public schools in Amherst.
Councilor Pam spoke about the importance of different modes of communication with residents, as not all
residents use the Town’s website to find information.
b. Studio Apartment Supportive Housing by Valley Community Development
Corporation at 132 Northampton Road: Update
Valley Community Development Corporation (Valley CDC) Real Estate Project Manager Laura Baker
and Executive Director Joanne Campbell gave an update regarding the Studio Apartment Supportive
Housing project on Northampton Road. The project is running behind the original timeline, in part due to
the hiring of a new design firm. Short term tenants will be moving in in the spring, to help defray the
costs of carrying the property. There have been four requests to delay submission of the application for
timing reasons, but the submission should be ready at the end of this month. There will be a 30-day public
comment period after the application is submitted. The comments received will be submitted along with
the application.
Councilor Brewer asked that the Town Council’s role be clarified in terms of the public comment process.
Public comment:
Kate Troast of Dana Street spoke about the perceived lack of site analysis regarding the project, that it is
the Council’s responsibility to ask for updates from the developer, asked that the Council follow up
regarding residents’ concerns, and brought up concerns about parking.
Hillary Wilbur-Ferro spoke in opposition to the size of the project, and asked about the Zoning Board
process, and public input in that process.
An Amherst resident of Dana Street spoke about their concerns about the project and asked if neighbors
may participate in the State’s site visit of the property.
Councilor Ross thanked the staff from Valley CDC for speaking to the Council.
Councilor Brewer asked that if the site visit is to be opened to residents it should be decided by the
Council and not by staff.
Councilor Pam asked about future support services for female residents.

the materials sent to the Council regarding the Community Choice Aggregation (CCA).

the previous motion.

agreement.

January 6, 2020 Town Council Meeting
Councilor Pam commented that the Vice President is like the President-in-training and spoke in favor of
sharing the President’s responsibilities more equitably with other Councilors.
Councilor DuMont agreed with Councilor Brewer’s comments about sharing responsibilities with other
Councilors, and asked what the Vice President’s current duties are.
Councilor Hanneke spoke about her role as Vice President and listed the following duties: review
minutes, track future agenda items, attend agenda setting meetings, craft and help craft motion language,
stand in for the President at a variety of events at the request of the President, preside over meetings that
the President is unable to attend. Councilor Hanneke also noted that she has been the go-to person on
Charter guidance, has given feedback to the President when requested including feedback regarding
committee assignments, and reviewed drafts of documents and motions at the request of the President.
Councilor Swartz commented about the difference between leadership and power, noted that the President
and Vice President have listened to feedback, and spoke in favor of sharing agenda setting experience
with other Councilors.
Councilor De Angelis expressed concern about the Vice President sitting in meetings with UMass and not
sharing that information with the rest of the Council, expressed concern about transparency and learning,
the willingness of other Councilors to take on some of those duties and expressed disappointment with the
lack of trust exhibited in conversations leading up to this meeting.
President Griesemer spoke about the meetings at UMass that the Vice President attended on her behalf,
which were regarding the student and family housing developments at UMass.
Councilor De Angelis suggested more regular executive sessions to update the Council on these meetings.
Councilor Steinberg some of the problem with executive session is that the law is very narrow in what it
will allow as a topic for discussion during executive session. The Select Board did act differently, but it
was a five-person board and it was a different group.
Councilor Schreiber spoke in favor of spreading the duties of the Vice President.
President Griesemer spoke about inviting a Councilor to agenda setting, and sharing duties with other
Councilors.
Councilor Swartz commented about the President and Vice President working together and explained her
change in perspective about the Vice President being the President’s helpmate.
Councilor Brewer noted the option of confidential emails from the Town Manager, which would be a way
of sharing information that could not be discussed in executive session.
7.c. Action items: Town Manager Goals - Second Reading
President Griesemer spoke about the changes to the goals based on Councilor feedback.
Councilor Ross thanked the committee for their work, and expressed disappointment with the final
document, lack of a committee report, the absence of an economic development section and the failure of
the committee to include the feedback received from Councilors into the final document.
Councilor De Angelis spoke about the assessment and development of a plan for staffing Fire and EMS
services, which was removed in the later draft.
Councilor Brewer spoke about the need to use the documents as an evaluation instrument, and suggested
that Councilors send feedback rather than continue committee meetings.
