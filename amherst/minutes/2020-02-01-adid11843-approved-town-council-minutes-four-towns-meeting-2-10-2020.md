Town Council Meeting
(Part of the Four Towns Meeting)

Amherst-Pelham Regional Middle School Library
170 Chestnut Street
Minutes
1. Call to order and declaration of a quorum
President Griesemer declared the presence of a quorum and called the meeting to order at 9:04 a.m.
Councilors present: Councilors Brewer, DuMont, Griesemer, Hanneke, Pam, Ryan, Schoen, Steinberg,
Swartz
Councilors absent: Councilors Bahl-Milne, DeAngelis, Ross, Schreiber
Councilors participating remotely: None
Others present: Town Manager Paul Bockelman, Interim Finance Director Sonia Aldrich; representatives
from the Amherst-Pelham Regional School Committee, Amherst School Committee, and the Towns of
Leverett, Pelham, and Shutesbury
2. Presentations and Discussion
 Presentation on School Finance
Eric Nakajima, Chair of the Regional School District, welcomed the representatives from the four
communities. Superintendent Morris and School Finance Director Slaughter presented an overview and
status of the Regional School District operating budgets. They reported variables about health insurance
and Charter School assessments were in hand and included in the budget which resulted in an overall
budget increase of 1.54%. They listed the advantages of the budget noting that it maintained many of
the positive characteristics of the District.
The Superintendent noted that the budget will be presented to the Regional School District Committee
on February 11th and that he anticipated a vote of the Committee on March 10th or 24th.
Minutes approved by Town Council on February 10, 2020

February 1, 2020 - Town Council Meeting
The four towns separated into individual Town caucuses. Members representing the Town of Amherst
included the Town Council, Amherst School Committee and Town staff. The group discussed the
presentation and clarifying questions were answered.
At 9:58 a.m., the four towns reunited. Representatives from the towns reported on their positions with
brief explanations of why. The Town of Pelham said the “45%” model was acceptable to them. Mr.
Steinberg, speaking for the Town Council, stated that the Town of Amherst preferred the “40%” model
as it was the second of a two year proposal. A representative from Leverett said they preferred the
“40%” model. And the representative from Shutesbury said they preferred the “45%” model with the
intention of continuing movement to the statutory method.
Messrs. Morris and Slaughter presented the Region’s capital budget for FY21. There were several
questions.
Mr. Demling stated that the different expectations of the communities may lead to a larger discussion
about the future of the Regional School District.
Mr. Morris thanked all for participating.
3. Topics Not Reasonably Anticipated by the President 48 Hours in Advance - None

Steinberg, and Swartz voted Yes; Councilors Bahl-Milne, De Angelis, Ross, and Schreiber were absent)
to adjourn the February 1, 2020 meeting of the Town Council.
