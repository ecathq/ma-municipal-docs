1. Call to Order
President Griesemer declared the presence of a quorum and called the meeting to order at 6:02 p.m.
Councilors present: Councilors Bahl-Milne, Brewer, De Angelis, DuMont, Griesemer, Hanneke, Pam,
Ross, Ryan, Schoen, Schreiber, Steinberg, and Swartz.
Councilors absent: Councilors DuMont and Swartz
Councilors participating remotely: None
Others present: Town Manager Paul Bockelman, Sonia Aldrich, Michael Morris, Clerk of the Council
Athena O’Keeffe
2. Presentations and Discussion
a. Council Order FY20-68: An Order Appropriating and Approving Borrowing for a Portion of the
Town of Amherst FY2020 Capital Program - borrowing authorization for new school design
Interim Finance Director Sonia Aldrich and Superintendent of Schools Michael Morris briefly explained
that funding the feasibility study is the first step in the enrollment period.
3. Public Comment
a. Public Forum [Charter Sec. 5.6] – Regarding an appropriation outside the annual
budget: Council Order FY20-68: An Order Appropriating and Approving Borrowing
for a Portion of the Town of Amherst FY2020 Capital Program – borrowing
authorization for new school design
President Griesemer opened the floor for public comments.
No public comments were made.
4. Adjourn
MOTION: Councilor De Angelis moved, second by Councilor Ryan, to adjourn the Public Forum.
VOTED unanimously to adjourn.
