b. Open Containers Bylaw Amendment – First Discussion Rule 8.4, first reading under
charter sec.
Councilor Ross spoke about the revised memo regarding the bylaw amendment, which includes
comparisons to other communities’ open containers bylaws.
Councilor Pam spoke in support of the bylaw amendment, and asked that if there should be a threat of
observance and enforcement. Councilor Ross stated that the Board of License Commissioners will
promulgate regulations. Councilor Brewer expressed concerns about the regulations, contested the
statement that the bylaw will benefit local breweries and stated bylaw should be limited to downtown
parks. Councilor Schoen commented that the bylaw is unclear that the permit is for an event rather than a
person or activity. Councilors discussed the scope of the bylaw and the desire for oversight of regulations
by the Board of License Commissioners. Councilor Steinberg spoke about his experience during his
service on the Select Board, an incident regarding the unregulated consumption of alcohol in an Amherst
public park, and spoke in favor of adopting the bylaw and trusting that the board of License
Commissioners will promulgate appropriate regulations. Councilor Brewer spoke in opposition to the
bylaw, and stated that she would vote in support if the bylaw were limited to downtown areas.

f. Reorganization of Standing Committees
Councilor Ross spoke about the Governance, Organization, and Legislation (GOL) proposal, the
establishment of Town Services and Outreach (TSO) Committee, the redistribution of duties among
Community Resources Committee (CRC), GOL, and the ultimate dissolution of Outreach,
Communication, and Appointments (OCA) Committee.
Councilors discussed the proposed charges.
Councilors discussed adding, “if referred by the Council” to the GOL charge in regard to recommending
appointments of liaisons, Joint Capital Planning Committee (JCPC), Budget Coordinating Group (BCG),
and non-voting members of Finance Committee.
Councilors discussed the proposed GOL charge sections regarding recommending appointments for the
Town Manager, interim Town Manager, Clerk of the Council, and other staff.
Councilors discussed the last bullet point in the proposed GOL charge, which allows the committee to do
the work that has already been referred by the Council.
Councilor Swartz spoke in opposition to the creation of the TSO committee. Councilor Hanneke
responded that the Council could propose legislation that would affect Town departments and services,
and there should be a place for discussion. Councilor DuMont commented that the redistribution of OCA
tasks on the other committees is too much of a burden, and spoke in opposition to dissolving OCA.
Councilor Steinberg spoke in favor of creating the TSO committee, commented that it does make sense to
divide CRC’s responsibilities, noted that Finance Committee deals with Town services as well, and stated
that he would prefer that the Council not vote on the charges today.
Councilor Ross spoke about the rationale of assigning public ways to TSO, and not explicitly stating
“parking.”
Councilors discussed the limitations of GOL review of bylaws, the TSO charge would allow the
committee to review a bylaw at greater depth.
Councilors discussed edits to the GOL charge, to make recommendations to the Council regarding non-
voting Finance Committee members a separate bullet, adding “if referred by the Council,” and to add
“process for” regarding recommendations for appointments for Clerk of the Council, additional Council
staff, Town Manager, and interim Town Manager. Councilor Ross spoke in favor of leaving the charges
broad and the referrals to committees specific. Councilors discussed the change in regard to Council
Rules of Procedure rule 10.6 regarding liaisons.
MOTION: Councilor Hanneke moved, second by Councilor Ross, to rescind the current Community
Resources Committee and Governance, Organization, and Legislation Committee charges, effective
immediately, adopt the proposed Town Services and Outreach committee charge, as amended at the 2-24-
2020 Town Council meeting, and Community Resources Committee charge as amended at the 2-12-2020
Governance, Organization and Legislation Committee meeting, effective immediately except for the
appointment responsibility for Planning Board and Zoning Board of Appeals in the Community
Resources Committee charge, adopt the Governance, Organization and Legislation Committee charge as
amended at the 2-24-2020 Town Council meeting effective immediately, and rescind all duties in the
Outreach, Communications, and Appointments Committee Charge except for making recommendations
regarding appointments to Planning Board and Zoning Board of Appeals. Appointment responsibility for
Planning Board and Zoning Board of Appeals shall remain in Outreach, Communications and
Appointments until July 1, 2020, at which time the appointments responsibility for those bodies shall shift
