2. Hearing -- Public Hearing of the Finance Committee on the One-Month budget for July
2020 and other relevant information - Charter Sec. 5.5(a)
Councilor Steinberg called the meeting to order at 7:36 p.m.
Town Manager Paul Bockelman stated that there will be another public hearing on the full FY21 budget.
Finance Director Sean Mangano spoke about the following:
 Outlined the one-month budget
 Broken into functional areas
 Gives authorization to spend funds in July
 Consulted with department heads to see what they needed to get through the month
 Pension system paid in one lump in order to receive a discount.
 12 month budget will completely replace this one-month budget
 Capital not included in enterprise funds for July

---

Councilor Schoen asked about savings of this years operating budget, COLA for non-union staff and if
decisions made in the one-month budget effect the FY21 budget.
S. Mangano responded that he is confident we will not need additional funds for June.
Comptroller Sonia Aldrich spoke about potential savings.
Town Manager Paul Bockelman responded that non-union employees are slated to receive a 2% COLA,
and spoke about potential furloughs.
Councilor Steinberg stated that there are no significant staffing or program changes in the one month
budget.
There were no public comments or questions.
Councilor Pam expressed her preference for a Zoom meeting rather than webinar format.
Councilor Brewer spoke in support of schools and Town employee COLA increases and furloughs rather
than cuts.
M. Theilman spoke in favor of consistency between Town, school, and library staff, and asked about
summer programs for children.
P. Bockelman responded that summer camp guidelines are so onerous we cannot do municipal camps this
year. An official announcement will be made tomorrow.
Councilor Steinberg called hearing to a close at 8:04 p.m. and announced the Finance Committee will
meet tomorrow to consider the one-month budget.
