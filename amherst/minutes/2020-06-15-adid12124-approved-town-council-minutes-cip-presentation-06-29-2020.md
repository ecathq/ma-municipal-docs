 Capital process in a typical year
 Impact of COVID-19 on funds and process

Councilor Hanneke asked about the differences in allocation between roads and sidewalks, different from
what was presented to JCPC weeks ago.
S. Mangano responded that roads and sidewalks were lumped together in the past.
P. Bockelman added that the Department of Public Works has made progress on roadwork but now the
Town needs to prioritize work on sidewalks.
Councilor Schoen stated that there was a recommendation that JCPC continue to meet in the fall to look at
the reserve fund, and asked to confirm that will be the focus for JCPC in the fall.
S. Mangano responded that there may be other funding sources at that time in addition to looking at the 5-
year forecast.
Councilor Schoen spoke about the need for a vehicle inventory and about topics to be addressed at the
JCPC meetings in the fall.
Councilor Pam asked if there is money for repair of existing sidewalks.
S. Mangano responded that yes, work sidewalks includes replacement and repair.
Councilor DuMont stated the Energy and Climate Action Committee submitted a budget request before
COVID-19; and spoke about the emission reduction goal.
S. Mangano responded that the request has not been approved.
