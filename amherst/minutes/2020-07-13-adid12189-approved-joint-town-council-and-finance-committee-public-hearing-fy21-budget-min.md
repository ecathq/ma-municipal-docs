After confirming all participants could hear and be heard, President Griesemer declared the presence of a
quorum, called the meeting to order at 7:03 p.m. and announced audio and video recording by Amherst
Media.
Councilor Steinberg declared the presence of a quorum of the Finance Committee, called the meeting of
the Finance Committee to order, and confirmed Hegner could hear and be heard.
Note: Pursuant to Governor Baker’s March 12, 2020 Order Suspending Certain Provisions of the Open
Meeting Law, G.L. c. 30A, §18, this meeting of the Town Council and Finance Committee was
conducted via remote participation.

Alexander Blount, Amherst resident, spoke about personal involvement in civil rights movements, the
need for change, and spoke in opposition to cuts to the Police department.
Jonathan Sivel, Amherst resident, spoke about structural racism.
Megan Lieff, Amherst resident, spoke about reinvesting APD budget cuts in Amherst schools.
Jessica Slattery, Amherst resident and UMass student, spoke in favor of implementing a permanent hiring
freeze for APD.
Ellisha Walker, Amherst resident, spoke in favor of cutting the APD budget
Lauren Mills, Amherst resident, spoke in favor of defunding the APD.
Jeff Mazur, Amherst resident, spoke in favor of defunding the APD.
Amara Donovan, Amherst resident, spoke about racism in Amherst.
Many of the public comments underscored the fact that a high proportion of police calls are for mental
health, medical care and other services that could be better handled by people trained for those services
than by police.
Councilor DuMont asked that written comments be sent to the clerk.
Councilor Pam thanked everyone who spoke.
Councilor Hanneke thanked those who spoke, apologized for not turning the camera on, and stated that
there is a lot for the Council to think about in the next week or two.
Councilor Bahl-Milne thanked everyone who spoke and stated that there is a lot to think about and work
to do.
Councilor Schoen commented that there is impressive level of energy and youthful voices from all areas
of Town, stated that she hoped that those who participated continue their involvement in local issues, and
commented that it was terrific to see 80 people attending the meeting.
Councilor DuMont agreed that the amount of public participation was great, and commented about not
seeing commenters’ faces.
