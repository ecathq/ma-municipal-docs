3. Hearings
a. 6:30 pm – Public Hearing: Eversource request to install two jointly owned poles on
Belchertown Road
President Griesemer stated that the hearing is held per Massachusetts General Law Chapter 166, Section
22 the requires that the Council hold a public hearing on the petition of any utility provider to construct or
locate poles, conduit or underground wires for the transmission of electricity.
This hearing is on the May 12, 2020 petition of Eversource to install two (2) jointly owned poles on
Belchertown Road. The first to be set is 9/70 M, placed in line with and approximately 128 feet
southeasterly of existing pole 9/70. The pole center line is approximately 27 feet from the road center
line. The second is 9/71m to be placed in line with and approximately 100 feet southeasterly of existing
pole 9/71. The pole center line is approximately 33 feet from Belchertown Road center line, 67 feet from
Harkness Road Center line and approximately 23 feet to the left of a fire hydrant.
As required by statute, written notice of the time and place of the hearing was mailed on July 27, 2020 to
all owners of real estate abutting the proposed pole locations.
The Department of Public Works has recommended approval of this petition and reminds the petitioner
that a street opening permit must be obtained prior to commencing work.
President Griesemer opened the public hearing.
Eversource Representative Carla Trosino-Laramy was available for questions.
Councilor Schoen asked if the poles would get in the way of a sidewalk.
Trosino-Laramy responded that poles may be moved if needed in the future.
DPW Superintended Guilford Mooring added that there is plenty of room in that section of Belchertown
Road.
Councilor Steinberg stated that in the past, objections were made after a pole hearing regarding shade
trees, and the Town Manager responded to a question that the Tree Warden saw no issue with the poles.
Councilor De Angelis asked about 5G technology in Amherst regarding this request.
G. Mooring responded that there is no 5G in this specific area, there is 5G in other areas, and there is no
known plan to add 5G at this point.
There were no public comments or questions.
Seeing no other questions or comments, President Griesemer closed the public hearing.
b. 6:30 pm – Public Hearing: Eversource request to install two jointly owned poles on
Blue Hills Road and Orchard Street
President Griesemer stated that per Massachusetts General Law Chapter 166, Section 22 the requires that
the Council hold a public hearing on the petition of any utility provider to construct or locate poles,
conduit or underground wires for the transmission of electricity.
This hearing is on the May 27, 2020 petition of Eversource to install two (2) jointly owned poles. The
first to be set is#13/1S to be placed directly across from 13/1 on Blue Hills Road and will support the
reverse corner from moving poles on the main road due to MassDOT Road Project. The second is
#99/EII to be placed approximately six feet from fire hydrant on Orchard Street. This Pole is need due to
Mass DOT Road Project as well and will support a single-phase side tap that feeds Orchard Street. Also,
both poles will have down guys and anchors.
