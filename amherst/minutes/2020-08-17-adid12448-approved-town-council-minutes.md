7. Presentations and Discussion
a. Town Manager Performance Evaluation
Councilors discussed edits to the document “FY20 Town Manager Evaluation Memo - DRAFT
as of 8-27-2020” including:
 Specific reference to the economic impact of COVID-19
 Clarification of the Town Manager’s length of service
 Agreements, and lack thereof, with higher education institutions
 Number of questions that Councilors responded “unable to judge”
 Phrasing of “police issue” that may be unclear without more context
 Discussion of the downtown vision and plans
 The tone of the memo
 Inclusion of minority views
 Inclusion of suggestions or goals
 Reference to individual Councilor evaluations, or addition of evaluations as an appendix
Public Comment:
Curry Kautz, Amherst resident, asked for more clarity regarding the timing of public comment,
commented that community safety is important, and stated that no one is doing enough regarding
community safety.
b. Town Council Performance Goals for Town Manager FY21
President Griesemer spoke about the draft goals, which represent a change to fewer specific
goals and reflects broad goals with examples of achievement of the goal.
Councilor Steinberg noted the policy goals are listed alphabetically and their placement does not
indicate priority.
Councilors discussed the following:
 Establishment of a police review board
 Adding a goal related to community health, safety, and social justice
 Adding a goal related to racial equity
 Diversity and empathy training for Councilors and Town staff

 Adopting the goals and making additions or revisions in the future
 Creating an oversight commission on police practices
 Use of the goals for the Town Manager evaluation next year
 Town Manager goals as a tool for accomplishing the Council’s policy goals
 How to incorporate racial equity into the goals
Public comment:
Demetria Shabazz, Amherst resident, spoke in support of inclusion of minority opinions, and in
support of a specific racial equity goal, commented that Councilors’ bias is written into policy,
and spoke about specific, measurable goals.
Lydia Irons, Amherst resident, echoed the previous speakers’ comments, added that racial equity
should be paramount in the Town Manager’s goals, spoke about Councilors comments made
during committee meetings and urged the Council to include racial equity goals in the Town
Manager’s goals.
Sarah Pallas agreed that racial equity needs to be front and center, and commented that it is
unacceptable for a Council member to state that comments made by black residents are
fabricated.
Allegra Clark, Amherst resident, spoke in support of creating a racial equity goal and weaving
racial equity into all of the Town Manager’s goals.
Gabriel Davila, Amherst resident, stated that it is unacceptable that the Council sees racial justice
as another goal that the council has to deal with, spoke about how racism exists in Amherst
including experiences as an Amherst student that never experienced a teacher of color, and stated
that people of color should not have to prove that racism exists.
Jose Adastra, Northampton resident, spoke about fighting abolition, lost family members due to
drug wars, colonization of Puerto Rico, the feeling of being a second class citizen, lack of
representation, asked the Council be sensitive to the requests of the Black Lives Matter
movement and asked the Council to reach out to Black Lives Matter activists.
Isolda Ortega-Bustamante, Amherst resident, commented that the Town Manager goals were not
available with enough time for reasonable discussion, stated that the goals fail to give a
meaningful response to systemic racism in Amherst, spoke about voting places and the need for
listening sessions.
Councilor Schoen spoke about suggested changes to the economic vitality and capital projects
sections.
Councilor Pam asked for a way forward regarding racial equity goals.
Councilor Ross asked about process.
Councilor DuMont commented about the climate action goal, and that it was disappointing that
the climate action goal was less than the goals adopted by the Council last year.
