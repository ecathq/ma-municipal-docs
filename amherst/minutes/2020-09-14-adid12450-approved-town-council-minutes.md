7. Presentations and Discussion
a. Community Safety
Town Manager Paul Bockelman spoke about the incorporation of racial and social justice values in public
safety responses, the plan to create a 9-member community safety working group with police chief or
designee and members of the BIPOC community to make a recommendation to the Town Manager who
would then make a recommendation to the Town Council. The timeframe is tight in order to consider it in
time for the next budget cycle. Under the Charter, the Town Manager is the appointing authority for
multiple-member bodies, and the Town Council approves those appointments. P. Bockelman spoke about
the planned committee charge, the appointing process, and the goal of filing appointments by October 19.
Councilor Hanneke commented that the Council heard a lot of concern about the ex-officio position of the
Police Chief, and suggested other ways of incorporating feedback from the police departments without
the Chief sitting on the committee.
Councilor Schoen asked about committing some of the Health Director’s time to hear thoughts about
alternatives such as the CAHOOTS program in Eugene, OR.
Councilor Brewer stated that the Council will not vote on the charge, and clarified that the Town Manager
is sharing information with the Council at this point. Councilor Brewer spoke about the ex officio position
for the Police Chief or designee on the committee, about the appointment process, and how an advisory
group would not be subject to the Open Meeting Law.
