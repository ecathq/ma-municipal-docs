2. Announcements
November 19, 2020, 6:30 pm: Public Forum on the Budget – Charter Sec. 5.3
3. Presentations and Discussion
a. Updated Financial Indicators – Town Manager Paul Bockelman, Finance Director
Sean Mangano, Comptroller Sonia Aldrich, Assistant Comptroller Holly Bowser,
and Collector Jennifer Lafountain
President Griesemer welcomed members of the Budget Coordinating Group.
Town Manager Paul Bockelman, Finance Director Sean Mangano, Collector Jennifer Lafountain,
Assistant Comptroller Holly Bowser, Comptroller Sonia Aldrich presented the financial projections for
fiscal year 2022 budget planning.
Demling asked about the difference between level funding and level services.
Mangano responded that level funding is a budget with the same dollar amount as last year, not
necessarily the same services.
Councilor Pam asked about loss of staff or furloughs.
Bockelman responded that the Town would like to retain staff.
Councilor Schoen asked about CARES Act funds that may be lost at the end of the calendar year.
Mangano responded that budgeted costs are not eligible for CARES funding.
Councilor Brewer asked that the Finance Committee pay attention to collective bargaining when working
on the Finance Guidelines.
Sarat asked about debt service.
Councilor DuMont asked about the availability of materials.
Councilor Hanneke asked about school enrollment and how enrollment will be projected into next year.
