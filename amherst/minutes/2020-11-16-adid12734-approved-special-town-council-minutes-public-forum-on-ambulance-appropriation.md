2. Presentations and Discussion
a. Council Order FY21-05B: An Order Appropriating Funds for the Town of Amherst
Capital Program – Purchase of a new Ambulance
Finance Director Sean Mangano spoke briefly about the need of a new ambulance. The Town’s oldest
ambulance failed inspection and the cost to replace it would be over $30,000. The cost of a new hybrid
ambulance is $300,000. The funds would come from the ambulance receipts fund.
