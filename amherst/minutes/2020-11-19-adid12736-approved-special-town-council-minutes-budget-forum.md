4. Public Comment
a. Public Forum on the Town Budget – Charter Sec. 5.3
Amherst residents Lydia Irons, Zoë Crabtree, Allegra Clark, Isolda Ortega-Bustamante, Maida Ives, an
anonymous resident, Dr. Demetria Shabazz, and José Lugo spoke about the need for equity in the budget
and about policing in Amherst, stating that $136 per resident is spent on policing and only $3.77 per
resident is spent on public health. Residents urged the Council to consider the proposal from the Racial
Equity Task force that requests funds for a BIPOC Residents Council, youth programming, a cultural
center, and hiring of a Chief Diversity and Equity Officer. Residents commented on financial systems that
contribute to systemic racism, stated that the Town’s financial decisions have consequences, and asked
the Council to prioritize the needs of BIPOC residents. Isolda Ortega-Bustamante commented that a
volunteer has been helping residents fill out paperwork to avoid eviction, and that a bilingual social
worker position funded by the Town could take on this work. Zoe Crabtree, speaking on behalf of an
anonymous Amherst resident, urged the Council to cut the Police Department budget by 50%, stating that
Police target black, brown, and disabled people, and that Amherst does not feel safe to residents of color.
