After confirming all participants could hear and be heard, President Griesemer declared the presence of a

Media.
Note: Pursuant to Governor Baker’s March 12, 2020 Order Suspending Certain Provisions of the Open
Meeting Law, G.L. c. 30A, §18, this meeting of the Town Council was conducted via remote
participation.
2. Presentations and Discussion
a. Council Order FY21-#21-08A: An Order Authorizing the Acquisition of
Belchertown Road Property for Community Housing Purposes
Assistant Town Manager David Ziomek spoke briefly about the project.
Affordable Housing Chair John Hornik spoke about the urgent need for housing, affordable housing
projects in Amherst, and the current project request.
Community Preservation Act Committee (CPAC) Chair Sarah Marshall conveyed the committee’s
unanimous and enthusiastic support for the project, the committee was pleased to learn the purchase was
successfully negotiated at a lower price than anticipated. Marshall spoke about the goals of the CPAC,
and the hope that the Council will approve the borrowing request.
3. Public Comment – More than one-half of the meeting time on the agenda will be
devoted to public comment

---

a. Public Forum – Charter Sec. 5.6 – Regarding an appropriation outside the
annual budget: Council Order FY21-08A: An Order Authorizing the
Acquisition of Belchertown Road Property for Community Housing
Purposes
Elisa spoke in favor of the project.
Linda Slakey, Amherst resident and president of the Amherst Community Land Trust spoke in favor of
the project.
Janet Keller, Amherst resident and board member of Amherst Community Land Trust spoke in support of
the project.
Kitty Axelson-Berry, Amherst resident, asked about the square footage of the proposed project.
Rob Morra spoke about the concept plan, which includes outdoor space for playground and space.
Adrienne Terrizzi, Amherst resident, speaking on behalf of the League of Women Voters, spoke in
enthusiastic support of the project and urged the Council to support the project and approve the
appropriation.
There were no other members of the public asking questions of speaking in support or opposition to the
project.
President Griesemer thanked John Hornik, Sarah Marshall, David Ziomek and others. The Council will
vote on the project on January 25, after review by the Finance Committee and Community Resources
Committee.
