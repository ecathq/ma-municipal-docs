Agenda item 7.a. Presentations and Discussion: Community Safety Working Group Update
Town Manager Paul Bockelman spoke about the requested 2-month extension of the Working Group’s
deadline and stated the Town will hold the two police positions vacant until that time.
Community Safety Working Group Chari Paul Wiley and Vice Chair Brianna Owen, spoke about:
• Volume of work and volume of tasks are behind the request for an extension.
• CSWG purpose of the group and charge
• How the group is achieving the goals and purpose
• The groups work thus far
• Next steps and actions
• Spread the word about the community survey
• How the Town Council can support the group’s work
President Griesemer thanked Wiley and Owen for the presentation.

Councilor Schoen commented about classism that could be addressed in the group’s work, asked about
how to collect more responses, suggested an announcement through the schools, and asked about the type
of consultant the group is looking for.
Wiley responded the Town Manager has been very supportive in terms of resources in people and
material and time. The group will need help collecting and analyzing data, and putting reports together

the project.

the lcation is appropriate.

6. Resolutions and Proclamations
a. Resolution Condemning the January 6, 2021 Insurrection and Violence at the U.S.
Capitol
Councilor Ryan reported the Governance, Organization, and Legislation (GOL) Committee unanimously
voted the resolution clear, consistent, and actionable.
Councilors Hanneke and De Angelis are the resolution cosponsors.
Councilor Hanneke spoke about the need and duty to call out injustice even if not in our Town, about the
lack of response from the Police Chief, urged the Council to support the resolution, and expressed
conflicted feelings about the section that people spoke about during public comment.
Councilor De Angelis spoke in support of the resolution.
Bockelman stated the first statement was released quickly, thinking about children harmed by seeing the
violence. The Chief was not invited to add support to the statement but Bockelman believes he would
have been in support if he had been invited.
Councilor Bahl-Milne asked why the section people spoke about during public comment was removed
from the resolution.
Bockelman responded that no Amherst Police Officers travelled to or participated in the riots.
Councilor Steinberg stated he raised the issue at the GOL meeting; the Charter is clear about the
Manager’s position in managing collective bargaining agreements and employees; the Council is not
empowered to request information. Felt uncomfortable that it would be an actionable item without an
opinion from the Town Attorney that it is an appropriate step.
Councilor Brewer referred to Charter Sec 2.8: investigations and access to information.
MOTION: President Griesemer moved, second by Councilor Hanneke, to adopt the Resolution
Condemning the January 6, 2021 Insurrection and Violence at the U.S. Capitol, as presented.
VOTED unanimously, 13-0 by roll call, to adopt the Resolution Condemning the January 6, 2021
Insurrection and Violence at the U.S. Capitol, as presented.

7.b. Presentations and Discussion: Anti-Racism Training for Councilors
Councilor De Angelis stated the Council is not representative of the Amherst community, spoke about
unconscious bias, need to develop skills to listen with understanding and hear conflicting viewpoints. If
we are going to become an anti-racist town, we need to do the personal work. It can be intimidating and
scary but we have an opportunity to come together and look at ourselves in an environment that we can
trust one another.

undoing racism. All of our wellbeing is tied to racism. No one is safe unless we are all safe and flourish
unless we all flourish. Proposing the Council participate in undoing racism workshop by People’s
Institute.
