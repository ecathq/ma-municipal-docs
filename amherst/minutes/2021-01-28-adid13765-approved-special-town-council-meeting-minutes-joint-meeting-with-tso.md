members can hear and be heard, and announced audio and video recording.
Griesemer declared the presence of a quorum, called the special meeting to order at 6:33 PM, and
confirmed all Councilors participating could hear and be heard.
2. General Public Comment - none
3. Presentation and Discussion Items
a. North Common – updated presentation
Ziomek briefly went over the goals of the project and reviewed the pictures of designs for the common,
including outlining such usages for the space such as public gatherings, holiday celebrations, vigils, etc.
Brestrup said Plan 3 was favored the most from the last meeting, but Plan 2 was also asked to be included
in tonight’s presentation. Brestrup presented the revised Plan 2 which involves a loss of 2 parking spaces
on South Pleasant Street and parallel parking on Boltwood Ave, amongst other revisions.

Brestrup presented the revised Plan 3 which includes 7 “nose-in” parking spaces on Main Street and 12
nose-in parking spaces on Boltwood Ave, along with other parking revisions. Brestrup presented Plan 3A
(a revision of Plan 3) which eliminates parking at the North end of the North Common and the bump-out
onto South Pleasant St and includes back-in parking on Main St and nose-in parking on Boltwood Ave.
Brestrup presented Plan 3B which is the same as Plan 3A except 3B has parallel parking on both sides of
Boltwood Ave and there would be no loss of land on the east side of the North Common
Brestrup showed a comparison chart of the parking space options for each proposed plan. She also said
the maintenance cost is estimated to be $10,000. Brestrup said most of the written councilor questions and
comments have been addressed in the presentation and most councilor input was about the budget.
Ross asked if there are only plans for a sidewalk along the common on Boltwood Ave in Plan 3 because
he did not notice such sidewalks in the other plans. He also asked about the change between nose-in and
back-in parking and why it varies with the different designs. Ross also asked about the Boltwood parking
being different numbers of spaces in different plans but they are different numbers of spaces in different
plans. Mooring said the sidewalk is in some of the plans based on how much parking the council wants to
take from the East side of the common. Mooring said back-in parking seems to be working well in other
parts of the country and drivers would be facing traffic so it is much easier to see traffic while parking and
leaving a spot. Mooring said for Plans 3 and 3A, there is one less space in one of those plans because a
steeper angle is used in the other plan which creates more room for spaces.
Brewer said she does not see the councilor questions and would like have them available to the council.
Ryan asked about safety on the parking on Main St and specifically backing into the spaces on Main
Street. Ryan said he is leaning towards Plan 3B and favors the idea of Boltwood being a one-way street.
Ziomek said the idea would be that in front of Town Hall the raised area would be a space for
celebrations. Ziomek said Main St could still be a one-way street and it could be blocked off if it needed
to be for town events.
Ryan asked about the safety in general on Main St regarding the parking options. Mooring said the
particular section of Main Street that they are discussing is very wide and has enough space to
accommodate a bike lane and parking without creating a safety issue.
Pam asked if Plan 3B is the only one that would have the raised area outside of Town Hall. Mooring said
every plan would have a platform or raised space outside the Town Hall for events and celebrations.
Pam asked what it means to bump out space on South Pleasant and why that would have to take property
on the east side. Mooring said the original Plan 2 included a bump out on South Pleasant St because the
Tree Warden recommended it in order to give more space to the Beech tree on the common. Mooring
added that if they want 2-way traffic, they must take out a little bit of the east side of the North Common.
Pam asked what the space in front of the raised platform area is (grass, concrete, etc.). Mooring said after
walking across the platform and proceed towards South Pleasant Street you would get to an open grass
area.

DuMont asked if it was possible to keep the cost at the amount that has already been funded for the
project and she asked if they have an estimate of how much staff would be needed to maintain the
grounds. Ziomek said not having the 500K from CPAC would cause them to make significant changes to
the plans. Mooring said the $10,000 is the cost they think will cover materials to maintain the ground and
they have not narrowed down the number of staff hours that would be required for maintenance yet.
DuMont asked what other maintenance would need to be done such as perennial plantings or other
plantings. Ziomek said they never envisioned a design with a large area of perennials or any plantings. He
said the design would be sustainable and not require too much maintenance cost besides shrubs and tree
work.
DuMont had concerns about putting additional town funds into the plan and said it is essential to have a
plan that utilizes the funds that they already have for the project. She also had concerns about the
sustainability of the project.
Hanneke asked about the safety of the platform being built in front of Town Hall. She was also concerned
about the safety of the parking and road changes. She said in terms of cost, she would rather see
sustainable materials added to the budget because it may lower maintenance costs in the long run.
Schoen said she would like to compare Plan 2’s safety and parking to Plan 3B, specifically back-in and
nose-in parking. She said she is concerned about the parking safety in 3B. Schoen suggested in Plan 2 to
incorporate Plan 3B’s 7 spaces along one side of Boltwood Ave and 8 on the other by turning Boltwood
Ave into a one-way. She also suggested turning the parking lot in the North Common into a performance
space instead of leaving it as an open field. Schoen asked if they will reach out to the small businesses
downtown and see what their opinions are of this project.
Schreiber was concerned about removing the parking lot in the North Common and said that back-in
parking seemed complicated when he saw it in Somerville, but he does not mind the idea of back-in. He
said parking off the common is his main concern and there needs to be work on the common where the
new land will be in place of the parking lot.
Brewer said she wants the parking lot of the Town Common to remain if it would just be an open field
instead. She would like to compare and contrast a revision of Plan 2 and show both options to people so
that they can see the intention. Mooring said the parking area was left as open space because there are a
lot of possibilities for that space and he wants to know what the council wants the outside of the common
to look like.
Ziomek said accessibility is difficult in the current parking area and having an open space could be very
flexible for different usages. He also suggested adding artwork and sculptures to fill the space. Ziomek
said he is working on the Civil War tablets and they are being advised that the tablets are better displayed
indoors due to their meaning and the possible weather elements that could tarnish them outside.
Ross disagreed with Brewer and Schreiber and said that he is not interested in the details of landscaping
and is more interested in choosing a plan to move forward with.

Pam said a parking consultant gave a number of options that nobody considered and she wants to restore
the nature of a classic New England common. Pam said restoring green spaces is important and she would
rather leave parking to those who are experts in that area.
Ryan suggested sending to the council 2 alternatives, a majority suggestion and a minority suggestion and
tell the council that they have not come to a consensus. He said a minority report could be written and the
council would come to a decision because TSO is split.
DuMont said she wants some discussion about outreach efforts with community members and abutting
businesses. Ziomek said some of the plans proposed are new to the council he would appreciate some
time to outreach to Grace Church to go over some of the revisions with them.
Amherst Business Improvement District Director Gabrielle Gould said their intention is to come to the
Council meeting with comments from all abutting businesses. She suggested having a statue of the Eric
Carle “Hungry Caterpillar.” Gould said the BID and the Chamber are committed to contributing to the
maintenance of the common. She said lighting and signage are important to focus on in regards to
parking.
Steinberg said these plans need to be very clear to the public and TSO needs to figure out the best way to
present the plans to the public and utilize the experts in town for advice on parking and other aspects of
this project.
Ryan said Plan 3B and slightly revised Plan 2 could be agreeable amongst the committee to move forward
with and suggested sending these 2 proposals to the Town Council and the chair would write a report on
the reasoning behind these 2 proposals.
DuMont said the alternative would be waiting until the next meeting to hear the outreach responses. Ryan
said Ziomek could take Plans 3B and slightly-revised 2 to Grace Church and bring them to the council as
well to move the process along and give Ziomek and Mooring a sense of which plans to pursue.
Ziomek said Plan 3B is new and he wants to respect Grace Church’s opinion on the new plan and he is
reluctant to move this forward to the next meeting because he may need more time. Ryan said TSO has
done enough and to move it forward it should go to the council next. Ziomek said they have a very strong
relationship with the BID and the Chamber and they will bring the results of their outreach to the council
meeting.
Ryan said DuMont should write a report to the council that the committee could not come to a consensus
and that the decision is split. Ross said he agrees with Ryan that it’s time to move this to the council. Ross
suggested taking a poll of the councilors present at the meeting to see which plans they prefer.
Griesemer said TSO needs to make a recommendation to the council and then the vote of the council at a
council meeting. DuMont said they could take a poll of just the TSO.
Brewer said she would not be in favor of removing the parking from the common at the TSO level. She
agreed to move forward with Ryan’s suggestion of presenting the Town Council the 2 plans that the
committee is currently split on.

MOTION: Ryan moved, second by Ross that TSO forward to the Town Council Plan 3B and Plan 2 as
revised according to Schoen’s points and that the chair provide a rationale for both in a report to the
Council.
DuMont said she will abstain because she feels that this needs to be looked at with other capital projects
in the budget.
DuMont said she would like the outreach before making a recommendation. Brewer said in this case they
should move this to the council because the outreach would not be enough for her.
Brestrup said at Town Council there will be opportunities to be mixing and matching of the plans
VOTED: 4-1 by roll call (DuMont abstained) that TSO forward to the Town Council Plan 3B and Plan 2
as revised according to Schoen’s points and that the chair provide a rationale for both in a report to the
Council.
Griesemer declared the special Town Council meeting adjourned at 8:22 PM.
