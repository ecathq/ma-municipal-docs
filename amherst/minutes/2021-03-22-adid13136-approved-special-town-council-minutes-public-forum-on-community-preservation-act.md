Delaney responded affirmatively and referenced what is shown on the Appropriation and Transfer Order
FY22-07.
Schoen noted the Belchertown Road affordable housing project CPA appropriation was approved earlier
this year in a separate order.
Bruce Coldham, Amherst resident, noted most projects are Town related and asked inquired about this
change in trend.
Griesemer noted two CPA appropriations are not on this order including the one Schoen announced and
another potential order for $1 million for the Jones Library. Griesemer stated the Town is considered a
respectable applicant.
Delaney agreed the Town makes up majority of the current requests, stated one non-Town proposal was
not accepted because it was ruled not eligible for funds. Delaney stated the CPA has ideas for marketing
and information sessions to advertise the program.
Marshal stated the pandemic may have slowed down the work of private organizations.
Janet Keller, Amherst Resident, asked if this was only comment on the North Common.
Gabriela Horvay, Amherst resident, asked to comment on the North Common project and if that project
will include adding a band shell.
Griesemer stated there are two proposals on the table for the North Common and neither include a band
shell. Griesemer stated there will be a public comment period for the North Common later in the meeting.
There were no further questions or comments raised by members of the public.
