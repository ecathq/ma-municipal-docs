b. Water and Sewer Rates
(1) Presentation
Mangano presented the proposed FY22 Rates and spoke about the following:
• General decline in consumption throughout Town, good from conservation standpoint but there is
a risk to the enterprise fund and fixed costs
• Both enterprise funds have significant capital improvements underway which will increase costs
to the fund including the Centennial water treatment plant, the gravity belt thickener and re-use

grant to offset capital infrastructure projects
• The Town is also working with the Finance Committee to consider alternative rate structures
• New software will allow the Town to analyze consumption data
• Comparison of rates to surrounding towns and state average

e. Addition of Town Council Rules of Procedure rule 10.10 Reappointment of
Members of the Planning Board, Zoning Board of Appeals, and Non-Voting
Resident Members of the Finance Committee – Second Discussion
(1) Governance, Organization, and Legislation (GOL) Committee
Report
DuMont stated she met with the chair of GOL and came up with minor edits.
MOTION: DuMont moved, seconded by Schoen, to amend Town Council Rules of Procedure Section 10
by inserting the following:
10.10 Reappointment of Members of the Planning Board, Zoning Board of Appeals, and
Resident Members of the Finance Committee
Generally, if a Member of the Planning Board, Zoning Board of Appeals or a Resident Member
of the Finance Committee has completed a term, they are given preference in reappointment for
up to least six years, to take advantage of the experience and expertise gained, and to honor the
voluntary time commitment of members, as long as the member is an active, contributing member
of the Board or Committee. If a person has served six consecutive years or will have served more
than six consecutive years if reappointed, and there are other qualified applicants, preference will
be given to a newcomer. In cases where special training or expertise is required, longer periods of
service may be appropriate. A committee or Board member is under no obligation to accept
reappointment, nor is the Town Council obligated to offer reappointment.
Brewer asked for clarification of what motions were posted in the Council packet.
MOTION: Ross moved, second by Steinberg, to amend the motion by replacing the entire motion with
the following: To refer to the Governance, Organization, and Legislation Committee development of a
Town Council policy on appointments to the Planning Board, Zoning Board of Appeals, non-voting
members to the Finance Committee, and Districting Advisory Board for recommendation to the Town
Council with a report to the Town Council by July 12, 2021.
Ross spoke to the motion, stating that the original motion should be a policy not a Council rule, that this
motion to amend would direct the GOL to create a policy to be adopted by the Council, and that the
original motion disregards most of the former Outreach, Communications, and Appointments (OCA)
Committee process.
Schreiber commented that this does not seem ready for Council action, but that the message has been
heard.
Steinberg spoke about selection criteria, and spoke in favor of the motion.
Brewer spoke about the OCA process and the urgency of the discussion when there are appointments
needing Council action in the near future. Brewer spoke in favor of a Council policy.
Pam spoke about the perception of applicants, and that the process may appear hostile to applicants.
DuMont spoke in favor of the original motion, stating the language comes from the Board and Committee
Handbook.
