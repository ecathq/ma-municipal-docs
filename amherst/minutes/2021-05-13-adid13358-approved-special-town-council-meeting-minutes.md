Town of Amherst
Special Meeting of the Town Council
Thursday, May 13, 2021 12:00 noon.
Virtual Meeting
Minutes
1. Call to Order
Councilors Participating Remotely: Bahl-Milne, Brewer, De Angelis, DuMont, Griesemer, Hanneke,
Pam, Ross, Ryan, Schoen, Steinberg, and Swartz
Councilors Absent: Schreiber
Others Participating Remotely: Town Manager Paul Bockelman, Clerk of the Council Athena O’Keeffe,
and KP Law Attorneys Lauren Goldberg and Gregg Corbo
After confirming all participants could hear and be heard, President Griesemer declared the presence of a
quorum, called the meeting to order at 12:00 p.m., and announced audio and video recording.
Note: Pursuant to Governor Baker’s March 12, 2020 Order Suspending Certain Provisions of the Open
Meeting Law, G.L. c. 30A, §18, this meeting of the Town Council was conducted via remote
participation.
2. Executive Session
a. [MGL c.30A §21(a) 3] To discuss strategy with respect to litigation: case number
2180CV00043 Gray, Carol et al vs. Susan Audette in her capacity Acting Town Clerk; as
an open meeting may have a detrimental effect on the litigating position of the body.
MOTION: Griesemer moved, second by Ross, to convene in executive session under MGL c.30A§21(a)
3, to discuss strategy with respect to litigation: case number 2180CV00043 Gray, Carol et al vs. Susan
Audette in her capacity Acting Town Clerk; I declare that an open meeting may have a detrimental effect
on the litigating position of the body; the Town Council will not reconvene in open session following the
executive session.
VOTED 11-0 by roll call (Councilors Bahl-Milne, Brewer, De Angelis, DuMont, Griesemer, Hanneke,
Pam, Ross, Ryan, Schoen, Steinberg and Swartz voted Yes; Councilor Schreiber was absent.) to convene
in executive session under MGL c.30A§21(a) 3, to discuss strategy with respect to litigation: case number
2180CV00043 Gray, Carol et al vs. Susan Audette in her capacity Acting Town Clerk; the chair having
declared that an open meeting may have a detrimental effect on the litigating position of the body; the
Town Council will not reconvene in open session following the executive session.
3. Topics Not Reasonably Anticipated by the President 48 Hours in Advance of the
meeting
4. Adjourn
