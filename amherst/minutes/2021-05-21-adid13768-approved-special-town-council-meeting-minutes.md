Town of Amherst
Special Meeting of the Town Council
Tuesday, May 21, 2021 8:00 a.m.
Virtual Meeting
Minutes
1. Call to Order
Councilors Participating Remotely: Bahl-Milne, Brewer, De Angelis, DuMont, Griesemer, Hanneke,
Pam, Ross, Ryan, Schoen, Schreiber, Steinberg, Swartz joined the meeting at 8:04.
Councilors Absent: None
Others Participating Remotely: Town Manager Paul Bockelman, Clerk of the Council Athena O’Keeffe,
and KP Law Attorneys Lauren Goldberg and Gregg Corbo
After confirming all participants could hear and be heard, President Griesemer declared the presence of a
quorum, and called the meeting to order at 8:01 a.m.
Note: Pursuant to Governor Baker’s March 12, 2020 Order Suspending Certain Provisions of the Open
Meeting Law, G.L. c. 30A, §18, this meeting of the Town Council was conducted via remote
participation.
Griesemer stated that at 4:53 p.m., yesterday the Town received a communication that the plaintiffs have
dismissed the case against the Town without prejudice and therefore there is no need to meet in executive
session.
Councilors discussed how information regarding the dismissed case should be shared with the public at
the Council meeting scheduled for Monday, May 24.
2. Executive Session
a. [MGL c.30A §21(a) 3] To discuss strategy with respect to litigation: case number
2180CV00043 Gray, Carol et al vs. Susan Audette in her capacity Acting Town Clerk; as
an open meeting may have a detrimental effect on the litigating position of the body.
The Town Council did not enter executive session during this meeting.
3. Topics Not Reasonably Anticipated by the President 48 Hours in Advance of the meeting -
None
4. Adjourn
Griesemer declared the meeting adjourned at 8:09 a.m.
