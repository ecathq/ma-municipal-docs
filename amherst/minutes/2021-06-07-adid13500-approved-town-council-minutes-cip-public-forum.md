2. Capital Improvement Program Public Forum – Charter Sec. 5.7(d)
Sean Mangano gave a brief presentation by on the FY 22 Budget Capital Improvement Program
including:
• Restores investments in 8.5% levy allocating $4, 671, 888 to cash capital
• Incorporates capital requirements of 3 of the 4 new building projects,
• Addresses deferred maintenance of existing infrastructure
• Maintains commitments to roads and sidewalks
• Highlights include
$100,000 for sustainability projects
o
$50,000 for accessibility improvements
o
$350,000 for roof repairs at all 3 elementary schools
o
$4,622,500 for borrowing for the Fire Station, Public Works buildings, Street sweeper
o
and pumper fire truck and chiller replacement at police station
