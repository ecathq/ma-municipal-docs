c. Town Council Policy on Making Recommendations for Town Council
Appointments to Multiple-Member Bodies – First Discussion
Ryan said this topic was generated by the fact that there are 2 different Council committees that follow
slightly different procedures for their appointments. He said the OCA process was their guide in forming
the policy. Ryan stated that in the GOL report, he highlighted sections where there were changes,
important recommendations or disagreements amongst committee members.
Ryan said GOL recommends when a vacancy notice is put out, everyone is required to submit a CAF
because it alerts Councilors as to who is in the applicant pool. Ryan said another recommendation is for a
clear statement of preference, and that every opening is treated as a vacant position. He said stating a
preference is important, but there is no guarantee. He added that one member argued that preference
should be a strong sense and reappointment should be almost guaranteed. Ryan said GOL also
recommends keeping SOIs because they are valuable tools and that the applicant pool not officially close
until the SOIs are posted publicly. He further stated that GOL recommends interview questions be
submitted in advance and noted that one member felt strongly about asking follow ups, but the committee
decided against follow up questions.
Steinberg said in Section 9 of the policy, it is fairly stringent that someone who cannot make it to the
interview will not considered which seems unfair. Ryan said there were a couple of factors that made the
committee decide on that stricter rule, one was to level the playing field to by having everyone present for
group interviews.
Griesemer said this is only for Town Council appointments, not for Town Manager appointments.
Schreiber said he would normally agree with Steinberg, but everything they need to be looking at this
policy through an equity lens and only residents of Amherst can apply for these positions, so he agrees
with Ryan.
Brewer said there is an unwritten practice of councilors to try and make interview times work for every
interviewer so that they can all be present. She asked if it is purposeful that the type of interview (group
or private) is not clear. Ryan said that decision is left to the committee. He said flexibility is something
they strive to realize but could not put that in a policy.
