1. Call to Order
Griesemer declared the presence of a quorum, called the special meeting to order at 6:10 PM,
confirmed all members can hear and be heard, and announced audio and video recording.
2. Announcements
3. Public Forum to Address the Master Plan – Charter Sec. 2.13
The public forum shall present the public with context, alert the public to
upcoming issues, and collect feedback from the public.
Suzannah Muspratt – Muspratt voiced concern that recent zoning changes all have ramifications
for one another as they are being dealt with one by one. She commented that she is very
concerned about not following the Master Plan in terms of planning before zoning amendments.
Muspratt suggested having an implementation committee. She also stated that parts of the Master
Plan having to do with cultural and economic aspects and historic preservation have not been
addressed.
Mary Sayer – Sayer commented that the Master Plan encourages community input, but there was
a survey that was going to be conducted regarding the East Pleasant Street sidewalk where and
that survey still has not been done. Sayer stated that she wants to echo the comments of the
previous speaker.
Town Manager Bockelman said the East Pleasant Street project has been in the offing for a
while, but an engineer just signed a contract for it. Brestrup said this issue does come up at TAC
meetings a lot and it is an important project that will be taken care of.

Schoen said the origin of this project was from a resident proposal supported by JCPC and
following through on these types of projects is very important.
Cinda Jones – disconnected
Tom Randall – Randall said the noise situation for residents living near UMass is untenable. He
said at night there are parades of loud students and the town can do something about this.
Randall stated that he is in support of the letter submitted about this issue and they must find a
way to control student housing.
Kitty Axelson-Berry – Axelson-Berry asked if the Master Plan says anything about UMass
housing. She also asked how the town envisions handling the need for more UMass housing.
Brestrup said the Master Plan talks about the need for dialogue between the town and UMass but
does not specifically talk about the need for UMass to house more students on campus. She said
the Master Plan is more of an outline of what the town can do but the town does not have control
over what UMass does.
Bockelman said every Monday he has a meeting with the Fire Department, Police Department,
and Dean of Students. He said he also meets with a representative from UMass with the
Assistant Town Manager to go through the events of the week. Bockelman added that he meets
regularly with a broader group about COVID-19, and the purpose for most of these meetings is
communication.
4. Topics Not Reasonably Anticipated 48 Hours in Advance of the Meeting - None
5. Adjourn
Griesemer declared the meeting adjourned at 6:29 PM.
