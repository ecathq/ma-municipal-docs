a. Primer on the Master Plan – Planning Director Christine Brestrup
Griesemer explained the purpose of the primer on the Master Plan and introduced Planning
Director Christine Brestrup.
Brestrup spoke about the following on the Master Plan:
• Master Plan is a broader plan for the town
• Many zoning changes since the last Master Plan (Apartments definition, ADUs, Mixed-
Use, etc.)
• The new Master Plan process should start in 2025
• The definition of a Master Plan – the beginning of a process
• A Master Plan is required by M.G.L. Chapter 41, Section 81D
• This Master Plan was created with extensive public input and research
• The history of the Master Plan
• 2006-2010 Master Plan process and Town Council adoption of the 2020 November
Master Plan
• Master Plan chapters required by MGL – (Goals and Policies, Economic Development,
etc.)
• The organization of the Master Plan
