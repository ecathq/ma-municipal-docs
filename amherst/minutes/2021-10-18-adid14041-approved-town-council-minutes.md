a. Redistricting – First Discussion
Dujovne called the District Advisory Board (DAB) meeting to order at 6:53 p.m.
Districting Advisory Board members spoke about the following:
• The DAB has 2 members per district and 3 non-voting members
• Review of the DAB charge and purpose – to review the results of the census to make sure
precincts meet state and federal requirements
• DAB process: they initially worked with a map of 15 precincts, but switched to 10 precincts
because the projected census numbers were much higher than the census results
• DAB priorities for redistricting
• DAB’s public outreach efforts: public comments, community input via email, posted draft maps,
and district meetings
• Population change: Amherst grew by 1,444 residents mainly from honors college dorms at
UMass and new downtown housing
• 8 out of 10 current precincts do not satisfy LEDRC guidelines
• 2 districts do not satisfy LEDRC (districts 3 and 5)
• DAB challenges: districts needing adjustments were not right next to each other, college
residential buildings are very dense within a small portion of land, and census blocks
• Different variations of maps the board came up with and the proposed map
• Reasons to recommend: meets state requirements, provides equity for voters, racial
representation, and minimizes the number of residents changing precincts and districts
• Next steps: submit the map by October 29
• State communications
Schoen stated that the report is very clear, and this was a challenging task. She said distributing precincts
makes sense, especially for Precinct 1.
Ryan said the proposed map does seem to leave the Dana Street neighborhood out in Precinct 4. He
suggested using Northampton Road as a dividing line.
