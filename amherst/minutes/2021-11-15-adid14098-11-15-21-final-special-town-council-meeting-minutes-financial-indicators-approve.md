Demling stated that there is no reimbursement for charter school tuition, only the increase year to year can
get reimbursed. Demling said there are some needed programs for students that he would like to add, and
staff should consider an increase for schools.
Brewer asked what the public can expect in following this process. Griesemer said the budget process
begins after this meeting and tonight is the budget forum. She explained that Bockelman would then
present the budget to the Council. Steinberg added that the Finance Committee will have a first discussion
on this tomorrow at 2 PM. He said at the December 20 Council meeting they hopefully will adopt the
guidelines.
Pam asked if any state grants have been used in the operating budget. Mangano said ARPA funds will be
used for new DEI position, proposed for firefighters, and one-time costs.
Lefebvre asked about the bump in reserves. She said that there are a lot of vehicles and other capital needs
that were pushed out so that more money could be put into the operating budget.
Hanneke asked if they hit the $15 minimum wage. She commented that reserves are quite high and asked
what the plan is about using more of the reserves to save borrowing costs/capital projects coming up.
Mangano said the library minimum wage is not there yet. He said they are anticipating if the gap in the
projection is not closed, they may need to use reserves to support that transition. Bockelman said this is a
fundamental decision that the Council will be developing.
Brewer asked if there is going to be a BCG meeting. Bockelman said this is the proposal and it will
revolve around the financial guidelines the Council puts forward.
Steinberg asked Mangano to supplement about ARPA funds, specifically transitioning the 6th grade.
Mangano said ARPA funds can help with the additional costs for the 6th grade transition.
Bahl-Milne asked about the drop in the school budget. Mangano said all operating budgets dropped and
the elementary schools dropped more partly because of accounting mechanisms. He explained that they
used to include the charter and choice tuition in the elementary budget, but an auditor told the Town not
to do that.
Schoen said they should be looking at 2 or 3 years of reserves and be careful about moving that money
around. She said the Elementary School Building Committee needs to pay a designer fee and asked where
that would be in the budget. Mangano said that would be in the authorized debt for that last year. Schoen
asked if they should pull that fee out of the reserves instead.
McDonald said she is just seeing this for the first time and hasn’t had time to fully read and understand.
She said it looks awkward that the budget is increasing 3.7% and the schools and library are getting 2.5%.
Mangano said that is not unheard of, but there is more conversation that needs to happen.
Lefebvre asked if library employees were included in the NFT charts. Mangano said he does not think
library employees are included in the charts.
De Angelis asked how they are planning for staffing costs. Bockelman said the Health Department will
look more different than it did before the pandemic. He stated that the CARES funds support 4 additional
firefighters and ARPA funds can also support those positions.
