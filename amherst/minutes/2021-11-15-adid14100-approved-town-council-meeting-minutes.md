Schoen asked what specifically the 6th grade transition funds are for. Mangano said there is the annual
cost for 3 years, signage, and upfront costs. He said there also may be rent costs and utilizing regional
staff.
Schoen asked if they would have some sort of metrics for measuring the progress of these positions.
Mangano said the Grant Coordinator is only managing this grant and working with recipients. He said the

Schoen asked if they could try to target Premium Pay to the lower end of the salary scale. Mangano said
the grant targets low to moderate incomes and that could be included in the eligibility criteria.
Schoen asked about a forgiveness program for the water fund. Mangano said they still have an allocation
for the resident emergency fund which is targeted toward COVID-19 effects.
Hanneke said childcare is not on the list of transition planning and asked if they are going to fund an
expanded childcare fund for 4 years and then drop it. She stated that she opposes using ARPA funds for
the 6th grade transition because schools also get their own ARPA funds.
Mangano said the 4 firefighter positions are not currently in the budget so those would be added. He said
in terms of the childcare allocation the intent is to do a request for proposals and get submissions from
existing preschool providers or others to expand their numbers. He said the schools allocated half of their
$1.5 million, but they still have 2 more years they are planning for and the major focus is mental health
for schools.
Pam asked for more information on mental health services for schools, adding that many parents are
spending their own money on counseling. Mangano said they can get something sent to the Council about
the draft of their plan.
Ryan asked if the DEI Coordinator is a multi-year position. Mangano said they are expecting it to last 2-3
years. Ryan asked about the affordable housing stock, specifically if it could be targeted for
homeownership. Mangano said they don’t know yet, but the Affordable Housing Trust (AHT) has ideas.
He commented that the plan is to work with the AHT to come up with a plan for allocation.
Ryan asked if the homelessness funds are attempting to find a permanent solution to shelters. Mangano
said they are exploring that and transitional housing. Bockelman said the goal is to try and have a
permanent location the shelter can utilize.
Bahl-Milne asked for the Youth Empowerment Center if they can include youth leadership in that and
community engagement as part of that process. She asked if the Recreation Director is going to be
compensated for work on this and said $30K seems low for the DEI position. Mangano said there are 2
DEI positions, one is already in the operating budget and one is partially funded in the operating budget.
He said he is not sure if the Recreation Director would be compensated extra.
Bahl-Milne asked for clarification on the 4 firefighter positions. Mangano said these positions were
originally hired during COVID with CARES funds and they were temporary. He said the plan is to make
them permanent and would be 4 more than they currently have.
Brewer asked about the pay for the DEI Director and Grants Coordinator. Mangano said the number is
different because the DEI position could be 3 years or could be extended. He said they are uncertain about
whether they can fund people after a certain point.
