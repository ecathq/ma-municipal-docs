2. Announcements
3. Presentation and Discussion
(1) Town Manager Performance Evaluation
Brewer expressed concerned that not all Councilors filled out the evaluation. She said after voting, they
should have had a conversation about the process.
Steinberg said this process was good and he is impressed by most comments. He spoke of the complaints
from new Councilors with the ranking system and said they are starting a new cycle of a new Council
every other year so they must be careful.
Schoen said she liked how this was set up, adding that many spoke of the strategic partnership with
UMass. She said that should have also been in the financial goals. Schoen also spoke about capital
projects and parking as a bigger set of issues.
Hanneke said for the Community Health and Safety portion of the document, the section on EMS staffing
was not given enough due. Schoen agreed with the Fire/EMS comment by Hanneke, adding that
sometimes it is good when the Town Manager says the Council is on a collision course and can’t do all of
these things.

Bahl-Milne spoke about the difficult conversations that were had while developing CRESS, adding that
having a DEI Director is needed.
Pam asked about the staffing study in the evaluation. Griesemer said it is mentioned in the goals.
Hanneke said for the Economic Vitality Section, the parking working group comments are not reflective
of what Councilors have said.
Hanneke said for the Four Major Capital Investments Section, the portion about the Fire Department and
DPW were not strong enough.
Schoen said they need a new approach which would involve decoupling DPW and AFD. She said they
also have a plan on this, but a hard choice needs to be made in the plan. Schoen said this issue will be
looming over the next couple of years.
Griesemer said they will start with the Housing Affordability Section at the regular Council meeting.
