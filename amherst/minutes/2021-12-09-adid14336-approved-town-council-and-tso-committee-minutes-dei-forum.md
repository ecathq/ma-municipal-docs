Martha Hanner – Hanner commented on behalf of the Amherst League of Women Voters Racial Justice
Committee that they strongly support the formation of a DEI Department with a Director and Assistant
Director. She said this is an exciting opportunity to implement the recommendations that the CRESS team
proposed. Hanner said this should be viewed as a long-term commitment to support the DEI office.
Jonathan Sivel – Sivel commented that he is a member of the Amherst-Pelham Education Association and
is in support of the CRESS program and efforts of the CSWG. He said educators see the way students and
colleagues are impacted by systemic racism. Sivel said he is rejecting the assumption that funding other
necessary progressive town programs somehow detracts from the wellbeing of the school community. He
said Amherst schools will benefit from funding the CRESS program fully and Amherst has a long way to
go for all residents to thrive.
Lydia Irons – Irons commented that they support the DEI as a member of Defund413 and as an Amherst
resident. They said they appreciate the work of the CSWG and also support the CRESS program.
Ash Hartwell – Hartwell commented as a member of the League of Women Voters Racial Justice
Committee and expressed his admiration and support for the CSWG’s work. He also appreciated the work
that has gone on in the CRESS Implementation Team. Hartwell clarified that the office is getting
underway in February but will be fully operational later than that. He said part of the DEI Charge is to
work on a larger vision of racial justice and equity, with safety as the top priority. Hartwell spoke of the
need for diversity in government and the establishment of mechanisms for recruiting and including
BIPOC residents in town government. He said this must be looked at holistically.
Jennifer Page – Page commented that the driver’s license requirement in the DEI Director job description
should be removed. She said not having a license could be due to a health condition or sight issue and
excludes many qualified applicants.
