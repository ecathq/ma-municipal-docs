3. Presentation: Town Manager’s Reorganization Plan
a. Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion (DEI) Office
Town Manager Bockelman presented the following: work mainly by BIPOC and 12-14:
• CSWG recommendation
• Goals for the DEI Office
• The work the DEI department will do – provide training/resources for staff, support HR, outreach
to BIPOC businesses and communities, etc.
• Plan – recruit a Director, finalize the job descriptions, prepare the office space
• Organizational structure
Mangano presented the following:
• $3,750 start-up cost
• $163,000 for the Director and Assistant Director position
• Other operating costs – $240,500 in total
• Implications – will start to see a gap in the municipal operating budget
• Strategies to close the gap
• Funding plan – ARPA, operating budget, existing appropriations
Bockelman answered some of the anticipated questions. He said he currently has 14 direct reports which
is a lot, but they will try to have some reports reorganized. Bockelman said in response to the question of
losing an administrative position they should be okay for coverage. He said the DEI Office will be on the
same floor as the Town Manager’s Office and there will be goals and metrics created to measure the
success of the DEI Director.

Bahl-Milne said a challenge they heard of with the Economic Development Office was it didn’t feel

b. Community Responders for Equity, Safety, and Service (CRESS)
Bockelman presented the following:
• Charter section 6.1
• CSWG recommendation for the CRESS program
• Town Council Resolution – voted on June 21, 2021
• Reorganization proposal
• Purpose – provide safety in situations that are non-violent
• Guiding principles from LEAP
• Implementation team members and support
• Implementation plan – scope of CRESS, calls directed to CRESS, job description, etc.
• Implementation team work so far
• Types of calls that could go to CRESS
• 2 team members per shift and 4 days on 2 days off scheduling
• How to contact CRESS
• Training and education – knowing when to call for support, recognizing a crime scene, CPR/first
aid, etc.
• Bylaws that need to be changed, deleted, or added
Mangano presented the following:
• Financial implications for operating costs
• Financial implications for non-operating costs
• Fiscal challenges and strategies
• Funding plan for operating costs – existing appropriations, 90k earmark from the State
• Non-operating costs – ARPA funds, other grants
• Status – reviewing with Town Council committees, Town Council approval, hiring,
implementation, etc.
• Status from January to February
• Status from February to April
Bockelman addressed some of the likely questions. Vernon-Jones said the Town has considered a
contracted-out model and decided that if they want to meet the needs of Amherst, they would be better off
with their own program. He said there is also lots of turnover in these positions and they are hoping to
hire and keep people.
Bockelman reviewed the other likely questions and answers in the slides. Kenneally explained the
compensation rates by the Town.
Bockelman said the intent of the program is that the responders will be independent but will also be
cooperating with the Fire Department and Police Department.

Livingstone said they are realizing there needs to be more of a relationship between the APD/AFD than
the CSWG was hoping for. He said they are worried about the community responders’ safety. Livingstone
gave examples of calls that were not originally violent but turned violent.
Vernon-Jones said there will need to be joint training and cooperation. He referred everyone to the LEAP
report.
Bockelman said he doesn’t know of any similar programs that have been funded by cutting the Police
Department.
i. Councilor questions
Steinberg asked what will happen if the hiring process doesn’t go as quickly as planned. Kenneally said
that is a nation-wide issue right now and they are sending ads out and would have to extend the process if
necessary. Bockelman said this is a different kind of process and hopefully people will be excited about it.
Ryan said in FY23 they have a $300K shortfall and FY24 and beyond, they have between half a million
and a million-dollar shortfall. Mangano said the $300K deficit is referring to the overall budget and at this
early stage that is not overly concerning. He said the Finance Committee looked at the overall budget and
the municipal operating budget which is where gaps start to show.
Ryan asked how many unions need to be talked to. Bockelman said there are 2 police unions and a fire
union. Kenneally said both police unions and SCIU have been notified, but they have not notified the fire
union yet.
Brewer asked when the program is new, can someone who is calling in who does not want to be diverted
to CRESS request police. Livingstone said a lot of that is going to be the responsibility of the dispatch
system. He said they are trained to ask the right questions to see which calls go where. Livingstone said if
uncertain or not sure, they should call for police or fire.
Ryan said in FY22 the Police Department is down 2 positions and asked if that means there are 44
budgeted positions. Livingstone confirmed that there are 44 budgeted positions and 2 administrative staff.
Bahl-Milne asked if $22,500 is an adequate compensation. She also asked about opportunities to grow
within this department. Kenneally said compensation is very dependent on the job descriptions so they
still have not rated them. She explained that they used the social union line and non-union scale for
estimating. Kenneally added that they will do a proper analysis once the job description is established.
Mangano said the estimated salary is about $61K.
Bockelman said right now they only have a Director and responders, but sometimes people move to
different departments.
Nelson confirmed that Brewer’s question would be talked about. He said if someone asks for a cop, they
will get a cop or whoever else they ask for and that is not going to be a discussion point.
Schoen said the Finance Committee had a conversation about mentoring and opportunities which is noted
in the report.
