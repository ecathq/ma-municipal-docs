De Angelis asked about the condition regarding consultation with Disability Access Advisory Committee
(DAAC).
Mooring spoke about collaboration with the DAAC.
Schoen asked about the decision to make the direction northbound and bicycle lanes.
Ross spoke about TSO deliberation regarding traffic direction and TAC input on bicycle lanes.
Mooring spoke about the roundabout changing directions rather than traffic making left-hand turns during
heavy traffic periods.
Schoen asked if a left-turn restriction would be possible.
Ross noted the TAC considered but did not recommend a restriction on left turns.
MOTION: Griesemer moved, second by Ross, to approve the following changes to parking and
permanent changes to the public way on North Pleasant St from Hallock St to Triangle St:
• Conversion of traffic flow to one-way traffic northbound from McLellan St to Triangle St
• Creation of back-in angled parking spaces on the eastern side of North Pleasant St
• Parking prohibited as a tow zone on the western side of North Pleasant St
• Removal of the grass belt on the western side
With the following conditions:
• Complete streets approach is applied to the final design to maximize bicycle and
pedestrian safety and use, including consideration of a separate southbound lane for
cyclists either at street or sidewalk level
• DAAC will be consulted on the number and placement of accessible spaces
• The Town will return to the Town Council for approval of parking regulation of new
spaces, including a public hearing on the mix of metered and permit parking and meter
regulations
VOTED 10-0, with 3 absent, by roll call, (Bahl-Milne, De Angelis, Griesemer, Hanneke, Pam, Ross,
Ryan, Schoen, Schreiber, and Steinberg voted Yes; Brewer, DuMont, and Swartz were absent) to
approve the following changes to parking and permanent changes to the public way on North Pleasant St
from Hallock St to Triangle St:
• Conversion of traffic flow to one-way traffic northbound from McLellan St to Triangle St
• Creation of back-in angled parking spaces on the eastern side of North Pleasant St
• Parking prohibited as a tow zone on the western side of North Pleasant St
• Removal of the grass belt on the western side
With the following conditions:
• Complete streets approach is applied to the final design to maximize bicycle and
pedestrian safety and use, including consideration of a separate southbound lane for
cyclists either at street or sidewalk level
• DAAC will be consulted on the number and placement of accessible spaces
• The Town will return to the Town Council for approval of parking regulation of new
spaces, including a public hearing on the mix of metered and permit parking and meter

MOTION: Griesemer moved, second by Pam, in accordance with Charter Sec. 6.1, to approve the Town

Community Responders for Equity, Safety, and Service.
