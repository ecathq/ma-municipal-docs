8. Action Items
b. Adoption of Town Council Rules of Procedure – Charter Sec. 2.6(d)
Griesemer stated a vote is not needed, the Governance, Organization, and Legislation Committee will
consider revisions and make recommendations to the Town Council regarding any changes.
MOTION: Hanneke moved, second by De Angelis, to Refer Rules 1.2, 3.3b, 8.4, and 8.6, to Governance,
Organization, and Legislation Committee for report and recommendation by February 28, 2022.
Hanneke spoke to the motion, stated that Council committees are not currently governed by rules, 3.3b is
not in conformance with the law, 8.4 is unnecessary, and a modification to 8.6 could be considered to
remove the requirement for committee review of Proclamations and Town Manager appointments.
Schoen asked that other Councilors bring suggestions for review of rules.
Pam asked about presentations and discussion of issues. How we hold a discussion. Spoke about work
meetings. Suggested a retreat topic for how we hold discussions and have more fruitful discussions.
Bahl-Milne spoke about debate and discussion to guide us to create a safe space for how we have
discussion and make sure everyone is heard.
c. Town Council Meeting Schedule

for the current agenda item.
VOTED unanimously, 13-0, by roll call, to suspend Town Council Rules of Procedure rule 8.4 for the
current agenda item.
MOTION: Griesemer moved, second by Schoen, to adopt the Town Council 2022 Meeting Schedule, as

use of the Town Room, and the day of the week the Council meets.
VOTED unanimously, 13-0, by roll call, to adopt the Town Council 2022 Meeting Schedule, as
presented.

Councilors discussed the following:

• Public participation
• Making attendees visible on screen
• Difficulty building relationships in a virtual format

Hanneke spoke in support of meeting in person, and noted that the legislature may be taking up
permanent changes to the Open Meeting Law.
MOTION: Griesemer moved, second by Hanneke, to suspend Town Council Rules of Procedure rule 8.4
for the current agenda item.
VOTED unanimously, 13-0, by roll call, to suspend Town Council Rules of Procedure rule 8.4 for the
current agenda item.
MOTION: Griesemer moved, second by Hanneke, effective immediately, to hold all Town Council
meetings in the in person meeting format until April 1, 2022.
VOTED 9-3-1 by roll call (Bahl-Milne, Devlin Gauthier, Griesemer, Hanneke, Lopes, Miller, Schoen,
Steinberg, Taub voted Yes; De Angelis, Rooney, and Walker voted No; Pam abstained) effective
immediately, to hold all Town Council meetings in the in person meeting format until April 1, 2022.
Councilors spoke in support of virtual public access and discussed how to determine who will be invited
physically in the room.
Bockelman spoke about the savings of time for professionals and staff attending virtual rather than in
person meetings.
MOVED: Hanneke moved, second by Devlin Gauthier, to allow virtual public access until April 1, 2022.
VOTED unanimously, 13-0, by roll call, to allow virtual public access until April 1, 2022.
Walker asked about public comment and spoke in favor of reading public comments during the meeting
and offering a voicemail option for public comment.
Bahl-Milne spoke about district meetings as a manner of connecting with residents and the Town Services
and Outreach Committee taking up the outreach portion of their charge.
Councilors discussed consistency with committee meeting formats.
MOTION Griesemer moved, second by Rooney, to instruct Council committees to hold meetings in the
virtual meeting format until April 1, 2022.
VOTED unanimously, 13-0, by roll call, to instruct Council committees to hold meetings in the virtual
meeting format until April 1, 2022.
