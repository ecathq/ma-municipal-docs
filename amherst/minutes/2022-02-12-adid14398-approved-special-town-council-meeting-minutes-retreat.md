Town of Amherst
Special Town Council Meeting – Retreat
Saturday, February 12, 2022, 9:00 am
Virtual Meeting
Minutes
Present: Bahl-Milne, De Angelis, Devlin Gauthier, Griesemer, Hanneke, Lopes, Miller, Pam, Schoen,
Taub, Walker
Participating remotely: Rooney (joined at 11:20 am), and Steinberg
Absent: None
Others present: Town Manager Bockelman, Assistant Town Manager Ziomek, Clerk of the Council
O’Keeffe
1. Call to Order
At 9:14 am, President Griesemer declared the presence of a quorum, called the meeting to order,
confirmed all participants could hear and be heard and announced audio and video recording.
2. Announcements
3. Facilitated Discussion
a. Introduction and Framing Today’s Work
Facilitator Erin Cohen introduced herself and Councilors spoke about the intentions for the retreat and the
selection process for a facilitator.
Cohen spoke about the outcomes, agenda, roles, and rules for the meeting today.
b. Ice Breaker - Individual Values Sharing
Councilors spoke about their individual values.
c. Concepts for Healthy Group Engagement
Cohen spoke about collaboration, productive and unproductive dissent and conflict, and listening.
d. Discussion of Council Procedures, Norms, and Values
Cohen spoke about the Council’s norms and values.
Councilors reflected on their inward and outward facing values.

Councilors reflected on the values echoed in the Town Council’s Rules of Procedure.
e. Setting Priorities - Reflection on Pre-Retreat Survey Results
Councilors reviewed the Council’s pre-retreat priorities, and reflected individually on the challenges the
Council may face in accomplishing its goals.
Rooney joined the meeting at 11:20 am.
Councilors discussed the pre-retreat priorities in terms of challenges.
Councilors individually reflected on processes to pursue and accomplish their priority areas and goals.
f. Designing Process, Part I - How might we pursue priorities?
Councilors discussed considerations when moving forward with priorities, and individually put their
combined ideas into a process flow chart.
g. Designing Process, Part II - Practice, and Applying Values
After a lunch break, Councilors discussed how a specific priority or Council action might move through
the process flow chart that was discussed earlier.
Lopes left the meeting at 1:30 pm.
Councilors discussed challenges and obstacles in working through the process on a specific issue.
h. Closing
Councilors reflected on the facilitated discussion, and shared their personal takeaways and appreciation
for the discussion and facilitator.
4. Councilor Discussion
Councilors discussed work sessions in terms of length, timing, frequency, potential topics, and Councilor
capacity for additional or longer meetings.
5. Topics Not Reasonably Anticipated by the Chair 48 Hours in Advance of the Meeting
6. Adjourn
Griesemer declared the meeting adjourned at 2:24 pm.
Respectfully submitted,
Athena O’Keeffe
Clerk of the Council
