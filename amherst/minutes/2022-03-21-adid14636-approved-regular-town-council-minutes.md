c. Polling Locations – First Discussion
Susan Audette says that the goal was to keep the polling locations as close to the precincts as possible.
There will be mail-in voting as well.
Audette said we’re still in the process of redistricting. Once the new polling places are established we will
know more. Devlin Gauthier said that if someone was in one district and is now in a new district she
wants to be sure their vote will still be counted. Audette said it will. Hanneke proposed putting all of
district one to Emmanuel Church and other changes as well.
Schoen said she would like to see all of UMass voting in one place. Audette says the town can’t have
more than 4,000 voters per precinct. Rooney said it is important to have a polling station at the Bangs
Community Center for the residents of the Anne Whalen House and the Clark House.
Lopes said that transportation can be a big issue involving voter participation. Rooney asked if we can
make it easier for UMass students to vote. Griesemer asked Audette to come back to the Council on April
4 or April 25.
d. Proposed Preservation of Historic Structures General Bylaw, Rescission of
Demolition Delay Zoning Bylaw
Jane Wald said that in 2016 there was a realization that a demolition delay zoning bylaw was needed to be
linked to the Historic Structures General Bylaw. The new name, the preservation bylaw, looks to the
future as Amherst tries to maintain its historic structures.
Breger said the existing bylaw has been successful in preserving buildings. When a building more than 50
years old is set for demolition it is decided by the town if it is a significant structure. If it is then

demolition is put on hold for 12 months. Three major changes being proposed to the bylaw. The
definition of demolition is being changed. The new bylaw has a more clear definition of demolition. It
would now include historic architectural features. The second change is in determining when a building is
historically significant. The historical Commission will decide. Third change is that the building dept.
gives a demolition permit.
Hanneke said that there will have to be a hearing on rescinding the zoning bylaw and the general bylaw.
Those hearings have to take place within 65 days of today. The Community Resources Committee would
hold those hearings.

the Town Council and Community Resources Committee no later than 21 days after the Planning Board
hearing, and for the Community Resources Committee to send a written recommendation to the Town
Council and to submit all materials to the Governance, Organization, and Legislation Committee for
review of clarity, consistency, and actionability within 60 days of the hearing held by the Community
Resources Committee.
