a. Public Forum on Community Preservation Act Appropriations – Charter Sec. 5.6(c)
Jane Wald of the historical commission said that town funding of historically significant property is well
established regardless of the form of ownership. Failure to repair certain things can make things harder to
fix later. Resident Sharon Judge supported funding for the Amherst Women’s Club. Resident Catharine

---

Porter said she is a member of the Amherst Women’s Club and that the club holds many events that are
available to the public. Pelham resident Cathryn Lombardi is the current president of the Amherst
Women’s Club. She noted that the club is open to any woman and urged passage of funding. Robin
Fordham is a member of the historical commission and noted that the preservation of historical properties
is a public benefit. She said the town of Amherst benefitted from the preservation of historic properties.
Barb Bilz, former town recreation director, spoke in favor of pickle ball courts as an affordable recreation

Jeanne Lardner also spoke in favor of the women’s club.
