1. Call to Order
After confirming all participants could hear and be heard, President Griesemer declared the presence of a
quorum, called the meeting to order at 5:33 p.m. and announced audio and video recording by Amherst
Media.
2. Work Session: Rental Permit and Inspection Policies: The Need for Revisions to
Safeguard Health, Safety, and Enhance Neighborhoods and Community
a. Residential Rental Property Bylaw and Rental Permit Registration System
1) Sponsor Memo
Goal is to make sure rental properties are properly maintained.
2) Council Discussion
Devlin Gauthier said it’s important to make sure rental housing, particularly for students, is well-
maintained. Pam asked about incentives for energy saving measures in rental housing. She said that may
be going too far right now. Devlin Gauthier disagreed and said this should not be put off. Taub noted that
student housing is different from other rental housing in town. Lopes said that some student rental
properties are well maintained. De Angelis said that she disagrees with references to well-behaved and
poorly behaved tenants, with students being singled out often as poorly behaved. Hanneke said the
committee working on this is open to other ideas. Steinberg said that students are an important part of a
university community and most are good tenants. He said he is hesitant to generate a new, long bylaw to
address a problem that only affects a small number of tenants. He said he would encourage less, not more,
in drafting a bylaw.
Walker noted that in terms of cultural differences, people have different lifestyles. And because we have
such different types of people living in Amherst, it’s important to be open to different lifestyles. Bahl-

---

Milne said that some people just don’t want students living in their neighborhood, even if there is not a
problem. Schoen said that we should be focused on properties that have problems, not the vast majority of
well-run rental properties with good tenants. Taub said she lives in town with lots of student rentals
around her. She noted that 97 percent of noise and nuisance complaints to Amherst Police are from rental
properties. Devlin Gauthier said that providing incentives for sustainability measures is a good idea.
Pam said that the problem seems to be absentee landlords who are letting their buildings fall apart. She
said students who are renting are not the problem, it’s the absentee landlords. Hanneke said that there is a
proposed 22-page bylaw. Miller said the working draft bylaw is a mixture of bylaws from various
communities. And she added that one goal is to have a bylaw that works in a college community.
3. Adjourn
President Griesemer declared the meeting of the Town Council adjourned at 6:26 p.m.
