3. Hearings
a. 6:30 p.m. Public Hearing: Eversource Petition to install a Joint Pole on
College Street
B. Costello, Eversource representative, spoke about the need for the capacitor bank needed in the area due
to the poles in the area with existing equipment; a new “clean” pole is needed for the capacitor bank.
Pam asked if putting the equipment underground is an option; Costello responded that is not an option for
this equipment.
Steinberg asked if the distance between the existing poles and new pole is 67’; Costello responded that it
is a midspan pole between the two existing poles.
Griesemer asked about shifting equipment to eliminate the need for an additional pole. Costello
responded that this is the best option for the equipment that is needed in the area.
Griesemer asked about the use of the pole for the planned solar field; Costello confirmed that is correct.
Devlin Gauthier commended that in the future it is worth considering pole implications when planning
solar arrays so we can avoid cluttering streets with poles in Town.
MOTION: Griesemer moved, second by Hanneke, to close the public hearing.
VOTED unanimously, 13-0 by roll call to close the public hearing.
