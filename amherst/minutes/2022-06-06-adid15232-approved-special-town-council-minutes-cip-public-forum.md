Town of Amherst
Special Town Council Meeting
Capital Improvement Program Public Forum
Monday, June 6, 2022, 6:30 p.m.
Minutes
Councilors in attendance: Bahl-Milne, Devlin Gauthier, Griesemer, Hanneke, Rooney, Steinberg, Taub
Councilors participating remotely: De Angelis, Lopes, Miller, Pam, Schoen, Walker
Councilors Absent:
Staff in attendance: Town Manager Paul Bockelman, Clerk of the Council Athena O’Keeffe, IT
Director Sean Hannon
Others Participating Remotely: Finance Director Sean Mangano
Note: Pursuant to Chapter 20 of the Acts of 2021, this meeting was conducted via remote means.
Members of the public were able to access the meeting in real time via Zoom, by telephone, via livestream
on Amherst Media, and on TV channel 17.
1. Call to Order
Griesemer announced the presence of a quorum, called the meeting to order at 6:32 p.m., confirmed
members can hear and be heard, and announced audio and video recording.
2. Capital Improvement Program Public Forum – Charter Sec. 5.7(d)
Per Charter Sec. 1.7, a Public Forum shall mean a meeting during which more than one-half of the
meeting time on the agenda is devoted to public comment.
Finance Director Mangano spoke about the Capital Improvement Program, including the following:
 Specific projects included in the plan, including road repairs, fire dept ladder truck, gym floor
at Crocker Farm School, sustainability projects, sidewalks, and escalation reserves
 Funding categories and projected growth and spending projections through FY27
Rani Parker, Amherst resident, spoke about the allocation for sustainability suggesting replacement of gas
equipment to electricity and publicize the change with UMass and Amherst businesses to encourage them
to do likewise. Parker went on to speak about the deficits and tax burden on homeowners and costs
related to climate change events in the coming years.
3. Topics Not Reasonably Anticipated by the Chair 48 Hours in Advance of the Meeting
4. Adjourn
President Griesemer declared the meeting adjourned at 6:45 p.m.
