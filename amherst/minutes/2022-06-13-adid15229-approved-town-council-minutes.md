d. FY23 Operating Budget
Steinberg reported that the Finance Committee submitted a complete report and that he can answer
questions, if any; Steinberg stated the committee voted unanimously to support the Town Manager’s
budget as proposed. Steinberg stated there was a request from the School Committee to increase the
School Committee budget, and there was a significant amount of public comment regarding the police
department budget.
MOTION: Griesemer moved, second by Schoen to adopt Appropriation and Transfer Order FY23-04,
“An Order Appropriating the Town of Amherst FY23 Operating Budget”, as recommended by the
Finance Committee report of June 1, 2022, and shown on page 16 of the motions sheet.
Pam asked if a yes vote would not include the additional funds for the schools. Pam commented this is an
unnecessary attempt at a power struggle, support full funding of arts and technology for the budget and
telling the school committee to take money from something else is not a good option. Protesting the
situation and wish we could vote the money to the school committee.
Bahl-Milne spoke in respect to the school committee’s request for an increase, asked about the reasoning
for not allocating the reimbursement for COVID leave for staff.
Mangano responded that it is a one-time reimbursement for this year and is not available for the coming
year.
Walker spoke in support of Pam’s statement, and read a statement from her child’s art teacher,
announcing to parents that she accepted an offer for a full-time position at another school. Walker stated
the decisions made by the Council affect real people and we need to figure out how to support our
students and our schools.
Miller commented that it is a difficult decision and that is was not the Town Council that decided to
remove the art and technology budget, but a decision by the Schools Superintendent; a way that we can
show support by encouraging the Superintendent to support those programs.
Hanneke stated she will not support a motion to increase the budget above what the Town Manager
proposed; the budget guidelines are difficult for all departments and there are difficult decisions to be
made; the School Superintendent decided that the schools can provide level services with less than full
time art and technology instructors. Hanneke stated the School Committee could decide to fund the full
time positions through a reduction in another area of the school budget. Hanneke stated that if a request
above guidelines from the regional schools, it would be much more difficult.
Rooney echoed the comments from Hanneke, stating that she supports the arts and technology positions
and asked that the School Superintendent to be creative by looking at other opportunities in the school
budget.
Griesemer stated the school decides how to spend their budget, and that the arts and technology cut does
not cut the number of classes, it cuts prep time for instructors.
Pam commented about mental health support for students, spoke in support of the students and teachers.
Bahl-Milne stated it is not a black and white issue, that the school committee asked for a 4.7% increase
and the Town was able to offer a smaller increase to the budget, the students and teachers are suffering
from the trauma they have experienced. It is a small amount, but it sends a message to the school
committee that this is a priority. Bahl-Milne asked if there is other state aid available to provide that
support.
