2. Public Forum on Appropriations Outside the Annual Budget: Centennial Water Treatment
Facility Funding – Charter Sec. 5.6
Per Charter Sec. 1.7, a Public Forum shall mean a meeting during which more than one-half of
the meeting time on the agenda is devoted to public comment.
Finance Director Sean Mangano spoke about the following:
 Council authorization of $11M to replace the Centennial Water Treatment Facility in July 2020
 New cost estimates bring the project cost to $18M
 To help mitigate the cost increases, the Town applied for a MassWorks grant and applied for
financing through the state’s drinking water revolving fund
 The program includes loan forgiveness and a special financing rate
 The Governor included a $3.5M MassWorks allocation in his FORWARD bill but that has been
delayed and approval is uncertain
 The Town’s share after loan forgiveness would be about $15M

---

 The Town must authorize funding for the entire program to enter the program
 Debt would be supported by water rates and is outside the Town’s debt limit
No public comment.
