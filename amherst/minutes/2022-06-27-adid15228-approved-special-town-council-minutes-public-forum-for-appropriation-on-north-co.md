Town of Amherst
Special Town Council Meeting
Public Forum on Appropriation for North Common Improvements
Monday, June 27, 2022, 6:30 p.m.
Virtual Public Access
Minutes
Councilors in attendance: Bahl-Milne, Devlin Gauthier, Griesemer, Hanneke, Steinberg, Taub,
Councilors participating remotely: De Angelis, Lopes (joined at 6:38 pm), Miller, Pam, Schoen, Walker
Councilors Absent: Rooney
Staff in attendance: Town Manager Paul Bockelman, Clerk of the Council Athena O’Keeffe,
Others Participating Remotely: Finance Director Sean Mangano, Assistant Town Manager David Ziomek
Note: Pursuant to Chapter 20 of the Acts of 2021, this meeting was conducted via remote means.
Members of the public were able to access the meeting in real time via Zoom, by telephone, via livestream
on Amherst Media, and on TV channel 17.
1. Call to Order
Griesemer announced the presence of a quorum, called the meeting to order at 6:30 p.m., confirmed
members can hear and be heard, and announced audio and video recording.
2. Public Forum on Appropriations Outside the Annual Budget: North Common
Improvements – Charter Sec. 5.6
Per Charter Sec. 1.7, a Public Forum shall mean a meeting during which more than one-half of
the meeting time on the agenda is devoted to public comment.
Assistant Town Manager Ziomek spoke about the Land Water Conservation Fund grant, project status,
construction date of 2023, expected design and future public participation opportunities.
Martha Hanner, Amherst resident, asked if there will still be accessible parking when the parking is
rearranged.
Ziomek responded that there will be ADA accessible parking spaces on Boltwood Ave.
3. Topics Not Reasonably Anticipated by the Chair 48 Hours in Advance of the Meeting
4. Adjourn
Griesemer declared the meeting adjourned at 6:45 p.m.
