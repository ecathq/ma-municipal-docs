1. Call to Order
Griesemer announced the presence of a quorum, called the meeting to order at 6:30 p.m., confirmed
members can hear and be heard, and announced audio and video recording.
2. Public Forum on Appropriations Outside the Annual Budget: Community Preservation Act
Borrowing Authorization for Amherst Regional High School Track and Field – Charter Sec.
5.6
Finance Director Mangano spoke about the Community Preservation Act Committee’s
recommendation to borrow funds for the Regional High School Track and Field, to supplement
other funding sources to finance the project. Mangano spoke about other funding sources and
pointed out that there are restrictions on uses for CPA funding, and that CPA funds would not be
used for the artificial turf.
Public Comment:
Kathleen Anderson, Amherst resident, asked why the track is being reoriented north-south,
commenting that it would be difficult for students in terms of the sun.
Assistant Town Manager Ziomek spoke about the project and studies the determine the best
orientation for the track and fields; currently players are facing the sun while playing at sunset.

---

Ziomek also spoke about the track being expanded from a 6 to an 8-lane track to accommodate
track and field tournaments.
