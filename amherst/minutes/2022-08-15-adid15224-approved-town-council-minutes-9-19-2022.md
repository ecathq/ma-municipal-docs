7. Presentation and Discussion
a. July 5, 2022 Youth and Police Interaction
Bockelman, Young, and Livingstone were asked to speak by President Griesemer.
Bockelman introduced Dr. Pamela Nolan Young as the recently appointed Diversity, Equity, and
Inclusion (DEI) Director and thanked her for her work thus far on behalf of the residents of the
Town of Amherst. He thanked her for her excellent analysis and legal frameworks. Bockelman
spoke briefly about the creation of a Resident Oversight Board and Community Responders for
Equity, Safety, and Service (CRESS).
Young reviewed the Police call on July 5, 2022. She gave an overview of the incident. Young
stated that the Police Officer erroneously told one of the minors that they (the minors involved)
did not have rights. Young reviewed the steps taken by the Amherst Police Department (APD)
and the Town Manager to address this incident. Young detailed the steps taken by the Human
Rights Commission and the members of the Community Safety Social Justice Committee
(CSSJC).
Young detailed the steps taken by the APD. Young detailed the investigation of the incident as
reported and summarized by the APD. Young explained that APD officers are subject to union
policies. Young reviewed her assessment of the APD officer language and explained how it
informed her report for this meeting. Young did not find that the behavior of the police officer
was an abuse of power.
Young read a quote from James Baldwin. Young quoted Nelson Mandela.
Young welcomed questions from the Town Councilors.
Griesemer asked Bockelman for comment. He had none at this time.
Griesemer asked Livingstone for comment.
Livingstone detailed his conversation with the officers involved in the July 5, 2022 incident.
Officer Martins had expressed remorse and regret for the statement made on the video.
Livingstone further explained that the incident ended cordially. Livingstone explained why the
minors were not allowed to leave on their own. All of the minors were underage and out past a
state-mandated driving curfew for drivers with provisional driving permits. Livingstone
mentioned further actions taken by the APD that would occur after this meeting.
Griesemer added that on July 18, 2022, Taub asked for a report on the investigation.
Griesemer opened the floor to Councilor questions.
Pam thanked Young for the clear and informative report.
