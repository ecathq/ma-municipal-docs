Town of Amherst
Special Town Council Meeting – Public Forum to Address the Master Plan
Monday, September 19, 2022 6:00 p.m.
Town Room, Town Hall
Minutes
Councilors in attendance: Griesemer, Hanneke, Steinberg

Rooney, Schoen, Taub, Walker
Councilors Absent: None
Staff in attendance: Town Manager Paul Bockelman, Clerk of the Council O’Keeffe
Others Participating Remotely: Assistant Town Manager Ziomek, Planning Director Brestrup
Note: Pursuant to Chapter 20 of the Acts of 2021 extended by Chapter 22 of the Acts of 2022, this
meeting was conducted via remote means. Members of the public were able to access the meeting in real
time via Zoom, by telephone, via livestream on Amherst Media, and on TV channel 17.
1. Call to Order
Griesemer announced the presence of a quorum, called the meeting to order at 6:08 p.m., confirmed
members can hear and be heard, and announced audio and video recording.
2. Announcements
3. Public Forum to Address the Master Plan – Charter Sec. 2.13
The public forum shall present the public with context, alert the public to upcoming issues,
and collect feedback from the public.
Public Comment:
Steve Dunn, part of the North Amherst Master Plan Study Group, spoke about what the group did in
terms of studying the Master Plan, discussions about the Master Plan, and a report that was produced as a
result of the group’s meetings. Dunn suggested a village design guideline and other ideas to implement
the Master Plan in neighborhoods.
Cathy Axelson-Berry, Amherst resident, spoke about a lack of an implementation committee, and the
perception that the preservation of the small-town character and neighborhoods, and suggested an
implementation committee.
Cinda Jones, Amherst resident, thanked the District One Neighborhood Association and praised their
process of studying the Master Plan.

---

Eve Vogel, Amherst resident, spoke about the DONA group’s process and why North Amherst could be
considered a microcosm of the Town and their ideas for North Amherst could be applied to the Town as a
whole. Vogel also spoke about transportation and affordable housing needs.
Janet Keller, Amherst resident, also spoke about the DONA master plan study group, and spoke about the
need for net-zero building for private development.
Laura Draucker, Amherst resident, stated it is disconcerting that the public forum has been focused on a
small neighborhood group and not on all residents.
Hilda Greenbaum, Amherst resident, spoke about issues and needs in North Amherst.
Meg Gage, Amherst resident, spoke about a draft transportation plan, and about achieving goals without
putting pressure on staff.
4. Topics Not Reasonably Anticipated 48 Hours in Advance of the Meeting
5. Adjourn
President Griesemer declared the meeting adjourned at 6:30 p.m.

Clerk of the Council O’Keeffe
