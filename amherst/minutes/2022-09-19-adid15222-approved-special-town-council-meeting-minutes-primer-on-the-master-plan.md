1. Call to Order
Griesemer announced the presence of a quorum, called the meeting to order at 5:30 p.m., confirmed
members can hear and be heard, and announced audio and video recording.
2. Announcements
3. Presentations and Discussion
a. Primer on the Master Plan – Planning Director Christine Brestrup
Planning Director Brestrup spoke about the Master Plan, including:
 What the Master Plan is, what it does and does not do
 How the Master Plan relates to the Zoning Bylaw
 What the law says about Master Plans; MGL Ch 41, Sec 81D
 How Amherst’s Master Plan was created, history of the Master Plan development and
planning process, public participation, cost
 What is contained in the Master Plan and how it is organized
 What the Master Plan says about demographics & housing, natural & cultural
resources, transportation & circulation, and how the Master Plan is to be
implemented
 What has been accomplished
 How the Town achieves more affordable housing

 Recently completed affordable housing projects
 The Amherst Municipal Affordable Housing Trust
 What the Town is continuing to work in as a community
