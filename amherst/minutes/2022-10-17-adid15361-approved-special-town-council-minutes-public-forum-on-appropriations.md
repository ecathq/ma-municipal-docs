Taub, Steinberg, Walker

Note: Pursuant to Chapter 20 of the Acts of 2021 extended by Chapter 22 of the Acts of 2022, this
meeting was conducted via remote means. Members of the public were able to access the meeting in real
time via Zoom, by telephone, and via livestream on Amherst Media.
1. Call to Order
Griesemer announced the presence of a quorum, called the meeting to order at 6:30 p.m., confirmed
members can hear and be heard, and announced audio and video recording.
2. Presentations and Discussion:
a. FY23 Supplemental Budget Appropriations:
(1) FY23 Operating Budget
(2) AHRS Track and Fields
(3) Roads and Sidewalks
b. Transfers from General Stabilization Fund to Capital Stabilization Fund
c. Transfers from Free Cash to Capital and Reparations Stabilization Funds
d. Creation of a Capital Stabilization Fund
Mangano spoke briefly about the financial orders and rationale for the requested appropriations and
transfers. 6:48
3. Public Forum on Appropriations Outside the Annual Budget – Charter Sec. 5.6
Public Comment:

Toni Cunningham, Amherst resident, asked about the Capital Stabilization Fund, if it will be a borrowing
and if it will come from the capital budget.
Mangano responded that the transfers to stabilization funds will come from free cash and will not be
borrowing.
Steinberg called the Finance Committee to order at 6:41 p.m. and confirmed Hegner can hear and be
heard.
Cunningham asked about borrowing for the track and field project, and if funds will come from the
capital stabilization fund.
Mangano spoke about the 3 or 4 funding sources for the track and field project; the current order is not for
a borrowing authorization and will be spent via bills coming directly to the Town or costs that will be
reimbursed to the regional schools for costs associated with field rehabilitation.
No further public questions or comments.
4. Topics Not Reasonably Anticipated by the Chair 48 Hours in Advance of the Meeting
5. Adjourn
Steinberg declared the Finance Committee meeting adjourned at 6:48 p.m.
The meeting was adjourned at 6:48 p.m.
