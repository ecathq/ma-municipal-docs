Town of Amherst
Special Town Council Meeting – Town Manager Evaluation Reading Period
Monday, November 7, 2022
5:00 p.m.
Town Hall, Town Room
Minutes
Councilors in attendance: Bahl-Milne, De Angelis, Devlin Gauthier, Griesemer, Hanneke, Lopes, Miller,
Rooney, Schoen, Steinberg, Taub
Councilors participating remotely: Pam, Walker (joined at 5:07 p.m.)
Councilors Absent: None
Staff in attendance: Town Manager Bockelman, Clerk of the Council O’Keeffe
Note: Pursuant to Chapter 20 of the Acts of 2021 extended by Chapter 22 of the Acts of 2022, this
meeting was conducted via in person and remote means. Members of the public were able to access the
meeting in real time in person and via Zoom, by telephone, and via livestream on Amherst Media.
1. Call to Order
Griesemer announced the presence of a quorum, called the meeting to order at 6:48 p.m., confirmed
members can hear and be heard, and announced audio and video recording.
2. Announcements
Griesemer announced there will not be a public comment at this meeting, and there will be public
comment period at the 8:00 p.m. regular Town Council meeting.
3. Presentations and Discussion
a. Town Manager Performance Evaluation
Griesemer announced Town Council members’ individual evaluations have been published on the Town
Website and subsequently made available to Councilors on paper and electronically.
The Council spent the remainder of the meeting reading evaluation materials.
4. Topics Not Anticipated by the Chair 48 Hours in Advance of the Meeting
5. Adjourn
Griesemer declared the meeting adjourned at 6:50 p.m.
Respectfully submitted,
Clerk of the Council O’Keeffe
