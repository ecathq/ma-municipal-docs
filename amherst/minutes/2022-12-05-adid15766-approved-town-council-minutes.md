MOTION: Griesemer moved, second by Devlin Gauthier, based on the submission of new information, I
move as a member of the non-prevailing side to reconsider the Town Council’s vote of November 21,
2022 on the Appropriation and Transfer Order FY23-05C: An Order Appropriating Funds for a Portion of
the Town of Amherst Capital Program – School Track and Field Rehabilitation.
Griesemer spoke about the letter from Amherst Schools Superintendent Morris and Athletic Director
Dawson, the order of votes and potential amendments, and the resolution that Councilor Miller submitted
regarding PFAS.
Superintendent Morris and Athletic Director Dawson spoke about feedback from Councilors who
expressed an interest in a natural grass field and would like Councilors to make a concrete commitment to
creating better playing surfaces. They suggested specific commitments from the Town Council to address
the fields and appreciated the level of commitment from the Council in exploring the issue.
Dawson thanked the students who came to speak at the meeting tonight.
Bockelman highlighted points in the memo response from the Town Manager, Assistant Town Manager,
and Finance Director regarding Dr. Morris and Director Dawson’s letter.
VOTED 9-3, with 1 abstention by roll call, based on the submission of new information, I move as a
member of the non-prevailing side to reconsider the Town Council’s vote of November 21, 2022 on the
Appropriation and Transfer Order FY23-05C: An Order Appropriating Funds for a Portion of the Town
of Amherst Capital Program – School Track and Field Rehabilitation.
Aye: Bahl-Milne, De Angelis, Devlin Gauthier, Griesemer, Hanneke, Lopes, Miller, Steinberg, Taub
Nay: Pam, Rooney, Walker
Present (abstain): Schoen
Absent: None
MOTION ON THE FLOOR: To adopt appropriation and transfer order FY23-05C: An Order
Appropriating Funds for a Portion of the Town of Amherst Capital Program – School Track and Field
Rehabilitation, recommended by Finance Committee on October 17, 2022, and shown on page 6 of the
November 21, 2022 Draft Motions sheet.
Walker asked about how information is decided to be “new” in terms of motions for reconsideration.
Miller supported Walker’s concerns about reconsideration.
MOTION: Miller moved, second by Schoen, to amend the motion by adding the words “2 or”
Miller spoke about the amendment to the order and the resolution she submitted to the Council regarding
safety of the ARHS athletic complex.
Hanneke asked about the impact of the amendment.
Mangano responded that there are not concerns about the amendment.

Steinberg stated he hopes the Regional School Committee takes the resolution seriously and considers the
important questions raised by the community regarding the fields, and that it is up to the Regional School
Committee to consider the information and do a thorough study before making a knowledgeable decision.
Devlin Gauthier spoke about concerns with some of the language in the resolution, asking if the
resolution asks an elected body to reconsider their decision through a non-binding resolution.
Schoen spoke in support of the resolution and commented on the issue of maintenance of grass fields.
Bahl-Milne commented that it is important to talk about PFAS with respect to non-essential consumer
products, that some of the claims made about the synthetic turf were not vetted by independent scientific
review. She stated she is not ready to vote on the resolution before knowing the impact on the booster’s
fundraising efforts.

for the current item.

Walker
Nay: Bahl-Milne, Rooney
Present (abstain): None

Bahl-Milne invoked her Charter 2.10(c) right to postpone to the next Council meeting, stating these
decisions have consequences on many people, she is in favor of looking at PFAS in consumer products,
but consumer products does not include athletic fields, and the vote might create fear among people who
might make donations.

MOTION: Walker moved, second by Pam, that the Town issue a statement to the SC and students
indicating our commitment to addressing the entire athletic complex. Refer this motion immediately to
the finance committee to determine:
a) A phased approach to ensure the repair and maintenance of all fields located at the middle and high
school
b) Allocate/identify funding within the FY24 budget to commence the repair of at least one of the
athletic fields located at the high school
c) Funding required for the town to hire someone to oversee the ongoing maintenance of these and all
town (owned) fields. This person would coordinate with key stakeholders including but not limited to
DPW workers, the Athletic Director and the Rec department.
Hanneke questioned who would write the statement, and if the second point is adoption of a policy. She
spoke about referring to the Finance Committee to determine what it would take to determine the needs of
the motion.
Walker spoke to the motion, stating that there is more needed than addressing this field and the turf issue,
considering the implications and what is needed to repair the fields, and added that even though the Town
does not own the fields, the Town is in charge of maintenance.
Pam spoke in support of the motion.
Miller spoke in support of the motion and suggested separating the two items.
Devlin Gauthier spoke about information already known in the Weston and Sampson report, that the first
part is not within the purview of the Finance Committee, and the motion appears to be more of a
declarative statement than an intent to explore.
Bockelman questioned the relationship between the motion and the Financial Guidelines, stated that the
Council cannot make an appropriation without a request by the Town Manager.
Mangano added the phased approach is the School Committee’s job.
Steinberg agreed with the comments from Bockelman and Mangano and expressed concern with the
Council’s interaction with the School Committee in terms of guidance or a request to explore the issue.
Hanneke stated the phased approach to repair and maintenance and allocation of funding for field repair
are not issues within the Council’s purview. The region would request funding and determine a phased
approach. She added that the greater financial burden the Town takes on could impact the other member
towns’ commitment to sharing costs.
Walker spoke about the intent of the motion and modifications she would be willing to make.
Councilors discussed the motion, including support of the idea but not the exact wording.
MOTION: Hanneke moved, second by De Angelis, to call the previous question.
VOTED 9-4, by roll call, to call the previous question.
Aye: De Angelis, Devlin Gauthier, Griesemer, Hanneke, Lopes, Rooney, Schoen, Steinberg, Taub
Nay: Bahl-Milne, Miller, Pam, Walker
