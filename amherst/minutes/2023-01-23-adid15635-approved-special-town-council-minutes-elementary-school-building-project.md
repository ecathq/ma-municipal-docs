a. Elementary School Building Project Presentation
(1) Building Design
Key points: education first, sustainable, net-zero energy design, focus on costs

Donna DiNisco presented to the joint meeting. Total square footage has not
changed. Finalized spatial relationships & adjacencies have been accounted for.
Proposed building location allows for continuous building use during building,
Proposed first, second, and third floor plans were presented with a brief
description of decisions on grade-level classroom grouping, flexible learning and
collaboration spaces, special education spaces, and administrative and all-school
spaces.
DiNisco shared a virtual walk-through of inside and outside spaces along with
details about design decisions associated with natural light, style/design,
consideration of creating quiet space, and other
The proposed building will be Amherst first net-zero building utilizing ground
source heat pumps, on-site photovoltaics, electric vehicle charging station
infrastructure, enhanced indoor air quality, and other energy efficient strategies.

b. Council Discussion
Hegner asked about demolition and waste management costs which are included in the
construction amount.
Hanneke asked about MSBA estimated grant number might be, and if the estimate could
be reduced. Devlin Gauthier asked when the final MSBA review would be conducted.
Margaret Wood confirmed that would take place at the time of schematic drawing
submission, at the earliest mid-March. Final numbers would be confirmed when
construction bids are received.

Bockelman reminded the special meeting that the MSBA estimates are the project team
estimates and not those confirmed by MSBA.
Rooney thanked the building committee for the beautiful design and for the cost estimate
coming in under $100,000,000. Rooney also asked for details on how soft
cost/furnishings being under 25%. Margaret Wood suggested that the furnishings and
equipment and technology numbers could increase.
Mangano confirmed the feasibility study will be funded through the normal process.
Steinberg talked about the building site itself, and potential costs for wetlands work and
other site preparation and also asked how unanticipated costs are accounted for. DiNisco
confirmed site development costs are included in the construction costs. Change orders
on DiNisco projects historically have been approximately 1%.
Pam asked about elevators between floors which were confirmed as a code requirement.
Pam also asked about security systems which are also part of the estimated costs.
Schoen directed people to the elementary school building committee website
https://www.amherstma.gov/3539/Elementary-School-Building-Committee. There is
also a project website at https://www.amherst-school-project.com/.
