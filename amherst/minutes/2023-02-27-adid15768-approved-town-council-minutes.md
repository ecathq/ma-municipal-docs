d. Water and Sewer Line Ownership in Proposed Water and Sewer Regulations:
Request to Town Manager
Devlin Gauthier reported the Town Services and Outreach Committee recommendation was to make a
change in ownership of the water and sewer lines automatic in two years.
Steinberg reported the Finance Committee recommendation was included in the written report, and
summarized that the committee did not recommend an automatic change but a reevaluation in two years
with consideration of information that is not yet know in order to make a knowledgeable decision.
Steinberg commented that the discussion in committees have not addressed some issues identified by a
constituent, including equity regarding residents on private ways, apartments, and residents of
neighboring towns with water from Amherst. Some of those people would pay higher water rates due to
an automatic change but would not benefit. Steinberg stated those issues should be considered before a
change in ownership were to take place.
Devlin Gauthier disagreed with Steinberg on his points, stating the Council should move forward with the
regulations and commit to making the change in the future and that it would be unsafe to assume that a
future Council would bring it up.
Bahl-Milne could be additional costs that we don’t know. Assumption that when the pipe is in public
property it is being damaged due to utility work,

Taub and Pam spoke in favor of the ownership change.
Hanneke stated she does not support an automatic change, that one or two years may not be enough time,
and that there may be residents who might postpone repairs until the ownership changes. If a future
Council decides against an automatic change going into effect, the rates would have increased for no
reason. Hanneke suggested that when the Council adopts regulations, including a request for the Town

afford.

An Act authorizing extending local voting rights for lawful permanent residents residing in

Section 1. Notwithstanding the provision of section one of M.G.L. Chapter Fifty-One, or any

i. Proposed Amendments to Town Council Rules of Procedure: Rules 1.4, 2.1.d.3,
2.1.d.4, 10.6, appendix A – Consent Agenda
VOTED via consent agenda to adopt the following revisions to the Town Council Rules of Procedure:
 In Rule 1.4, deleting the numeral “11” and replacing it with the numeral “12”;
 In Rule 2.1.d.3, deleting the word “brief”;
 In Rule 2.1.d.4, deleing the word “brief”;
 In Rule 10.6, deleting the section label “a.” and deleting section b. in its entirety; and
 In Appendix A, adding a new bullet point after the Healthy Balance sectionthat reads
“Lead with Curiosity: Replace judgements and automatic reactions with curiosity and
seeking to understand the different perspectives before responding.”
