1. Call to Order
Griesemer declared the presence of a quorum, called the meeting to order at 5:31 p.m., confirmed
Councilors could hear and be heard, and announced audio and video recording.
Steinberg declared the presence of a quorum of the Finance Committee, called the meeting to order at
5:33 p.m. and confirmed non-voting members could hear and be heard.
2. Presentations and Discussion:
a. Centennial Water Treatment Facility Funding
(1) Council Order FY24-09A: An Order Approving and Authorizing Borrowing
to Fund Capital Projects – Bond Authorization
Finance Director Mangano spoke about the bids that came in for the project that were higher than the
previous borrowing authorization for $18M. The town has a State Revolving Fund loan for the project
that will allow the Town to obtain a loan at a lower rate of 1.5% and to receive 19.8% in loan forgiveness.
3. Public Forum – Charter Sec. 5.6
There were no public comments.
