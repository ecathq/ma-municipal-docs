e. Financial Orders
1) Order FY23-16A: An Order Appropriating from Free Cash to the Cannabis
Impact Special Revenue Fund
MOTION: Griesemer moved, second by Hanneke, in accordance with Charter Sec. 5.6, having been
reviewed by the Finance Committee, a Public Forum held on April 24, 2023, notice of which was posted
for a minimum of 10 days on April 13, 2023, to adopt Council Order FY23-16A: An Order Appropriating
from Free Cash to the Cannabis Impact Special Revenue Fund, as shown on p. 18.
Rooney asked if there have been discussions about the cannabis impact funds being used for reparations.
Mangano spoke about the lack of a precedent for that suggestion and clarified that an amount equal to the
cannabis revenue has been transferred to the reparations fund. Mangano spoke about the appropriate uses
for the cannabis impact fund, and that additional guidance is being sought to determine appropriate uses.
Bockelman added that this is a developing field, and towns are in the process of determining what uses
are appropriate.
Pam asked how long the funds will be available, and possible youth programming uses.
Mangano stated incoming funds will dwindle over the next few years.
Steinberg stated the cannabis impact funds can probably not be used for the reparations fund, as the funds
are to be used to mitigate the effects of legalization.
Miller spoke in support of the motion.
VOTED unanimously, 13-0 by roll call, in accordance with Charter Sec. 5.6, having been reviewed by
the Finance Committee, a Public Forum held on April 24, 2023, notice of which was posted for a

6) Order FY23-05D: An Order Appropriating Funds for a Portion of the Town
of Amherst Capital Program – Demolition of the Gas Station located at 24
Montague Road and site improvements
MOTION: Griesemer moved, second by Hanneke, in accordance with Charter Sec. 5.6, having been
reviewed by the Finance Committee, a Public Forum held on April 24, 2023, notice of which was posted
for a minimum of 10 days on April 13, 2023, to adopt Council Order FY23-05D: An Order Appropriating
Funds for a Portion of the Town of Amherst Capital Program – Demolition of the Gas Station located at
24 Montague Road and site improvements, as shown on p. 23.
Walker asked about the timing for the order, why the request is coming at this point.
Mooring spoke about the projected completion date for the North Amherst Library in August, and the
intent is to have the gas station and garage removed by the completion date.
Rooney asked about the traffic at the intersection, and that the amendment to this order seemed to be easy
in this situation when it was not when the Council was considering amending the borrowing and
appropriation order for the school building project.
Mooring stated there will be a traffic and intersection improvement project in the future, but not at this
time.
Schoen spoke about the site plan for the project.
Pam spoke in favor of the motion, and the hope that there will be plantings.
Walker asked if the library could not open on the scheduled date if demolition of the gas station were
delayed.
Mooring added that a portion of the property will continue to be rented to the towing company located
adjacent to the property, which will bring in revenue.
VOTED 12-1 by roll call, in accordance with Charter Sec. 5.6, having been reviewed by the Finance
Committee, a Public Forum held on April 24, 2023, notice of which was posted for a minimum of 10 days
on April 13, 2023, to adopt Council Order FY23-05D: An Order Appropriating Funds for a Portion of the
Town of Amherst Capital Program – Demolition of the Gas Station located at 24 Montague Road and site
improvements, as shown on p. 23.

g. Postpone Indefinitely Referral to Community Resources Committee Regarding
Definition of Apartments in the Zoning Bylaw
MOTION: Griesemer moved, second by Hanneke, to discharge the CRC and postpone indefinitely the
portion of the following referral made on June 28, 2021, partially reported back to Council on September
14, 2021, and partially acted on by the Council on October 18, 2022, that relates to the definition of
Apartments in the Zoning Bylaw:
MOVED: To refer the proposed amendments to Zoning Bylaw Section 3.323 Apartments, to
the Planning Board and the Community Resources Committee for hearings held no later than
September 1, 2021, and for a written recommendation and an explanation as to whether the
proposed bylaw is not inconsistent with the Master Plan from the Planning Board to the Town
Council and to the Community Resources Committee no later than 21 days after the Planning
Board hearing, and for the Community Resources Committee to send a written
recommendation to the Town Council and to submit all materials to the Governance,
Organization, and Legislation Committee for review of clarity, consistency, and actionability
within 60 days of the hearing held by the Community Resources Committee.
Hanneke spoke about her recommendation as Chair of the Community Resources Committee to discharge
the committee from this referral.
VOTED unanimously, 13-0 by roll call, to discharge the CRC and postpone indefinitely the portion of
the following referral made on June 28, 2021, partially reported back to Council on September 14, 2021,
and partially acted on by the Council on October 18, 2022, that relates to the definition of Apartments in
the Zoning Bylaw:
MOVED: To refer the proposed amendments to Zoning Bylaw Section 3.323 Apartments, to
the Planning Board and the Community Resources Committee for hearings held no later than
September 1, 2021, and for a written recommendation and an explanation as to whether the
proposed bylaw is not inconsistent with the Master Plan from the Planning Board to the Town
Council and to the Community Resources Committee no later than 21 days after the Planning
Board hearing, and for the Community Resources Committee to send a written
