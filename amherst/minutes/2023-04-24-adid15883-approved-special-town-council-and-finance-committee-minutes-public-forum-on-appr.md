3. Public Forum – Charter Sec. 5.6
Toni Cunningham, resident,
Maria Kopicki, resident, asked why the $5M reuse water project was halted.
DPW Superintendent Mooring responded that the project was halted due to UMass pursuing geothermal
heating rather than using the central heating plant.
Toni Cunningham, resident, spoke in support of using capital funds rather than free cash for the gas
station demolition project, and commented about the process for amending that financial order when the
financial order for the school building project was not able to be amended by the Town Council.
Cunningham asked about the $300,000 for engineering fees. Cunningham asked why the Town is not
being reimbursed for the engineering fees when UMass is not moving forward with the project.
Mooring spoke about the engineering work that was done, that the work that was done will be used in the
future despite UMass not moving forward with their project. The Town will likely proceed with a water
reuse project in the future, so that reuse water can be used instead of potable water for irrigation and other
non-household uses. The state is pushing for towns to use more reuse water, and that will be addressed in
the future.
