Finance Director Mangano and Town Manager Bockelman presented the Town Manager’s FY24 budget.

Rooney asked about the budget increase of 3%, and limit on increases to 2.5%.
Mangano explained that the additional .5% increase is coming from non-tax sources.
Pam asked about the funds for tree planting, removal, and care.
Mangano responded that in past years, the line item did not include planting and this year additional funds
were added for planting.
Bockelman added that the Tree Warden has expressed there are challenges due to invasive species which
threaten existing trees and that some trees may be treated rather than removed, and that the Town will try
to maintain the existing canopy.
b. Proposed Water & Sewer Rates
Mangano gave a brief presentation about the proposed water and sewer rates.
VOTED via consent agenda to refer the Town Manager’s May 1, 2023 recommendation to increase water
rates from $4.75/100cf to $5.00/100cf, and sewer rates from $5.20/100cf. to $5.50/100cf to the Finance
Committee, for a report and recommendation to the Council by May 15, 2023.
Hanneke and Griesemer asked if the water and sewer regulations and wastewater management plan will
need to be updated when the new rates are set.
O’Keeffe stated the required notices will be published in order to amend the regulations with new rates.
c. Proposed Council Order FY24-11: An Order Approving the Acceptance of Optional
Tax Exemptions for FY2024
VOTED via consent agenda to refer Council Order FY24-11: An Order Approving the Acceptance of
Optional Tax Exemptions for FY2024 to the Finance Committee, for review and recommendation to the
Town Council by May 15, 2023.
Griesemer and Rooney asked about the amounts and criteria used for tax exemptions.
Mangano responded that the Town offers the maximum exemptions allowed by the state and that
eligibility criteria is posted on the Town website.

12. Town Manager Report
Bockelman spoke about the following:
• Assistant DEI Director Jennifer Moyston’s 2023 3rd Hampshire District Black Excellence on the
Hill Award
• Cup o’ Joe with the Town Manager
• Seeking members to fill vacancies on boards and committees
• The State has set May 11 as the official end to the state of emergency due to the pandemic
• The front steps of Town Hall will be repaired; the Main Street entrance will be open during
repairs
• Upcoming commencement dates at Amherst College, Hampshire College, and UMass
• Successful Sustainability Festival on April 22
• Tree evaluation
• Continuing work on Kellogg Avenue; work will begin on Pomeroy and West Pomeroy Lanes
• Election day tomorrow
Hanneke asked about the strategic partnership discussions.
Bockelman responded that he hopes to have an update in the next several weeks.
Rooney asked about development of the Resident Oversight Board and expansion for the Boltwood
garage.
Bockelman stated the engineer’s report is on the way, and that the DEI department is putting out a request
for proposals for consulting services for establishing the board.
Steinberg asked if there is a report on the bike share program, and if the Town will have a program this
year.
Bockelman stated the company that ran the program has declared bankruptcy, and the City of
Northampton is seeking legal guidance in the hopes that the program can be maintained this year and in
the future.
