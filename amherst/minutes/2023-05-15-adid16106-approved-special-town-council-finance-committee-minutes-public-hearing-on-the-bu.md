1. Call to Order
Griesemer declared the presence of a quorum, called the meeting of the Town Council to order at 5:33
p.m., confirmed Councilors could hear and be heard, and announced audio and video recording.
Steinberg declared the presence of a quorum, called the meeting of the Finance Committee to order at
5:35 p.m., and confirmed non-voting members could hear and be heard.
2. Hearing: Public Hearing on the FY2024 Town Budget – Charter Sec. 5.5(a)
Steinberg explained the budgetary process and the Finance Committee’s role in reviewing the budget. He
also stated that a copy of the budget can be found on the Town website.
Mangano presented a summary and highlights of the FY2024 budget.
Pam expressed concern about the lack of a salary increase for elected officials. She commented that a
salary increase would help to recruit a diverse and representative body of elected officials.
Griesemer noted the Finance Committee will discuss Councilor stipends, dependent care, and health
insurance at an upcoming meeting.
a. Public Comment

---

Allegra Clark requested that the Town Council take action on the Amherst School Committee budget that
includes $84,000 in additional funds. She also requested that the Town fund Crest instead of the Police
Department.
Zoe Crabtree requested that the Town move funds out of the Amherst Police Department Budget and into
an increased Crest budget. She spoke in favor of operationalizing the Town’s climate action plan but
criticized the funds allocated for electric police cars. Crabtree urged the Council to embrace the CSWG’s
full recommendations and to advance the Town’s commitment to diversity, equity, and inclusion.
Lauren Mills requested that the Town implement youth empowerment programs.
Hanneke joined the meeting at 5:55 p.m.
3. Topics Not Reasonably Anticipated by the Chair 48 Hours in Advance of the Meeting -
None
4. Adjourn
Finance Committee motion: Griesemer moved, seconded by Devlin Gauthier, to close the public
hearing at 6:00 p.m.
Finance Committee voted 4-0 by roll call, with 1 member absent (Schoen), and two non-voting
members in support, to close the hearing at 6:00 p.m.
Griesemer declared the Special Town Council meeting adjourned at 6:01 p.m.
Steinberg declared the Finance Committee meeting adjourned at 6:01 p.m.
