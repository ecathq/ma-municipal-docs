1. Call to Order
Griesemer declared the presence of a quorum, called the meeting of the Town Council to order at 6:31
PM and announced audio and video recording. She then verified that all council members present can
hear and be heard and reviewed the meeting agenda.
Steinberg declared the presence of a quorum and called the meeting of the Finance Committee to order at
6:32 PM.
2. Public Forum on Council Order FY24-07B An Order Appropriating the FY2024
Community Preservation Act Budget as required under M.G.L. Chapter 44B – Charter Sec.
5.6
Mangano explained the need for the order, stating that the action will move reserves from a FY23 fund to
an FY24 fund.
Steinberg reported the Finance Committee recommended Town Council approval of Order FY24-07B.
a. Public Comment
Lauren Mills, resident, advocated for the creation of a cultural youth community center and suggested
financing its creation with CPA reserves.
No other public comment.
3. Topics Not Reasonably Anticipated by the Chair 48 Hours in Advance of the Meeting –
None
4. Adjourn
Steinberg declared the meeting of the Finance Committee adjourned at 6:47 p.m.
