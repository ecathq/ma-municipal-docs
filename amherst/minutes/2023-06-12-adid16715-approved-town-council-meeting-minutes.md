1. Call to Order
Griesemer declared the presence of a quorum, called the meeting of the Town Council to order at 6:48
p.m. and announced audio and video recording. She then verified that all council members present can
hear and be heard and reviewed the meeting agenda.
2. Announcements
Griesemer announced the upcoming Town Council meetings and committee meetings. She then
announced the Juneteenth events beginning on Saturday, June 17 at 10 a.m.
3. Hearings – None
4. General Public Comment
Vincent O’Connor, resident, requested that the Town pass a bylaw prohibiting unshielded lighting. He
stated that unshielded lighting is environmentally wasteful and can be distracting to drivers.
Birdy Newman, resident, urged the Council to vote “no” on the FY24 budget due to its allocation of
additional funds to the police.
Lauren Mills, resident, stated the importance of having affirming community spaces for BIPOC youth.
She encouraged the Council to look into the Eagle Eye Institute as a positive example.
Allegra Clark, resident, urged the Council to vote “no” on the FY24 budget due its allocated amounts to
schools and the police.
Vira Cage, resident, stated that the Black Business Association will host a community Juneteenth
celebration on June 19th. Cage praised the recent Race Amity Day celebration. Cage also expressed
concern about different accessibility standards for Town businesses, specifically at the Drake and Hazel’s
Blue Lagoon.
Pat Ononibaku, resident, inquired about the allocation of ARPA funds to Black-owned businesses in
Town. She also expressed that she does not feel represented by her Town Councilors.

Agenda item 14. Topics Not Reasonably Anticipated by the Chair 48 Hours in Advance of the
Meeting
a. Request to waive Town Council Policy on Making Recommendations for Town
Council Appointments to Multiple-Member Bodies to re-open the Applicant Pool
for the Impending Zoning Board of Appeals Vacancies and to waive associated
other deadlines
Hanneke informed the Council that CRC has been handling interviews for vacant positions on the
Planning Board and the Zoning Board of Appeals. She explained the procedure for filling those vacancies
and explained that the CRC received a statement of interest from an interested party after the applicant
pool had been declared closed.
Schoen asked for clarification on what the motion does and does not do. O’Keeffe displayed a memo
explaining this information on the screen.
Pam expressed that she thinks the CRC has done their due diligence on this issue.
De Angelis expressed concern about making an exception to the normal policy. Devlin Gauthier
responded that the present circumstances seem to justify making an exception in this case.
Walker asked about the size of the applicant pool. Hanneke answered that there are four applicants
scheduled for interviews on Thursday, June 15th.
MOTION: Hanneke moved, seconded by De Angelis, to (1) waive Sections 7 and 9 of the Town Council
Policy on Making Recommendations for Town Council Appointments to Multiple-Member Bodies to (a)
re-open the applicant pool for the impending ZBA vacancies that closed at 10:32 am on June 8, 2023, so
that the applicant pool includes individuals that submitted both a CAF and a Statement of Interest prior to
11:59 pm on June 11, 2023, (b) permit the late posting of applicant Statements of Interest and not as a
group, and (c) permit less than 7 days’ notice of the adopted interview questions to all applicants; (2)
direct the CRC Chair and Council Clerk to cause the meeting agenda be modified to list any additional
applicants and to post any additional Statements of Interest in the packet and attach them to the meeting
notice by 11:30 am on Tuesday, June 13, 2023, and (3) to direct the CRC Chair to send any additional
applicants the adopted interview questions by 11:30 am on June 13, 2023; provided however, that any
additional candidates meeting this criteria that cannot attend the group interview scheduled for June 15,
2023 at 11:30 am by zoom shall not be considered applicants for the impending vacancies that the June 15
meeting is intended to make recommendations to fill.
VOTED 12-0 by roll call, with 1 absent (Miller), to (1) waive Sections 7 and 9 of the Town Council
Policy on Making Recommendations for Town Council Appointments to Multiple-Member Bodies to (a)
re-open the applicant pool for the impending ZBA vacancies that closed at 10:32 am on June 8, 2023, so
