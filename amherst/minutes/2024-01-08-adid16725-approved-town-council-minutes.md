Jeanne Horrigan urged improvements be made at the Senior Center, including security cameras and
bathroom emergency pull cords.

---

Dick Yourga, President of the Friends of the Senior Center, spoke about the need for space for exercise
equipment purchased by the Friends and asked that ARPA funding be used to create an exercise space
and kitchen.

Center and the center needs space and volunteers.
Vincent O’Connor spoke about high property taxes, Amherst College, UMass’ impact on parking, and the
need for road repairs throughout Town.

7. Presentations and Discussion
a. Town Manager Policy Goals: Major Capital Investments, Community Health and
Safety, Racial Equity and Social Justice
Schoen provided an overview of the Major Capital Investments goal.
Councilors discussed the goal briefly, noting that a youth empowerment center, senior center, and a
community center could be combined into one location, the potential to use ARPA funds for Senior
Center renovations, and the need for a location for a new DPW facility.
Taub provided an overview of the Community Health and Safety goal.
Walker gave an overview of the Racial Equity and Social Justice Goal.
Councilors briefly discussed the goal, speaking about the need to include anti-racism in every direction
and that anti-Black racism should be separate from anti-racism.

c. Annual Review of Town Council Rules of Procedure – Rules 1-4, 7-10
De Angelis reported on the Governance, Organization, and Legislation Committee’s recommendation
regarding the proposed rule changes.
MOTION: Hanneke moved, second by Devlin Gauthier, to adopt amendments to the Town Council
Rules of Procedure rules 2-4 and 7-9, with additions shown in red or green text and deletions shown in
red or green strikethrough text on pages 8-25 of the motions sheet.
Steinberg raised a concern that there is a grammatical error in rule 4.5 and suggested replacing “who”
with the word “that” in reference to a committee.
MOTION: Steinberg moved, second by Rooney, to amend the motion by replacing “who” with “that” in
the second sentence of rule 4.5.
VOTED Unanimously by roll call to amend the motion by replacing “who” with “that” in the second
sentence of rule 4.5.
Councilors discussed the rules regarding the consent agenda, what should be placed on the consent
agenda, discussion of consent agenda items permitted before the vote, and the quantum of vote for the
consent agenda.
Steinberg raised a concern that the quantum of vote for the consent agenda, stating that he would prefer
that an action with a higher vote quantum required not be placed on the consent agenda.
Councilors discussed the vote requirement for consent agenda items, with disagreement about which
items are appropriate for the consent agenda and the vote threshold needed for the consent agenda when
there are some items that require a higher number of votes to pass.
MOTION: Hanneke moved, second by De Angelis, to amend the motion by diving rule 4.6 from the
motion.
