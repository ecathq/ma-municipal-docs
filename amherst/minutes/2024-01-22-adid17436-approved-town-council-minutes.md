8. Action Items
a. Annual Review of Town Council Rules of Procedure – Rules 4.6, 5, and 6
MOTION: Griesemer moved, second by Rooney, to adopt amendments to the Town Council Rules of
Procedure rule 5, with additions shown in red or green text and deletions shown in red or green
strikethrough text on pages 11-14 of the motions sheet.
De Angelis reported on the Governance, Organization, and Legislation Committee recommendations for
amendments to rule 5.
Councilors discussed the issue of yielding time to other speakers during public comment.
Councilors discussed rule 5.1.a. and the meaning of the section that reads “Public comments shall be on
matters within the jurisdiction of the Council, be made in a peaceable manner, and comply with the First
Amendment of the United States.”
Councilors discussed rules regarding signing up for public comment on the register and the time limit for
public comment of 120 minutes.
VOTED Unanimously by roll call with 1 absent (Walker) to adopt amendments to the Town Council
Rules of Procedure rule 5, with additions shown in red or green text and deletions shown in red or green
strikethrough text on pages 11-14 of the motions sheet.
MOTION: Griesemer moved, second by Hanneke, to adopt amendments to the Town Council Rules of
Procedure rule 6, with additions shown in red or green text and deletions shown in red or green
strikethrough text on pages 15-18 of the motions sheet.
De Angelis and Hanneke spoke about the Governance, Organization, and Legislation Committee
amendment recommendations to rule 6.
MOTION: Steinberg moved, second by Ryan, to amend the motion by inserting the words “or
communicate with other Councilors or the public by text phone, email, or other means” in proposed 6.1.f.
so that the rule reads, “Participants shall not hold private conversations or communicate with other
Councilors or the public by text phone, email, or other means during Council meetings.”
Griesemer suggested the rule read, “Participants, while actively engaged in a discussion shall not hold
private conversations or communicate with Councilors or the public by text, phone, or other means during
Council meetings.”

The motion was withdrawn.
MOTION: Steinberg moved, second by Ryan, to amend the motion by replacing “Participants” with
“Councilors” and by inserting the words “or communicate with other Councilors or the public by text
phone, email, or other means” in proposed 6.1.f. so that the rule reads, “Councilors shall not hold private
conversations or communicate with other Councilors or the public by text, phone, email, or other means
during Council meetings.”
VOTED 2-10 by roll call with 1 absent to amend the motion by replacing “Participants” with
“Councilors” and by inserting the words “or communicate with other Councilors or the public by text
phone, email, or other means” in proposed 6.1.f. so that the rule reads, “Councilors shall not hold private
conversations or communicate with other Councilors or the public by text, phone, email, or other means
during Council meetings.”
