President Griesemer declared the presence of a quorum, called the meeting to order at 6:33 p.m.,

3. Hearings – None

b. Amherst Resolution for a Ceasefire in Gaza
Devlin Gauthier reported that the Governance, Organization, and Legislation Committee declared the
resolution clear, consistent, and actionable.

---

MOTION: De Angelis moved, second by Walker, to adopt the Resolution in Support of a Ceasefire in
Gaza, amended as follows:
 In the Community Sponsor line, replace the number 240 with the phrase “More than 760”;
 In the first Whereas clause, replace the word “took” with the word “killed” and replace the word
“lives” with the phrase “people, including civilians and children, and took approximately 240
hostages”;
 In the second Whereas clause, add the words “and bombardment” after the word “siege” and
replace the number 27,500 with the number 30,000;
 In the fifth Whereas clause, replace the comma after the word released with a semi-colon, replace
the phrase “no further hostages or prisoners” with the phrase “only two more hostages”, and
replace the phrase “more than twenty-five have died” with the phrase “, as long as the violence
continues, more hostages will continue to die”;
 In the sixth Whereas clause, replace the phrase “government has pledged” with the phrase
“administration has requested”, replace the word “to” after military aid with the word “for”, and
add the phrase “(currently pending in Congress)” after the words “Israeli government”;
 In the seventh Whereas clause, replace the word “was” with the phrase “has three times been”,
replace the word “the” after the word veto with the word “a”, and add the phrase “in Gaza” after
the word ceasefire;
 In the eighth Whereas clause, add the word “continuing” before the word conflict and replace the
phrase “is rapidly spreading to the West Bank, Lebanon, Yemen, Iraq, Syria, and beyond” with
the phrase “in Gaza threatens to destabilize the whole region”;
 In the first Now Therefore clause, delete the comma and add the word “and” after the first use of
the word Gaza, replace the phrase “an end to unconditional” with the word “for” and add the
phrase “to be conditioned on strict adherence to U.S. and international human rights laws” after
the phrase “Israeli government”; and
 Adding the list of community sponsors to the end of the document.
Sponsors requested that the Council vote on the resolution as moved without additional amendments.
Councilors discussed the sponsors’ request and read the resolution with the changes proposed.
MOTION: Devlin Gauthier moved, second by Hanneke, to move the previous question.
VOTED 2-10 by roll call with 1 abstention to move the previous question.
Aye: Devlin Gautier, Schoen
Nay: De Angelis, Ette, Griesemer, Hanneke, Hegner, Rooney, Ryan, Steinberg, Taub, Walker
Abstain: Lord
Absent: None
MOTION: Taub moved, second by Schoen, to amend the resolution by adding the phrase “and the
Resolution in the Wake of the Hamas Attack on Israel” to the final BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED after
the words “shall cause a copy of this Resolution.”
Councilors discussed the proposed amendment; sponsors agreed that they would not object to the
amendment.
MOTION: Hanneke moved, second by De Angelis, to move the previous question.
VOTED unanimously by roll call to move the previous question.

VOTED unanimously to amend the resolution by adding the phrase “and the Resolution in the Wake of
the Hamas Attack on Israel” to the final BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED after the words “shall cause a
copy of this Resolution.”
MOTION: Steinberg moved, second by Rooney, to amend the resolution by inserting, “WHEREAS,
Hamas positioned its military assets in Gaza such that many Gaza residents are in harm’s way; and
WHEREAS, fighting continues on both sides with Hamas firing rockets into Israel, Israel attacking
Hamas, and noncombatant citizens of Gaza and Israel displaced from their homes, injured and killed;
and” between the first and third WHERAS clauses.
Councilors attempted to discuss the amendments; audience members repeatedly disrupted the meeting
proceedings.
Sponsors indicated they would not support the resolution with the proposed amendments and the
amendments could be incorporated into a separate resolution for Council action.
VOTED 8-4 by roll call to amend the resolution by inserting, “WHEREAS, Hamas positioned its
military assets in Gaza such that many Gaza residents are in harm’s way; and WHEREAS, fighting
continues on both sides with Hamas firing rockets into Israel, Israel attacking Hamas, and noncombatant
citizens of Gaza and Israel displaced from their homes, injured and killed; and” between the first and third
WHERAS clauses.
Aye: Ette, Griesemer, Hegner, Rooney, Ryan, Schoen, Steinberg, Taub
Nay: De Angelis, Devlin Gauthier, Hanneke, Lord, Walker
Abstain: None
Absent: None
Audience members repeatedly interrupted the proceedings; Griesemer repeatedly attempted to bring the
meeting to order.
MOTION: De Angelis moved, second by Walker, to amend the resolution by removing “Community
Sponsors: More than 760 Amherst residents (see attached list) and Councilor Sponsors: Patricia De
Angelis, Ellisha Walker, and Mandi Jo Hanneke,” and by adding the attached list of community sponsors
at the end of the resolution.
Council sponsors reiterated they would not like their names listed as sponsors of the resolution as
amended and are requesting the Council remove their names and the names of the community sponsors,
who have indicated they would not support amendments. Hanneke added that the Council rules do not
allow withdrawal of a measure at a meeting, and require a withdrawal memo submitted prior to a Council
meeting where the withdrawal is listed on the agenda; the proposed amendment is intended to withdraw
support for the resolution given the constraints of the rules.
Councilors discussed how to respond to the sponsors request and if the motion to adopt the resolution will
be in order following a vote to remove Council sponsors.
Hanneke stated she did not believe the motion to adopt the resolution would be in order without Council
sponsors listed.
VOTED unanimously by roll call to amend the resolution by removing “Community Sponsors: More
than 760 Amherst residents (see attached list) and Councilor Sponsors: Patricia De Angelis, Ellisha
Walker, and Mandi Jo Hanneke,” and by adding the attached list of community sponsors at the end of the
resolution.

Griesemer stated “the Council sponsors and community sponsors have been removed. We are now
without a resolution.”
Members of the audience became disruptive, shouting at Council members.
Ryan called a point of order.
President Griesemer declared the meeting adjourned.
Amid shouts from the audience, Councilor Ryan repeatedly called point of order.
President Griesemer stated again the meeting is adjourned.
After De Angelis loudly stated “point of order,” bringing it to the President’s attention, the Council
continued deliberation on the resolution.
Ryan asked if Councilors can be added as sponsors to the resolution at this point, stating that he would be
willing to add his name.
Throughout this period the audience interrupted Councilors with shouting. President Griesemer repeatedly
asked the audience to quiet down and maintain decorum in the room.
Ryan suggested the meeting be adjourned due to the interruptions from the audience.
The audio connection on Zoom was briefly interrupted.
Councilors discussed the resolution; the sponsors advocated for a vote on the resolution as it was rather
than making changes.
De Angelis stated that sponsors wished to remove their names from the resolution due to the nature of the
amendments that were approved by the Council.
MOTION: Schoen moved, second by Griesemer, to reconsider the vote to amend the resolution by
inserting, “WHEREAS, Hamas positioned its military assets in Gaza such that many Gaza residents are
in harm’s way; and WHEREAS, fighting continues on both sides with Hamas firing rockets into Israel,
Israel attacking Hamas, and noncombatant citizens of Gaza and Israel displaced from their homes, injured
and killed; and” between the first and third WHERAS clauses.
Ryan spoke about the interruptions from the audience, stating the Council was being bullied and harassed
during the meeting, impeding the Council from doing their business and Councilors from having
thoughtful consideration of the matters before them. Ryan spoke in opposition to a reconsideration due to
the interruptions from the audience.
Devlin Gauthier spoke in support of the motion to reconsider and asked audience members to maintain
decorum so the Council can continue its business.
Steinberg stated he has not been heard, spoke about his election by the entire community, and as a
legislative body the Council has an obligation to craft resolutions presented. Steinberg stated the Council
has a right to make amendments to a resolution before it is adopted as part of its deliberation and careful
consideration of matters before it.
VOTED 12-1 by roll call to reconsider the vote to amend the resolution by inserting, “WHEREAS,
Hamas positioned its military assets in Gaza such that many Gaza residents are in harm’s way; and
WHEREAS, fighting continues on both sides with Hamas firing rockets into Israel, Israel attacking
Hamas, and noncombatant citizens of Gaza and Israel displaced from their homes, injured and killed;
and” between the first and third WHERAS clauses.

MOTION ON THE FLOOR: To amend the resolution by inserting, “WHEREAS, Hamas positioned its
military assets in Gaza such that many Gaza residents are in harm’s way; and WHEREAS, fighting
continues on both sides with Hamas firing rockets into Israel, Israel attacking Hamas, and noncombatant
citizens of Gaza and Israel displaced from their homes, injured and killed; and” between the first and third
WHERAS clauses.
Rooney spoke in support of including the first WHEREAS clause and not the second.
MOTION: Rooney moved, second by Ryan, to amend the motion by striking “WHEREAS, fighting
continues on both sides with Hamas firing rockets into Israel, Israel attacking Hamas, and noncombatant
citizens of Gaza and Israel displaced from their homes, injured and killed; and” from the motion.
De Angelis and Devlin Gauthier spoke in opposition to including either of the amendments.
Taub spoke in support of including the second WHEREAS clause rather than the first.
Councilors clarified the motion on the floor and possible further amendments.
Rooney and Ryan withdrew their motion.
Hanneke stated the original sponsors, whose names have been removed, would not support the resolution
with the two added clauses.
Ryan urged Councilors to maintain fortitude and remember that Councilors represent the entire town and
not only the people in the room, and urged Councilors to support the motion.
Walker stated the Council has already voted a resolution condemning Hamas; the current resolution is a
message to residents that the Town values the lives of Palestinians. Walker urged Councilors to consider
their privilege to uplift marginalized voices.
Taub stated the crux of the resolution is a ceasefire, humanitarian aid, release of hostages on both sides,
and that remains in the resolution.
De Angelis likened Palestinians in Israel to Black citizens in the United States in terms of how they are
treated, adding that the Council cannot be balanced on this issue because the Israeli government has
called Palestinians “animals.”
VOTED 5-8 by roll call to amend the resolution by inserting, “WHEREAS, Hamas positioned its
military assets in Gaza such that many Gaza residents are in harm’s way; and WHEREAS, fighting
continues on both sides with Hamas firing rockets into Israel, Israel attacking Hamas, and noncombatant
citizens of Gaza and Israel displaced from their homes, injured and killed; and” between the first and third
WHERAS clauses.
Aye: Ette, Rooney, Ryan, Steinberg, Taub
Nay: De Angelis, Devlin Gauthier, Griesemer, Hanneke, Hegner, Lord, Schoen, Walker
Present (Abstain): None
Absent: None
The motion failed.
