2. Announcements
3. Action Items
a. Response to Offner Open Meeting Law Complaint
MOTION: Griesemer moved, second by Devlin Gauthier, to determine that the Town Council did not
violate the Open Meeting Law during the last 45 minutes of the March 4, 2024 Town Council meeting; to
acknowledge the Council actions taken during the last 45 minutes of the March 4, 2024 Town Council
Meeting; to confirm the record of the meeting; and to authorize KP Law to respond to the Open Meeting
Law complaint filed by Mike Offner on March 6, 2024, consistent with the Council’s discussion on
March 13, 2024, that the Town Council did not violate the Open Meeting Law.
Hanneke stated the record of the meeting reflected what occurred at the March 4 meeting and spoke in
favor of the motion.
VOTED unanimously by roll call to determine that the Town Council did not violate the Open Meeting
Law during the last 45 minutes of the March 4, 2024 Town Council meeting; to acknowledge the Council
actions taken during the last 45 minutes of the March 4, 2024 Town Council Meeting; to confirm the
record of the meeting; and to authorize KP Law to respond to the Open Meeting Law complaint filed by
Mike Offner on March 6, 2024, consistent with the Council’s discussion on March 13, 2024, that the
Town Council did not violate the Open Meeting Law.
b. Response to Steinberg Open Meeting Law Complaint
MOTION: Devlin Gauthier moved, second by Lord, to determine that the Town Council did not violate
the Open Meeting Law during the last 45 minutes of the March 4, 2024 Town Council meeting; to
acknowledge the Council actions taken during the last 45 minutes of the March 4, 2024 Town Council
Meeting; to confirm the record of the meeting; and to authorize KP Law to respond to the Open Meeting
Law complaint filed by Lonna Steinberg on March 6, 2024, consistent with the Council’s discussion on
March 13, 2024, that the Town Council did not violate the Open Meeting Law.
Hanneke again stated the record of the meeting reflected what occurred at the March 4 meeting and spoke
in favor of the motion.

---

VOTED unanimously by roll call to determine that the Town Council did not violate the Open Meeting
Law during the last 45 minutes of the March 4, 2024 Town Council meeting; to acknowledge the Council
actions taken during the last 45 minutes of the March 4, 2024 Town Council Meeting; to confirm the
record of the meeting; and to authorize KP Law to respond to the Open Meeting Law complaint filed by
Lonna Steinberg on March 6, 2024, consistent with the Council’s discussion on March 13, 2024, that the
Town Council did not violate the Open Meeting Law.
