Special Town Council Meeting – Retreat
Wednesday, March 27, 2024, 6:00 p.m.
Town Room, Town Hall
Minutes
Councilors Present: De Angelis, Devlin Gauthier, Ette, Griesemer, Hanneke, Hegner, Rooney, Ryan,
Schoen, Steinberg, Taub
Councilors participating remotely: Lord, Walker
Staff present: Town Manager Bockelman, Assistant Town Manager Ziomek, Council Clerk O’Keeffe
1. Call to Order
Griesemer declared the presence of a quorum, called the meeting to order at 6:14 p.m., confirmed
Councilors could hear and be heard, and announced audio and video recording.
2. Announcements
3. Opening Exercise
Councilors participated in an opening exercise regarding speaking, listening, and being heard.
4. Meeting Management
Councilors discussed the Town Council Rules of Procedure, parliamentary procedure, and options
available to Councilors and the President during meetings when there is not clarity on what is happening
or being voted, and when the meeting is disrupted.
5. Presentations and Discussion
a. Town Manager Policy Goals
I. Climate Action
II. Community Health and Safety
III. Economic Vitality
IV. Housing Affordability
V. Major Capital Investments
VI. Racial Equity and Social Justice
Councilors discussed their rankings of the Town Manager goals in terms of priorities of the subgoals;
what can be accomplished and what are ongoing, long-term goals; and resources required to accomplish
the goals.
6. Town Council Comments
7. Topics Not Reasonably Anticipated by the Chair 48 Hours in Advance of the Meeting
8. Adjourn
MOTION: Griesemer moved, second by Schoen to adjourn.
VOTED unanimously by roll call to adjourn.
Respectfully submitted,
Council Clerk O’Keeffe
Record of documents and other exhibits used during the meeting
