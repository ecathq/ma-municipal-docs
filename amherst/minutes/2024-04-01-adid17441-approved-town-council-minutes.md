a. Amherst Pelham Regional School Budget
Slaughter presented the Amherst-Pelham Regional School Budget as voted by the Regional School
Committee.
Sarahbess Kenney, chair of the Regional School Committee, spoke about the committee’s review of
budget cuts and efforts to avoid student-facing budget cuts.
Sarah Marshall, member of the Amherst School Committee and Regional School Committee, spoke about
the damage that would result in cuts that would need to be made to budget within the 4% increase.
Councilors spoke about the following:
 Putting pressure on the state to increase Chapter 70 funding
 Advocating at the state level for charter school cost reduction
 Cost of choice-in special education
 The process for approving the budget among the four regional member towns
 Required acceptance of the assessment method by all four members towns
 The proposed FY25 budget would become the base for the FY26 budget and assessment method
 Regional School Committee delay in communicating to the four towns that cuts were
unacceptable
 Regional School Committee and leadership planning for the foreseen fiscal cliff
 Reduction in availability of ESSER (Elementary and Secondary School Emergency Relief)
budget support
 Funding changes due to the implementation of the Student Opportunity Act
 Suggestion of using reserves that were set aside for a DPW facility building and south Fire
Station
 Cost of solving the problem in terms of making cuts in the Town budget
 Urging tax-exempt higher education institutions to help fund schools
 Use of ESSER funds for one-time expenses rather than ongoing costs
 Impact of new union contracts in cost of living and salary increases
 Decreasing enrollment
 Maintaining quality educational services in Amherst with the given challenges
 Needed repairs to the middle school roof that have not been funded by the Massachusetts School
Building Authority, and use of ARPA funds for the repairs
 Conversations with Amherst College about funding for schools
MOTION: Griesemer moved, second by Steinberg, in accordance with Charter Section 5.5(a), to refer
the Amherst Pelham Regional School Budget to the Finance Committee for a Public Hearing and
recommendation to the Council within 30 days.
VOTED unanimously by roll call with 1 absent (Lord) in accordance with Charter Section 5.5(a), to refer
the Amherst Pelham Regional School Budget to the Finance Committee for a Public Hearing and
recommendation to the Council within 30 days.

Taub, Griesemer, and De Angelis disclosed that their spouses have relationships with some of the
applicants and projects, but their relationships do not pose a conflict of interest.
MOTION: Griesemer moved, second by Rooney, in accordance with Charter Sec. 5.6, having been
published on the Town Bulletin Board for a minimum of 10 days on February 23, 2024, a Public Forum
held on March 18, 2024, and having been reviewed by the Finance Committee with a report on April 1,
2024, to adopt Council Order FY25-07A: An Order Appropriating the FY2025 Community Preservation
Act Budget, as shown on page 7 of the Motions Sheet.
Hegner reported the Finance Committee recommended the Council approve the order with the exception
of the East Amherst local historic district due to the language in the application and the language in the
original order that appears to be to fund the district rather than a study.
Community Preservation Act Committee chair MacLeod spoke about the committee’s recommendation
and the description of the project listed and the committee is not able to alter the language in the
applications as submitted.
Councilors discussed the following:
 Wording in the application that appears inaccurate
 Staff support of the application and proposed east village study
 Use of the words “intrusions,” “shoddily built student hotels…” in the application
 Unclear wording in the order that appears to approve the district rather than a study
 Suggestion to change the application, if allowed by the Division of Local Services
 The perceived intent of the applicant
 Preserving the historic character of the Town
 The goal of the study to establish what structures may be historic in the area and if establishing an
historic district would be appropriate
MOTION: Ryan moved, second by Rooney, to amend the order by reinserting the East Amherst Local
Historic District item back to the order, by changing the appropriation amount to $1,835,136, under
Historic Preservation, adding East Amherst historic district, with an amount of $20,000 with a funding
source of CPA UDFB, changing the Less Projects Recommended above to $(1,855,136), and the
Remaining UDFB for Debt Service (to be voted in the Gen Fund) to $520,250.
VOTED 10-2 by roll call with 1 absent to amend the order by reinserting the East Amherst Local Historic
District item back to the order, by changing the appropriation amount to $1,835,136, under Historic
Preservation, adding East Amherst historic district, with an amount of $20,000 with a funding source of
CPA UDFB, changing the Less Projects Recommended above to $(1,855,136), and the Remaining UDFB
for Debt Service (to be voted in the Gen Fund) to $520,250.
