c. Authorization for the Town Manager to enter an Intergovernmental Agreement and
Memorandum of Understanding for ValleyBike Share
MOTION: Griesemer moved, second by Hegner, to authorize the Town Manager to (re-)enter the Town
of Amherst into an intermunicipal governmental agreement in accordance with M.G.L. Ch. 40, Section
4A with the municipalities of Chicopee, Easthampton, Holyoke, Northampton, Springfield, South Hadley,
West Springfield and such other municipalities that join later, and the Pioneer Valley Planning
Commission and the University of Massachusetts, Amherst.
Sustainability Director Ciccarello spoke about her ongoing work developing partnerships with other
communities to restart the bike share program.
Steinberg asked about the cost of the program and funding source.
Ciccarello spoke about the sustainability funds that will be used to partially fund the program, and that the
program will be supplemented by sponsorships and state funding.
VOTED Unanimously by roll call to authorize the Town Manager to (re-)enter the Town of Amherst into
an intermunicipal governmental agreement in accordance with M.G.L. Ch. 40, Section 4A with the
municipalities of Chicopee, Easthampton, Holyoke, Northampton, Springfield, South Hadley, West
Springfield and such other municipalities that join later, and the Pioneer Valley Planning Commission and
the University of Massachusetts, Amherst.
d. North Pleasant Street Pedestrian Improvements between Pine Street and Eastman
Lane
MOVED: Griesemer moved, second by Ryan, to approve the conceptual plan titled “North Pleasant
Street Pedestrian Improvements” dated April 1, 2021 and consisting of 4 pages showing improvements to
the sidewalks, crosswalks, and bus stops along North Pleasant Street from Pine Street to Eastman Lane.
DPW Superintendent Mooring spoke about the planned improvements from Eastman Lane to Pine Street
and Meadow Street on North Pleasant Street:
 Multi-use path with space for bicycles and pedestrians
 Rehabilitation and widening from 3’ to 5’ of the sidewalk
 Adding crosswalks
 Improvements to bus pull-off areas
Schoen expressed concerns with moving the bus stop, the cost of the project, and that the existing
conditions do not warrant the extensive rehabilitation planned. Schoen stated there are other sidewalks in
the area that need attention.

Steinberg reported that the Town Services and Outreach Committee recommended Council approval of
the project after consulting with the Transportation Advisory Committee.
Ziomek noted that funding is not currently available for the entire plan and the density and number of
users in the area is high. Ziomek also spoke about the availability of using Community Development

Pleasant Street from Pine Street to Eastman Lane.
e. Proposed Surplus Property Disposition Policy – First Reading
Hegner gave a brief report from the Finance Committee on the updated policy and when the policy
applies, which is only when the Town determines a property has no viable public use and the Town
wishes to sell the property.
Rooney asked for a list of Town facilities and properties and asked how the process is initiated.
Ziomek spoke about the Town Manager initiating the process.
Councilors briefly discussed a school property that is not proposed to be declared surplus at this time.
f. Authorization for Council President to sign a letter on behalf of the Council in
Support of S.1836/H.2963 – An act relative to payments in lieu of taxation by
organizations exempt from property tax
MOTION: Griesemer moved, second by Schoen, to authorize the Council President to sign a letter on
behalf of the Council in support of S.1836/H.2963 An Act Relative to Payments in Lieu of Taxation by
Organizations Exempt from Property Tax.
Griesemer spoke to the motion and shared some suggested edits to the letter that she received from
Councilors. Griesemer clarified that the legislation would not apply to the University of Massachusetts at
Amherst.
VOTED Unanimously, 12-0, with 1 absent (Hanneke) by roll call to authorize the Council President to
sign a letter on behalf of the Council in support of S.1836/H.2963 An Act Relative to Payments in Lieu of
Taxation by Organizations Exempt from Property Tax.
