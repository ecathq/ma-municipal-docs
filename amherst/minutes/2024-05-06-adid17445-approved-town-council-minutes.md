8. Action Items
a. Acceptance of a Public Way: Amherst Hills
Brestrup reported on the Planning Board deliberations and recommendations on acceptance of the public
way; the Planning Board voted to recommend the Town Council accept the requested roads.
Mooring stating the roads have been through several reviews; over the course of the project the roads
aged and deteriorated; repairs were made by the developer and the roads are now in adequate condition
for acceptance by the Town.
Council discussion:

---

 Concerns regarding sureties given to the Town
 Comments unrelated to the topic regarding another private way that will be taken up at a future
meeting
 Request for drawings of the roads
No action taken; action on the item will be taken up on May 20.
b. Fiscal Year 2025 Budget, Capital Inventory, and Capital Improvement Program
VOTED via consent agenda in accordance with Charter Section 5.5(a), to refer the FY25 Budget, Capital
Inventory, and Capital Improvement Program to the Finance Committee for a public hearing and
recommendation to the Council within 30 days.
The Town Manager, Co-Interim Finance Directors, Consultant, and Council Clerk presented the Town
Manager’s proposed FY2025 budget.
Council discussion:
 Five-year projections; sources to increase revenue such as the Municipal Empowerment Act
 Capital budget may be updated based on the status of the Jones Library building project
 Need for conversations about long-range budgeting between the Town and Regional Schools
 How the Town might achieve a 6% increase for the regional schools
 Future debt service payments for the library
 Regional school budget request higher than the Town Manager’s proposed budget
 The process the Town Council could take to increase the regional school budget
 Where cuts could be made to increase the regional school budget
 Upcoming Budget Coordinating Group meeting in the coming month
 Shortfalls in the budget projections for the years ahead
 Unfilled positions; any savings from the previous fiscal year from unfilled positions would not be
available until free cash is certified
 Structural deficit in the regional school budget; need for a multi-year strategy
 School committee request to increase the regional school budget
 Solutions to the regional school structural deficit must be from a recurring source to solve the
issue
 Amherst College gives the regional schools funds, and the schools determine how to spend it
 Budgeting within the 2 ½% limit
 Financial ramifications of creating a Sustainability Department; no additional funds were used to
establish the department
 Regional schools begin budgeting with cost to maintain services, which results in rising costs at a
greater rate
 Regional schools’ use of one-time funds as stopgap measure but not solving the structural deficit
 Cost of Jones library project compared to repair options
 Need for collaboration with the regional schools to address budget shortfalls
c. Proposed Council Order FY25-11: An Order Approving the Acceptance of Optional
Tax Exemptions for FY2025
VOTED via consent agenda to refer proposed Council Order FY25-11: An Order Approving the
Acceptance of Optional Tax Exemptions for FY25 to the Finance Committee, for a recommendation to
the Council within 30 days.
