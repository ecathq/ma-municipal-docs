Special Town Council & Finance Committee Meeting
Public Forum on an Appropriations Outside the Annual Budget
Town Hall, Town Room
Monday, May 20, 2024, 6:30 p.m.
Minutes
Councilors present: De Angelis, Devlin Gauthier, Ette, Griesemer, Hanneke, Rooney, Ryan, Schoen,
Steinberg, Taub
Councilors participating remotely: Hegner, Walker
Councilors absent: Lord
Non-voting Finance Committee members participating remotely: Holloway
Non-voting Finance Committee members absent: Kubiak
Staff present: Town Manager Bockelman, Council Clerk O’Keeffe
1. Call to Order
Finance Committee Chair Hegner declared the presence of a quorum of the Finance Committee, called the
Public Forum on an Appropriation Outside the Budget to order at 6:34 p.m., confirmed members could
hear and be heard, and announced audio and video recording.
President Griesemer declared the presence of a quorum of the Town Council, called the Special Town
Council meeting to order at 6:32 p.m., confirmed Councilors could hear and be heard, and announced
audio and video recording.
2. Public Forum on a Supplemental Budget Appropriations – Charter Section 5.6
a. Council Order FY24 – 05D An Order Appropriating Funds for a Portion of the
Town of Amherst Capital Program – New Fire Department Pumper Truck –
supplemental appropriation to cover cost escalation
Griesemer briefly spoke about the previous appropriation for the pumper truck and subsequent request for
an additional appropriation to cover cost escalation.
There were no public comments.
3. Finance Committee Recommendation of FY24 – 05D An Order Appropriating Funds for a
Portion of the Town of Amherst Capital Program – New Fire Department Pumper Truck –
supplemental appropriation to cover cost escalation
Hegner briefly reported on the Finance Committee recommendation to the Town Council to adopt the
appropriation order.
4. Topics Not Reasonably Anticipated by the Chair 48 Hours in Advance of the Meeting
5. Adjourn
MOTION: Hanneke moved, second by Steinberg, to adjourn the Finance Committee Public Forum.
VOTED Finance Committee voted unanimously by roll call, with 1 non-voting member absent (Kubiak)
to adjourn the Finance Committee Public Forum at 6:41 p.m.
