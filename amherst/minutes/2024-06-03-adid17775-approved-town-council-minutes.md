7. Presentations and Discussion
a. Update on the Jones Library Building Project
Bockelman gave a brief update on the Jones Library Building Project:
 The project has received approval from elected officials and the public at the polls
 Challenges to the proposal
 COVID-19
 Original bid was over budget
 Options to repair, rebid, or do value engineering
 The Town needs an extension from the Massachusetts Board of Library Commissioners to move
forward with the project with the MBLC grant
 Private fundraising is an unknown if the projects shifts to repair
 Challenges with swing space for the library during construction
 Inflation is a factor that impacts bidding
Council discussion:
 Gap in fundraising
 Interest rates
 Town’s risk of being “on the hook” for gap in fundraising
 Confidence the Trustees will secure adequate fundraising
 Cost of repair without expansion
 Most recent repair estimate that excluded certain issues, including asbestos abatement
 Outdated repair estimate
 Unknown value engineering changes and bids
 Cost to the Town of halting the project
 Next steps if the MBLC were to deny the Town’s extension request
 Value engineering changes to the project
 Town’s financial responsibility to repair the building if the project fails
 Cost of deferred maintenance
 Option of repairs made over time
 Timing of the bid that may have impacted the cost of the bid received
 Need to move staff out of the building to address asbestos
 Need for a permanent home for the Civil War tablets that are currently housed in the Bangs
Community Center
MOTION: Schoen moved, second by Walker, to recommend that the Town Manager rescind the request
that the Massachusetts Library Commission extend the deadline for the Jones Library project and inform
the MBLC that Amherst will not be moving forward with the project due to evidence from bids that the
expense exceeds available financial resources.
VOTED: 6-7 by roll call; the motion fails.
Aye: Hegner, Lord, Rooney, Schoen, Taub, Walker
Nay: De Angelis, Devlin Gauthier, Ette, Griesemer, Hanneke, Ryan, Steinberg
Present (abstain): None
Absent: None
