2. Capital Improvement Program Public Forum – Charter Sec. 5.7(d)
Bockelman, Drake, and LaFountain gave a brief presentation of the Capital Improvement Program:
 Alignment with Town Council financial guidelines
 Project highlights:
o Roof replacements
o Sustainability projects
o Public safety radio equipment
o Sidewalks
o Roads
o Police cruisers
o DPW building replacement
