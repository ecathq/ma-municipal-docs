Special Town Council & Finance Committee Meeting
Public Forum on Appropriations Outside the Annual Budget
Town Hall, Town Room
Monday, June 17, 2024, 6:45 p.m.
Minutes
Councilors present: De Angelis, Devlin Gauthier, Ette, Griesemer, Hanneke, Hegner, Lord, Rooney,
Ryan, Schoen, Steinberg, Taub
Councilors participating remotely: Walker
Councilors absent: None
Non-voting Finance Committee members present: None
Non-voting Finance Committee members participating remotely: Holloway, Kubiak
Non-voting Finance Committee members absent: None
Staff present: Town Manager Bockelman, Council Clerk O’Keeffe
Staff participating remotely: DPW Superintendent Mooring, Amherst Schools Superintendent Slaughter,
Assistant Town Manager Ziomek
1. Call to Order
2. Public Forum on Amendments to Council Order FY23-08A An Order Appropriating and
Authorizing Debt for the Rehabilitation of and Improvements to the Amherst-Pelham
Regional School District’s High School Track and Field Facilities under Open
Space/Recreation; Amendment to Council Order FY23-05C An Order Appropriating
Funds for a Portion of the Town of Amherst Capital Program – School Track and Field
Rehabilitation; and Council Order FY24-03B An Order Approving the Town of Amherst
Gift the Amherst-Pelham Regional School District, $756,160 for the High School Track and
Field Project, for the North-South oriented design option 3C
Assistant Town Manager Ziomek and Amherst School Superintendent Slaughter spoke briefly about the
project and funding scenarios.
Public comment: six residents and the Principal of Amherst High School spoke in support of the project.
3. Finance Committee Recommendation
There were no motions to reconsider the Finance Committee recommendations of the orders.
4. Topics Not Reasonably Anticipated by the Chair 48 Hours in Advance of the Meeting
5. Adjourn
Finance Committee Motion: Hanneke moved, second by Schoen to adjourn the Finance Committee
meeting.
VOTED Unanimously with two resident members’ support at 7:16 p.m.
MOTION: Griesemer moved, second by Hanneke, to close the public forum and adjourn the Public
Forum meeting.
VOTED Unanimously by roll call to adjourn the Public Forum meeting at 7:16 p.m.
