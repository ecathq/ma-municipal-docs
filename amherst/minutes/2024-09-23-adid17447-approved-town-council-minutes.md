f. Referral of Proposed University Drive Overlay District – Adoption of Overlay
District, Changes to Official Zoning Map by adding the University Drive Overlay
District
MOTION: Griesemer moved, second by Hanneke, to refer the Proposed University Drive Overlay
District – Adoption of Overlay District, Changes to Official Zoning Map by adding the University Drive
Overlay District to the Planning Board and the Community Resources Committee:
 Community Resources Committee and Planning Board public hearings must be held no
later than November 27, 2024 (within 65 days of the referral)
 Written Planning Board recommendation must be sent to the Town Council and
Community Resources Committee no later than 21 days of the close of the Planning
Board public hearing
 Written Community Resources Committee recommendation and all materials must be
sent to the Town Council and Governance, Organization, and Legislation Committee no
later than 30 days of the close of the CRC public hearing
 Written Governance, Organization, and Legislation report on clarity, consistency, and
actionability must be sent to the Town Council within 60 days of the close of the CRC
public hearing
 Town Council action is required within 90 days of the close of the CRC public hearing
Malloy spoke about the proposed overlay:
 Existing zoning will remain
 Overlay applies to mixed-use developments by site plan review
 Effort to encourage infill development
 Planned pedestrian pathway on University Drive
 Streetscape with shops, housing, and flexible parking
 Requirement for applicants to provide parking memo and study that shows there will be adequate
parking
 Inclusionary zoning applies
 Goal to address housing needs and encourage economic development
 Will require a majority vote of the Council to adopt
 Flexible dimensional and parking standards
 All other bylaws and regulations will continue to apply
Council discussion:
 Access road is private property with easements; could be revisited with adoption of the new
district; previously the Town limited curb cuts, which created the need for the access road
 Concerns that the Big Y supermarket property will be sold to a developer
 Desire to keep the supermarket at the current location
 High water table in the area
 Need for housing for families and seniors
 Parking concerns
 Concern there is no provision for apartments and only mixed-use buildings; want to encourage
housing development in the area
 Could tailor the definition of apartments in the overlay and create a threshold of 75% of frontage
for commercial use
 Desire for the overlay to be larger
 Desire for the overlay to be smaller to exclude the supermarket shopping center
