a. Proposed University Drive and Amity Street Roundabout
MOTION: Griesemer moved, second by Ryan, to recommend that the Town Council approve the
proposed changes to the intersection of Amity Street and University Drive to convert the existing
intersection from signaled controlled to a full-size roundabout, as shown on the Pavement Marking and
Signing Plans drawing number CT-101, dated April 15, 2024 and as modified by additional adjustments
to be made by the DPW Superintendent to align with changes requested by the Transportation Advisory
Committee and Disability Access Advisory Committee.
Steinberg reported on the Town Servies and Outreach Committee review and recommendation; the
committee concluded that it was a well thought out proposal, with grant funding, and strong support from
the Department of Public Works, Town Manager. TSO consulted with the Disability Access Advisory
Committee and Transportation Advisory Committee, who recommended the changes with concern for
disabled individuals and cyclists, reflected in the committee’s recommendation to the Town Council.
Schoen asked why the change is needed, as her experience with the intersection has not been problematic.
Steinberg spoke further about the DAAC and TAC review and discussion.
Mooring added that roundabouts have been shown to slow traffic, which will make a safer intersection for
pedestrians and cyclists; non-vehicle users will have a flashing crosswalk button.
VOTED 11-1, with 1 abstention to recommend that the Town Council approve the proposed changes to
the intersection of Amity Street and University Drive to convert the existing intersection from signaled
controlled to a full-size roundabout, as shown on the Pavement Marking and Signing Plans drawing
number CT-101, dated April 15, 2024 and as modified by additional adjustments to be made by the DPW
Superintendent to align with changes requested by the Transportation Advisory Committee and Disability
Access Advisory Committee.

b. Proposed Request to Town Manager for Financial Order for Road and Sidewalk
Repairs
MOTION: Griesemer moved, second by Ette, to request the Town Manager submit an appropriation
request for a portion of the Amherst Capital Program -- Road Repairs and Sidewalk Repairs in the amount
of $500,000.
Ryan spoke to his proposal, which is intended to address the backlog of road and sidewalk repairs; the
Town Manager’s previous request for a supplemental appropriation for $1M was defeated by a tie vote;
the current proposal would allow the Town to make headway on the longstanding problem.
Hanneke asked how much the elementary school spent repairing alarm systems, and why the proposal is
being brought now rather than via the FY26 Capital Planning process.
Taub spoke in support of the motion and asked about capital requests from the schools.
Bockelman stated the alarm system repairs at Crocker Farm Elementary cost roughly $80,000, which
came from the school’s capital account; no other capital requests have come forward from departments.
Councilors discussed the timing of this request, the capital allocated to road and sidewalk repairs in the
FY25 Capital Plan, and questions related to elementary school capital needs.
Hanneke raised concern regarding use of the funds now.
Ette spoke in support of the motion, pointing out the use of roads and sidewalks by all residents.
Steinberg spoke in support of the motion, stating the roads and sidewalks will continue to deteriorate if no
action is taken.
VOTED Unanimously, 13-0, by roll call, to request the Town Manager submit an appropriation request
for a portion of the Amherst Capital Program -- Road Repairs and Sidewalk Repairs in the amount of
$500,000.
c. FY26 Financial Guidelines
MOTION: Griesemer moved, second by Hegner, to adopt the FY26 Budget Guidelines, as presented.
Hegner spoke about changes the Finance Committee made that were reflected in the draft presented and
recommended by the committee. Hegner pointed out the change to the section regarding construction
costs for the elementary school building to reflect the fact that any savings will reduce the borrowing
amount rather than a budget surplus; currently the final building cost is unknown.
Councilors discussed and agreed to changes by consensus.
Council discussion:
 The Council should be consulted prior to the Budget Coordinating Group in case of a budget
surplus
 How to address unmet and upcoming urgent capital needs
 How to minimize capital appropriation requests outside the Joint Capital Planning Committee
review and recommendation process
