Minutes
Councilors present: De Angelis, Devlin Gauthier, Griesemer, Hanneke, Hegner, Rooney, Schoen,
Steinberg, Taub

1. Call to Order
President Griesemer declared the presence of a quorum, called the meeting to order at 7:10 p.m.,
confirmed Councilors could hear and be heard, and announced audio and video recording.
Hegner declared the presence of a quorum of the Finance Committee, called the meeting to order at 7:11
p.m., and confirmed Porter could hear and be heard.
2. Public Forum on Supplemental Budget Appropriations – Charter Section 5.6
a. Council Order FY25 – 04B – An Order Appropriating a supplemental increase to
the Town of Amherst Sewer Enterprise Fund Operating Budget for Fiscal Year
2025
b. Council Order FY25 – 04C – An Order Appropriating a supplemental increase to
the Town of Amherst Solid Waste Enterprise Fund Operating Budget for Fiscal
Year 2025
c. Council Order FY25 – 05B – An Order Appropriating Funds for the Town of
Amherst Sewer Fund Capital Program – Sewer Collection System and Wastewater
Treatment pH and Chlorine Studies and Additional Cost associated with the
Gravity Belt Thickener project
d. Council Order FY25 – 05C: An Order Appropriating Funds for a Portion of the
Town of Amherst Capital Program – Sidewalk Equipment
e. Community Preservation Act Appropriation and Transfer Order FY25 – 08A An
Order Appropriating and Authorizing Debt for construction of the re-oriented
track & field facility, known as Option 3C at the Amherst Pelham Regional High
School
Vincent O’Connor spoke about school funding and budget surpluses.
3. Finance Committee Recommendation
Hegner reported on the Finance Committee’s recommendation to the Town Council. There were no
motions to reconsider the committee’s recommendation.
