Ryan, Schoen, Steinberg, Taub

1. Call to Order
President Griesemer declared the presence of a quorum, called the meeting to order at 6:02 p.m.,
confirmed Councilors could hear and be heard, and announced audio and video recording.

3. 2025 Town Manager Goals
O’Keeffe shared the rankings Councilors submitted prior to the meeting and the Town Manager’s
comments regarding staff capacity and other resources required to achieve proposed goals.
Councilors discussed including the Complete Streets plans in the goals, reaching tier 2 or tier 3.

tier 2 and tier 3 prioritization to Mass DOT, and keep in mind the kind of data we need to complete tier 2
and 3.
