8.a. Open Meeting Law Complaint filed by Mr. Ahmed Esfahani, dated Jan 29, 2025:
Acknowledge receipt of complaint, review and discuss Council’s response, and authorize
Council President to work with Town Attorney to finalize submission of response to
Complainant and Attorney General
MOTION: Griesemer moved, second by De Angelis, to determine that the Town Council did not violate
the Open Meeting Law as alleged in the Open Meeting Law complaint filed by Ahmad Esfahani dated
January 29, 2025 and to authorize KP Law to work with the Council President to respond to the Open
Meeting Law complaint consistent with the Council’s discussion on February 10, 2025, that the Town
Council did not violate the Open Meeting Law as alleged; additionally, in the interests of transparency

---

and the Town Council’s commitment to compliance with the Open Meeting Law, to review and approve
any outstanding meeting minutes and voluntarily post them to its website as promptly as feasible.
Councilors briefly discussed the complaint with Town Attorney Goldberg.
Hanneke noted the Council and its Committees have a backlog of minutes to approve and requested a
schedule of when minutes will be prepared.
VOTED Unanimously with 2 absent (Taub and Walker) to determine that the Town Council did not
violate the Open Meeting Law as alleged in the Open Meeting Law complaint filed by Ahmad Esfahani
dated January 29, 2025 and to authorize KP Law to work with the Council President to respond to the
Open Meeting Law complaint consistent with the Council’s discussion on February 10, 2025, that the
Town Council did not violate the Open Meeting Law as alleged; additionally, in the interests of
transparency and the Town Council’s commitment to compliance with the Open Meeting Law, to review
and approve any outstanding meeting minutes and voluntarily post them to its website as promptly as
feasible.

c. Referral of Appropriation and Transfer Request – Bangs Community Center
Project to Finance Committee
MOTION: Griesemer moved, second by Ryan, to refer appropriation and transfer order FY25-05F An
Order Appropriating Funds for a Portion of the Town of Amherst Capital Bangs Community Center
Renovation to the Finance Committee, for recommendation and report to the Council by March 24, 2025.
Bockelman, Morra, and Ziomek briefly presented the appropriation request, the funding source, and plans
for improvements at the Bangs Community Center.
Council discussed the planned improvements, asked about the space at the Senior Center for exercise
equipment and kitchen areas, and use of the Bangs Center for voting purposes.

05F An Order Appropriating Funds for a Portion of the Town of Amherst Capital Bangs Community
Center Renovation to the Finance Committee, for recommendation and report to the Council by March
24, 2025.

Activity Form and Town Council Policy on Making Recommendations for Town Council Appointments
to Multiple-Member Bodies to the Governance, Organization, and Legislation Committee, with a report to
the Council by March 24, 2025.

Rooney spoke to her proposal, stating that the online form to volunteer for Town Council appointed
committees does not align with the Town Council policy on recommendations for appointments.
Hanneke noted the memo should be addressed to the Council, not to the Governance, Organization, and
Legislation Committee; the language that is requested to be amended is unclear, the language is unclear
about communication between applicants and recommending committee chairs, and it is not clear that
Community Activity Forms would remain private.
VOTED Unanimously with 1 absent (Taub) by roll call to refer the proposed amendments to the
Community Activity Form and Town Council Policy on Making Recommendations for Town Council
Appointments to Multiple-Member Bodies to the Governance, Organization, and Legislation Committee,
with a report to the Council by March 24, 2025.
e. Request for Long-Term Reservation of Public Way: Amherst Farmer’s Market
MOTION: Griesemer moved, second by Ryan, to approve the Amherst Farmers’ Market Long-Term
Event Use reservation of the South Common and associated parking spaces at no charge, under Town
Council Policy Regarding the Control and Regulation of the Public Ways on Saturdays from 8:30 a.m. to
1:30 p.m. beginning on April 19, 2025 and ending on November 15, 2025:
 Approve exclusive use of the area on the South Common as shown on the plan entitled
“Amherst Farmers’ Market; Proposed Layout 2022 Season: January 27, 2022”;
 Reserve 15 spaces in the Spring Street lot, six spaces on Boltwood Avenue, and five
spaces on South Pleasant Street for all for vendor parking and customer pick-up to be
clearly marked by the Amherst Farmer’s Market.
And to further authorize the Town Manager the ability to modify the approval through November
15, 2025 in order to schedule concurrent use of the Town Common for short-term event uses.
VOTED Unanimously with 1 absent (Taub) by roll call to approve the Amherst Farmers’ Market Long-
Term Event Use reservation of the South Common and associated parking spaces at no charge, under
Town Council Policy Regarding the Control and Regulation of the Public Ways on Saturdays from 8:30
a.m. to 1:30 p.m. beginning on April 19, 2025 and ending on November 15, 2025:
 Approve exclusive use of the area on the South Common as shown on the plan entitled
“Amherst Farmers’ Market; Proposed Layout 2022 Season: January 27, 2022”;
 Reserve 15 spaces in the Spring Street lot, six spaces on Boltwood Avenue, and five
spaces on South Pleasant Street for all for vendor parking and customer pick-up to be
clearly marked by the Amherst Farmer’s Market.
And to further authorize the Town Manager the ability to modify the approval through November
15, 2025 in order to schedule concurrent use of the Town Common for short-term event uses.
f. Town Manager Goals
MOTION: Griesemer moved, second by De Angelis, to adopt the 2025 Town Manager Goals.
MOTION: Schoen moved, second by Rooney, to amend by adding, “Provided that the funding for the
Trustee share, grants and other non-taxpayer share of the Jones Project is secured and the budget is no
more than $46.1 million, sign a contract and break ground on the Jones Library renovation and expansion
project” to the Infrastructure Management, Maintenance, and Land Stewardship” goal area.
Schoen spoke to the motion, stating she wants to keep the taxpayer share of the funding as what it has
been stated it will be.
Ryan stated it is unclear what is meant by “secured.”

Councilors discussed the motion.
Steinberg spoke in favor, stating that it appears to be authorization for the Town Manager to sign a
contract, and would allow the Council an opportunity to make a different judgement if funding is not
secured.
Rooney spoke in favor.
Councilors discussed the timeline for securing funding.
Walker spoke in support.
VOTED 6-5 to amend by adding, “Provided that the funding for the Trustee share, grants and other non-
taxpayer share of the Jones Project is secured and the budget is no more than $46.1 million, sign a
contract and break ground on the Jones Library renovation and expansion project” to the Infrastructure
Management, Maintenance, and Land Stewardship” goal area.
Aye: Devlin Gauthier, Hegner, Rooney, Schoen, Steinberg, Walker
Nay: De Angelis, Ette, Griesemer, Hanneke, Ryan
Present (abstain): None
Absent: Lord, Taub
MOTION: Hanneke moved, second by Schoen, to delete residential rental and five-year capital plan.
Hanneke spoke to the motion, stating that the Council was told the goals do not need to include actions
that will be completed as a matter of course.
VOTED 10-1 by roll call to delete residential rental and five-year capital plan.
Aye: De Angelis, Devlin Gauthier, Ette, Griesemer, Hanneke, Hegner, Rooney, Ryan, Schoen, Steinberg
Nay: Walker
Present (abstain): None
Absent: Lord, Taub
VOTED Unanimously with 1 absent (Taub) by roll call to adopt the 2025 Town Manager Goals, as
amended.
