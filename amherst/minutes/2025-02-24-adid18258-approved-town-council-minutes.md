a. APPROPRIATION and TRANSFER ORDER - FY25 – 05F An Order Appropriating Funds for
a Portion of the Town of Amherst Capital Bangs Community Center Renovation
MOTION: Griesemer moved, second by Ette, having held a public forum on February 24, 2025, with a
recommendation by the Finance Committee, to adopt Appropriation and Transfer Order FY25-05F: An
Order Appropriating Funds for a Portion of the Town of Amherst Capital Program Bangs Community
Center Renovation, as shown on page 9 of the Motions Sheet.
Schoen spoke to sustainability in the renovated community center and grant funds to supplement
capital funding.
Ryan asked about funding sources.
Councilors asked about a commercial grade kitchen, which is not in the project budget at this time.
VOTED unanimously having held a public forum on February 24, 2025, with a recommendation by the
Finance Committee, to adopt Appropriation and Transfer Order FY25-05F: An Order Appropriating Funds
for a Portion of the Town of Amherst Capital Program Bangs Community Center Renovation, as shown
on page 9 of the Motions Sheet.

c. Proposed Amendment to the Town Council Policy on Making Recommendations for
Town Council Appointments to Multiple-Member Bodies
MOTION: Griesemer moved, second by Hanneke, to refer the proposed amendment to the Town
Council Policy on Making Recommendations for Town Council Appointments to Multiple-Member
Bodies by deleting the phrase “for up to six years of consecutive service,” in the first sentence and
deleting the second and third sentences in Section 5, Reappointments to the Governance, Organization,
and Legislation Committee, with a recommendation to the Council by April 7, 2025.
Hanneke spoke to her proposal, which would align the Council Policy with the President’s allowable
term length.
Council discussion:

Schoen, Ryan, and Rooney spoke in opposition to the motion.
Councilors discussed term lengths, experience needed, and consistency with Council appointments to
multiple-member bodies.
VOTED 2-11 by roll call to refer the proposed amendment to the Town Council Policy on Making
Recommendations for Town Council Appointments to Multiple-Member Bodies by deleting the phrase
“for up to six years of consecutive service,” in the first sentence and deleting the second and third
sentences in Section 5, Reappointments to the Governance, Organization, and Legislation Committee,
with a recommendation to the Council by April 7, 2025.
Aye: Ette, Hanneke
Nay: De Angelis, Devlin Gauthier, Griesemer, Hanneke, Hegner, Lord, Rooney, Ryan, Schoen, Steinberg,
Taub, Walker
Absent: None
d. Vote to Refile An Act relative to the implementation of the charter for the city known
as the town of Amherst
MOTION: Griesemer moved, second by Hanneke, to petition the General Court for special legislation
and to authorize the Town Council President to sign the letter “Town of Amherst - An Act Relative to the
Implementation of Elements of the Charter for the City Known as the Town of Amherst” as presented,
requesting Senator Joanne Comerford and Representative Mindy Domb petition the General Court for
special legislation set forth in Council Order 21-33: An Act Relative to the Implementation of Elements of
the Charter for the City known as the Town of Amherst; provided, however, that the General Court may
make clerical or editorial changes of form only to the bill unless the Town Council approves
amendments to the bill before enactment by the General Court.
VOTED Unanimously by roll call with 1 absent (Walker) to petition the General Court for special
legislation and to authorize the Town Council President to sign the letter “Town of Amherst - An Act
Relative to the Implementation of Elements of the Charter for the City Known as the Town of Amherst”
as presented, requesting Senator Joanne Comerford and Representative Mindy Domb petition the
General Court for special legislation set forth in Council Order 21-33: An Act Relative to the
Implementation of Elements of the Charter for the City known as the Town of Amherst; provided,
however, that the General Court may make clerical or editorial changes of form only to the bill unless
the Town Council approves amendments to the bill before enactment by the General Court.
