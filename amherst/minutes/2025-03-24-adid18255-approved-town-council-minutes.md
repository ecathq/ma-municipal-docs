Collins, Jr. Center for Public Management at the University of Massachusetts Boston for consulting
services for the Charter Review Committee.

f. Amherst Pelham Regional School District Assessment Method
MOTION: Griesemer moved, second by Hanneke, to reject the following amendment to the Regional
School District Agreement: Replace subsection j) as follows:
“For Fiscal Year 2026 only, the assessments shall be allocated to the member towns in
accordance with the per-pupil method found in Section VI e) of the Amherst Pelham
regional School District Agreement. If any member town's assessment based on the regional
agreement method increases or decreases more than 6.5% from their Fiscal Year 2025
assessment, the fiscal Year 2026 assessment shall be limited to a 6.5% change provided that
the requirements of Minimum Local Contribution are still met.”; or take any other action
relative thereto.
MOTION: De Angelis moved, second by Ryan, to call the previous question.
VOTED 11-2 by roll call to call the previous question.
Aye: De Angelis, Ette, Griesemer, Hanneke, Hegner, Lord, Rooney, Ryan, Schoen, Steinberg, Taub
Nay: Devlin Gauthier, Walker
Absent: None
VOTED 9-4 by roll call to reject the following amendment to the Regional School District Agreement:
Replace subsection j) as follows:
“For Fiscal Year 2026 only, the assessments shall be allocated to the member towns in
accordance with the per-pupil method found in Section VI e) of the Amherst Pelham
regional School District Agreement. If any member town's assessment based on the regional
agreement method increases or decreases more than 6.5% from their Fiscal Year 2025
assessment, the fiscal Year 2026 assessment shall be limited to a 6.5% change provided that
the requirements of Minimum Local Contribution are still met.”; or take any other action
