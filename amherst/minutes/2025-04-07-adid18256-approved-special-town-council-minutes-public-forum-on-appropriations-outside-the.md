Special Town Council & Finance Committee Meeting Public Forum
on Appropriations Outside the Annual Budget
Community Preservation Act Budget & Charter Review Committee Appropriation
Monday, April 7, 2025, 6:30 p.m.
Town Room, Town Hall
Minutes
Councilors present: De Angelis, Devlin Gauthier, Ette, Griesemer, Hanneke, Hegner, Rooney, Ryan,
Steinberg, Taub
Councilors participating remotely: Lord, Schoen, Walker
Councilors absent: None
Non-voting Finance Committee members present: None
Non-voting Finance Committee members participating remotely: Kubiak
Non-voting Finance Committee members absent: Porter
Staff present: Town Manager Bockelman, Council Clerk O’Keeffe, Finance Director Zawadzki
1. Call to Order
Griesemer declared the presence of a quorum, called the meeting to order at 6:30 p.m., confirmed
Councilors could hear and be heard, and announced audio and video recording.
2. Presentations and Discussion:
a. Community Preservation Act Project Allocations
1) APPROPRIATION and TRANSFER ORDER – Community Preservation Act Project
Allocations: An Order Appropriating the FY2026 Community Preservation Act
Budget as required under M.G.L. Chapter 44B
b. Charter Review Committee Appropriation of $15,000 for Consultant Services
1) APPROPRIATION and TRANSFER ORDER: An Order Appropriating Charter
Review Committee Appropriation of $15,000 from Free Cash
3. Public Forum – Charter Section 5.6 More than one-half of the meeting time will be
devoted to public comment
All members of the public who wished to speak were given an opportunity to do so.
Finance Committee vote:
MOTION: Hanneke moved, second by Hegner, to recommend the Town Council adopt Appropriation and
Transfer Order FY25-05G: An Order Appropriating Funds for consulting services for the Charter Review
Committee.
VOTED Unanimously by roll call, with 1 non-voting member in support (Kubiak), and 1 non-voting member
absent (Porter) to recommend the Town Council adopt Appropriation and Transfer Order FY25-05G: An
Order Appropriating Funds for consulting services for the Charter Review Committee.
Finance Committee vote:
MOTION: Hegner moved, second by Hanneke, to adjourn the special meeting.
VOTED Unanimously by roll call, with 1 non-voting member in support (Kubiak), and 1 non-voting member
absent (Porter) to adjourn the special meeting.
