1. Call to Order
President Griesemer declared the presence of a quorum, called the special meeting of the Town Council
to order at 6:30 p.m., confirmed Councilors could hear and be heard and announced audio and video
recording.
President Sarat called the special meeting of the Jones Library Trustees to order at 6:32 p.m. and
confirmed Trustees could hear and be heard.
2. Announcements
3. Proposed Rescission of ORDER FY21-06C An Order Approving and Authorizing Borrowing
of $35,279,700 for the Expansion and Renovation of the Jones Library and Proposed
Rescission of 2023 supplemental borrowing authorization of $9,860,100 for the Expansion
and Renovation of the Jones Library
Legal Questions – Lauren Goldberg, KP Law
Councilors asked about the binding nature of the MOU between the town and the library trustees.
Goldberg confirmed it is legally binding; if trustees fail to meet their fundraising obligation, the Town is
ultimately responsible for the full cost but may pursue legal remedies.
Councilors asked what happens if project costs exceed the $46.1M authorization. Goldberg confirmed
the Town cannot spend beyond the appropriated amount without additional Council authorization. Any
excess would require either change orders to reduce scope or additional appropriations.
Presentation – Town Manager Paul Bockelman
Paul Bockelman presented a PowerPoint and detailed memo responding to Council’s prior questions:
 Project Cost & Bids: Contractor bids are mostly locked in, with exceptions (plumbing and metal
windows). The project remains within the $46.1M appropriation and includes an 8.5%
contingency. The roof will be re-bid in late spring.
 Grants, Fundraising and Endowment

---

o MBLC: $13.8M awarded, plus a $1.7M escalation grant. ~$5.5M more expected by June
30.
o Federal: $2M in HUD and NEH grants expected, though not yet under contract.
o Local/Private: Town’s $15.8M share (includes CPA funding) is secure. Trustees and
Friends have raised $10M of their $13.8M goal, with $2.5M received in cash.
o Trustees provided detail on endowment strategy and projections.
o Annual draw projected at $250K, increasing with inflation.
o Trustee modeling anticipates a drawdown of ~$4.3M with ongoing fundraising to
replenish the endowment.
 Potential Consequences of Rescinding Borrowing:
o Loss of >$30M in grant and external funding
o Repayment of $2.77M MBLC funds
o Delays of 6–18 months for repair-only planning, with costs up to $1M
o Repair-only option estimated to cost more than current project
Clarifying Questions – Councilors and Trustees
 Pam Rooney asked for details on total fundraising costs. Trustees estimate ~$125K/year,
covered by Friends (not endowment).
 Cathy Shoen inquired about cash flow and the feasibility of fronting funds. Trustees affirmed
approximately $4.5M available now, with pledge income projected at ~$2M over 3 years.
 Jennifer Taub asked for clarification on pledged vs. secured gifts. Trustees responded that
~$2.3M is already in hand, with robust donor retention.
 Additional questions focused on risk mitigation, project scope reduction if costs increase, and
fiduciary safeguards.
MOTION: Taub moved, second by Rooney, to rescind Appropriation and Borrowing Order FY21-06C an
order that approved and authorized bond authorization for $36,279,000 and to rescind the 2023
Supplemental appropriation for the expansion and renovation of the Jones Library, said sum to
supplement the amount appropriated under the Town Council’s April 5, 2021 Order Approving and
Authorizing Borrowing of Funds for the Expansion and Renovation of the Jones Library, $9,860,100.
VOTED 3-9 by roll call with 1 absent to rescind Appropriation and Borrowing Order FY21-06C an order
that approved and authorized bond authorization for $36,279,000 and to rescind the 2023
Supplemental appropriation for the expansion and renovation of the Jones Library, said sum to
supplement the amount appropriated under the Town Council’s April 5, 2021 Order Approving and
Authorizing Borrowing of Funds for the Expansion and Renovation of the Jones Library, $9,860,100.
Aye: Rooney, Taub, Walker
