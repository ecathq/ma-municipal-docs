b. Amherst Black Reparations Committee Charge
Devlin Gauthier reported on the Governance, Organization, and Legislation Committee’s
recommendation, which is a revised charge developed by GOL following significant feedback from
Councilors and community stakeholders.
MOTION: Griesemer moved, second by Devlin Gauthier, to adopt the Amherst Black Reparations
Committee Charge, as presented.
Key Themes in Discussion:
 Clarification of committee’s mission: Is it to “implement reparations” or to provide
recommendations and advice to Council?
 Questions on committee composition and qualifications (e.g., business/economic development
experience missing)
 Suggestions to revise appointment term from 2 years to 3 years
 Importance of evaluation mechanisms for funded programs
 Proposals to include gift acceptance language and define funding timelines (annual or biennial)
Councilor Comments and Suggested Amendments:
 Councilors Schoen and Hanneke proposed language around budgeting, evaluating program
success, and clarifying mission language
 Councilor Ryan raised concerns about the phrase “implement reparations”
 Councilor Taub asked for clarification about legal and procedural oversight of fund disbursement
(Town Manager confirmed legal review is required before any expenditure)
MOTION: Hanneke moved, second by Griesemer, to refer the Amherst Black Reparations Committee
Charge back to Governance, Organization, and Legislation Committee.
VOTED Unanimously by roll call with 1 absent (De Angelis) to refer the Amherst Black Reparations
Committee Charge back to Governance, Organization, and Legislation Committee.

f. Long-Term Public Way Request: Mobile Market
VOTED via consent agenda to approve the requests from the Amherst Mobile Market for the
2025 Mobile Market season (June 1 – October 31, 2025) as follows, exact location and
duration to be determined by the Town Manager in consultation with the Amherst Mobile
Market:
 East Hadley Road at Riverglade Drive:
o Placement of two tents and six tables in the public way near the Valley Bike Station
on East Hadley Road, once per week on Saturdays from 9 a.m. to 3 p.m.
 990 North Pleasant Street:
o Placement of one sandwich-board-style sign advertising the Amherst
Mobile Market permanently during the Mobile Market season at 990
North Pleasant Street plus the placement of two tents and six tables in the
public way on the grass space next to the bus stop Wednesdays from 2
p.m. to 7 p.m.
 Entrances to Colonial Village at 81 Belchertown Road and at South East Street:
o Placement of two sandwich-board-style signs advertising the Amherst
Mobile Market permanently during the Mobile Market season at 81
Belchertown Road and the South East Street entrance to Colonial Village
advertising the market located in Colonial Village.
