a. Fiscal Year 2026 Budget, Capital Inventory, and Capital Improvement Program
FY26 Town Budget Presentation – Town Manager Paul Bockelman & Finance Team
Key Highlights:
 FY26 Budget is balanced with a total of $103,261,100, the first budget exceeding $100M.
 Town maintains strong financial footing to support capital projects and maintain favorable bond
ratings.
 Health insurance increases (14%) and collective bargaining obligations drove budget pressures.
 Town Manager emphasized fiscal responsibility and trade-offs required to support public
schools.
 Frozen Positions: 6.4 vacant positions across departments (DPW, Fire, Police, CRESS, Recreation,
Finance) have been frozen, not eliminated.
 Departmental Impact: Municipal operating budgets remain level funded, limiting flexibility for
inflation-driven costs.

---

 Investments preserved for public education: Elementary Schools: Funded at 5% (School
Committee requested 6%); Regional Schools: Funded at 4.81% (in line with Committee request)
 Capital Improvement Program (CIP):
o Total capital funding: $9.6M, including: Public safety equipment, School facility planning
and transportation (electric buses, $450K for reconfiguration), Road repairs ($750K
supplementing Ch. 90 funds), Sustainability initiatives ($250K), Building repairs (Town
Hall roof)
 Policy Goals Addressed
 Revenue Projections: Local receipts increased by 8.1% (not expected to be sustainable year over
year)
 Five-Year Outlook:
o Conservative assumptions included for tax growth, state aid, and local receipts
o Capital spending targets to return to 10.5% of revenues, following this year's 10.265%
allocation
MOTION: Griesemer moved, second by Schoen, in accordance with Charter Section 5.5(a), to refer the
FY26 Budget, Capital Inventory, and Capital Improvement Program to the Finance Committee for a
public hearing on the FY26 Budget and recommendation on the Budget and Capital Improvement
Program to the Council within 30 days.
VOTED Unanimously by roll call with 1 absent (Devlin Gauthier) in accordance with Charter Section
5.5(a), to refer the FY26 Budget, Capital Inventory, and Capital Improvement Program to the Finance
Committee for a public hearing on the FY26 Budget and recommendation on the Budget and Capital
Improvement Program to the Council within 30 days.
b. Acceptance of Roads as Public Ways: Palley Village Place
MOTION: Griesemer moved, second by Hegner, that the Town Council hereby declares its intention to
lay out Palley Village Place as a public way and to refer the layout and plan to the Planning Board for its
review in accordance with MGL c.41, s.81G – 81I; with report back to the Town Council within 45 days
from this referral.
VOTED Unanimously by roll call with 1 absent (Devlin Gauthier) that the Town Council hereby declares
its intention to lay out Palley Village Place as a public way and to refer the layout and plan to the
Planning Board for its review in accordance with MGL c.41, s.81G – 81I; with report back to the Town
Council within 45 days from this referral.
