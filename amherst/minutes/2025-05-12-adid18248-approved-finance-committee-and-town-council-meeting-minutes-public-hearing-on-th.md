1. Call to Order
Schoen declared the presence of a quorum of the Finance Committee, called the Finance Committee
meeting to order at 6:30 p.m., confirmed members could hear and be heard, and announced audio and
video recording.
Griesemer declared the presence of a quorum of the Council, called the Council meeting to order at 6:35
p.m., and confirmed Councilors could hear and be heard.
2. Hearings – Public Hearing of the Finance Committee on the FY26 Town Budget – Charter
Sec. 5.5(a)
Town Manager Bockelman presented a brief overview of the FY26 Town Budget.
Public comment:
Vince O’Connor spoke about street repairs.
Rich MacLean spoke about regional and elementary school funding.
Cathleen Mitchell spoke about how information in the budget is presented and regional and elementary
school funding.
Martha Hanner spoke about funding for the CRESS Department.
Rani Parker echoed Hanner’s comments.
Jeff Mazur echoed previous comments regarding CRESS and school funding.
Amber Cano-Martin also spoke about CRESS and school funding.
Laura Evonne spoke about school funding.
Anjelica Castro also spoke about CRESS funding.
