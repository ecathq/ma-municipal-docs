Town of Amherst
Special Meeting of the Town Council and Finance Committee

Monday, June 2, 2025, 6:30 p.m.

Minutes

Taub

Mooring
1. Call to Order
Griesemer declared the presence of a quorum, called the meeting to order at 6:30 p.m., confirmed
Councilors could hear and be heard, and announced audio and video recording.
Schoen declared the presence of a quorum of the Finance Committee, called the meeting to order at
6:33 p.m., and confirmed members could hear and be heard.
2. Capital Improvement Program Public Forum – Charter Sec. 5.7(d)
Bockelman provided a brief presentation, highlighting the Town’s investments and 5-year capital
planning outlook.
Schoen spoke to the Finance Committee’s review of the Capital Improvement Program.
Public Comment:
Vincent O’Connor spoke about sidewalks and road conditions.
3. Topics Not Reasonably Anticipated by the Chair 48 Hours in Advance of the Meeting
4. Adjourn
MOTION: Griesemer moved, second by Hanneke, to adjourn the Public Forum.
VOTED Unanimously with 2 absent (Devlin Gauthier, Walker) to adjourn the Public Forum.
FINANCE COMMITTEE MOTION: Schoen moved, second by Hanneke, to adjourn.
FINANCE COMMITTEE VOTED Unanimously by roll call with 3 absent (Walker, Kubiak, Porter) to
adjourn.
