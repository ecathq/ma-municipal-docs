Others Participating Remotely:
1. Call to Order
After confirming all participants could hear and be heard, Council President Lynn Griesemer
declared the presence of a quorum, called the Town Council to order at 8:10 PM, and announced
audio and video recording. She also announced that the Finance Committee was already in
session.
Griesemer stated that the financial orders under consideration in this public hearing had already
been voted on by the Finance Committee and that later in the forum, the Finance Committee
would have the opportunity to comment on whether they stand by their existing votes.
2. Public Comment
Vincent O’Connor requested that the Council not place any money in the capital reserve fund
because he believes the current budget does not sufficiently prioritize schoolchildren and
educators. He also expressed concern that more funding is allocated to departments staffed
primarily by men. He stated that he does not have an opinion on the other appropriations at stake.

---

Deb Leonard asked why there is a rush to vote on these appropriations before the end of the
calendar year and requested that budgetary surpluses be used to address the needs of the regional
schools.
3. Finance Committee Comments
Regarding Appropriation and Transfer Order FY2505c, Finance Committee Chair Bob Hegner
informed the Council that the Superintendent of Public Works recently updated the desired
amount from $200,000 to $140,000 for sidewalk equipment purchases.
Schoen asked if the Department of Public Works had provided any more information about
potential uses for the requested sidewalk equipment beyond snow ploughing. Hegner stated that
the $200,000 device had a variety of uses, while the more inexpensive models under
consideration could only be used for snow removal. He also opined that the Finance Committee
needed to discuss the matter more before voting on it again.
Devlin Gauthier asked if the rest of the Council should consider Appropriation and Transfer
Order FY2505c stricken from the agenda if the Finance Committee should decide not to vote on
it. She also asked if questions on the matter should be emailed to the Finance Committee Chair.
Steinberg suggested that the Council postpone any action on Appropriation and Transfer Order
FY2505c so that the Finance Committee could discuss it further at their next meeting.
Devlin Gauthier stated that removing one of the requested appropriations from free cash may
impact decisions about how the remainder of the free cash is spent. She therefore asked if all the
appropriations should be pulled back until the Finance Committee has made a final decision on
Appropriation and Transfer Order FY2505c. O’Keeffe clarified that the current decision point at
stake was for the Finance Committee to reaffirm or amend their previous recommendations
regarding the appropriations requests.
4. Adjourn
MOTION: Hanneke moved, seconded by Steinberg, to adjourn the meeting of the Finance
Committee.
VOTED unanimously with one absent (Kubiak) to adjourn the Finance Committee meeting at
8:25 PM.
MOTION: Griesemer moved, seconded by Steinberg, to adjourn the public hearing on the
supplemental budget appropriations.
VOTED unanimously with one absent (De Angelis) to adjourn the public hearing on the
supplemental budget appropriations at 8:25 PM.
