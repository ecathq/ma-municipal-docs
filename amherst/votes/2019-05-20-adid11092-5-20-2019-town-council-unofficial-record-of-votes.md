b. Local Option Community Impact Fee – Short-Term Rentals
MOVED: To accept the provisions of MGL Chapter 64G, Section 3D to impose a local option
community impact fee at the rate of 3%, effective July 1, 2019 and applying to transfers of occupancy
of:
a. “Professionally-managed units” (one of two or more short-term rental units in the same city/town
operated by the same operator (owner) and not located within a single- or two- or three-family
dwelling that includes the operator’s primary residence.
Motion by: Pam
Seconded by: Schoen
VOTED: Unanimously
MOVED: To accept the provisions of MGL Chapter 64G, Section 3D to impose a local option
community impact fee at the rate of 3%, effective July 1, 2019 and applying to transfers of occupancy
of:
b. Short-term rental units located in a two- or three-family dwelling that includes the operator’s
primary residence.
Motion by: Pam
Seconded by: Hanneke
VOTED: Unanimously
MOVED: That 35% of the community impact fee collected under MGL Chapter 64G, Section 3D be
dedicated to affordable housing or local infrastructure projects and the balance of the funds be a general
fund revenue of the Town of Amherst that may be appropriated for any municipal purpose.
Motion by: Schoen
Seconded by: Pam
VOTED: 11-1-1
Yes Bahl-Milne, Yes Brewer, Yes De Angelis, Abstain DuMont, Yes Griesemer, Yes Hanneke, Yes
Pam, Yes Ross, Yes Ryan, Yes Schoen, Yes Schreiber, Yes Steinberg, Yes Swartz
