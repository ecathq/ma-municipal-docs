ORDER # FY20-24
An Order Repealing and Replacing the Zoning Bylaws of the Town
WHEREAS, in accordance with the Amherst Town Charter (“Charter”), which became effective upon approval by the
voters at the March 27, 2018 Annual Town Election, the Amherst Town Council serves as the legislative body of the
Town, including responsibility for adoption and amendment of General and Zoning Bylaws of the Town;
WHEREAS, Section 10.1 of the Charter provides that all Town Bylaws in effect on the effective date of the Charter that
have not been specifically or by implication repealed by the Charter, shall continue in full force and effect until the
Amherst Town Council (“Council”) shall amend or repeal them, or until they are rescinded by due course of law or expire
by their own limitations;
WHEREAS, the Charter, Section 10.7(u) expressly charged the Select Board to appoint a committee to begin a review of
the Town’s General and Zoning Bylaws for the purpose of preparing revisions and amendments as may be necessary to
bring them into conformity with the provision of the Charter and to fully implement the provisions of the Charter, with a
Bylaw Committee Charge adopted April 23, 2018, and revised May 2, 2018 and such Committee (“Section 10.7(u)
Committee”) appointed on April 23, 2018;
WHEREAS, the Section 10.7(u) Committee prepared and submitted to the Council-elect on November 29, 2018, and later
to the Amherst Town Council on December 3, 2018, its report with recommendations with respect to revisions and
amendments to the General Bylaws and Zoning Bylaws of the Town;
WHEREAS, the Charter, Section 10.7(v), expressly charged the Council with appointing a committee for the purpose of
completing the review started by the Section 10.7(u) Committee and preparing revisions and amendments as necessary to
bring the Bylaws into conformity with, to fully implement the provisions of, the Charter, with such Bylaw Review
Committee Charge adopted December 10, 2018; and such Committee (“Section 10.7(v) Committee”) appointed on
January 28, 2019;
WHEREAS, the Section 10.7(v) Committee prepared and submitted to the Council and to the Planning Board its report
with recommendations with respect to the Zoning Bylaws of the Town, including a proposal to repeal the existing Zoning
Bylaws in their entirety and to adopt as the Zoning Bylaws of the Town the provisions set forth in the document attached
to this Motion and incorporated herein by reference, entitled “Zoning Bylaws of the Town of Amherst - (Month) 2019”;
WHEREAS, as required by General Laws chapter 40A, §5, the Planning Board, after complying with statutory notice and
publication requirements, held a public hearing on June 5, 2019 on the proposed Zoning Bylaw amendments and revisions
and submitted to the Council its report with recommendations on June 12, 2019;
WHEREAS, as required by General Laws chapter 40A, §5, the Council, after complying with statutory notice and
publication requirements, held a public hearing on July 22, 2019 on the proposed Zoning Bylaw amendments and
revisions; and
WHEREAS, as required by Section 2.10(a) of the Charter, the Council, after complying with notice and publication
requirements, arranged for the proposed amendments and revisions to be read at two separate Council meetings held on
June 17, 2019 and July 22, 2019.
NOW THEREFORE BE IT ORDERED by the Town Council of the Town of Amherst that the Amherst Zoning Bylaws
be amended by deleting the current Zoning Bylaws in their entirety and inserting in place thereof a new Zoning Bylaw, in
the form set forth in the document attached hereto and incorporated by reference entitled, “Complete Zoning Bylaw July
2019”; and, further, to authorize the Town Clerk, following consultation with the Town Manager, to make such
ministerial and clerical changes of form only with respect to identification of articles, chapters, sections, subsections,
paragraphs and subparagraphs as are needed to bring such Bylaws into accord with the format of the bylaws of the Town
of Amherst.
