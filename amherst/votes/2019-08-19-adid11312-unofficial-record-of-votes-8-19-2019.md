President Griesemer called the meeting to order at 5:05 p.m.
7. Action Items
a. Rules of Procedure
MOVED: I move to amend the Town Council Rules of Procedure that were adopted on May 20, 2019
with the revisions indicated in the document titled "Rules of Procedure-adopted-2019-05-20 - proposed
GOL amendments - 2019-07-10 GOL Vote - Marked Up Version" to add a Table of Contents and the
footer throughout the document, add the language and hyperlinks, which are indicated as pink,
underlined language, delete the lined out language, and change the language in section 3.5.b.4 from
three months to six months and delete “and not later than October 31 of every calendar year.” I further
move that the Clerk shall update the pagination of the Table of Contents as necessary, once these
revisions are adopted.
Motion by: Hanneke
Seconded by: De Angelis
VOTED: 11-0-0 (Councilors Ross and Ryan were absent)
Councilors Bahl-Milne, Brewer, De Angelis, DuMont, Griesemer, Hanneke, Pam, Schoen, Schreiber,
Steinberg, and Swartz voted Yes;
b. Amend Order #: 01282019-6b2 passed on January 28, 2019
MOVED: to Amend Order #: 01282019-6b2 passed on January 28, 2019 ordering the Amherst
Energy and Climate Action Committee to submit initial goals to the Town Council by striking
the phrase “within 90 days of its first meeting” and replacing it with “within 180 days of its first
meeting.”
Motion by: DuMont
Seconded by: Steinberg
VOTED: 11-0-0 (Councilors Bahl-Milne, Brewer, De Angelis, DuMont, Griesemer, Hanneke, Pam,
Schoen, Schreiber, Steinberg, and Swartz voted Yes; Councilors Ross and Ryan were absent)
