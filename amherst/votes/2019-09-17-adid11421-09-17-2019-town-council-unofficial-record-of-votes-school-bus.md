Town of Amherst
UNOFFICIAL RECORD OF VOTES OF THE TOWN COUNCIL
Tuesday, September 9, 2019 5:30 p.m. Town Room, Town Hall
Members Present: Bahl-Milne, Brewer, De Angelis, Griesemer, Hanneke, Pam, Ryan, Schoen, Schreiber,
Swartz
Members Absent: None
Members Participating Remotely: DuMont, Ross, Steinberg
Staff Present: Town Manager Paul Bockelman, Clerk of the Council Athena O’Keeffe
President Griesemer called the meeting to order at 5:30 p.m.
Request to use previously appropriated and unspent capital to purchase a new school bus
MOVED: To postpone to September 23, 2019 at 9:00 p.m.
Motion by: Hanneke
Seconded by: Schoen
VOTED BY ROLL CALL: 6-6-1 (Councilors Bahl-Milne, De Angelis, DuMont, Hanneke, Schoen, and
Swartz voted Yes; Councilors Brewer, Pam, Ross, Ryan, Schreiber, and Steinberg voted No; President
Griesemer Abstained) The motion failed.
MOVED: To call the question.
Motion by: Schreiber
Seconded by: Hanneke
VOTED BY ROLL CALL: 11-1-1 (Councilors Bahl-Milne, Brewer, De Angelis, Griesemer, Hanneke,
Pam, Ross, Ryan, Schreiber, Steinberg, and Swartz voted Yes; Councilor Schoen Abstained)
Request to use previously appropriated and unspent capital to purchase a new school bus
On September 9, 2019: MOVED: To adopt Appropriation and Transfer Order FY20-25, "An
Order Appropriating Funds for New School Bus" as recommended by the Finance Committee
report dated September 6, 2019.
Motion by: Griesemer
Seconded by: Ryan
VOTED BY ROLL CALL: 10-2-1 (Councilors Bahl-Milne, Brewer, Griesemer, Hanneke, Pam, Ross,
Ryan, Schreiber, Steinberg and Swartz voted Yes; Councilors De Angelis and DuMont voted No;
Councilor Schoen Abstained)
MOVED: To adjourn the September 17, 2019 Special Meeting of the Town Council.
Motion by: Hanneke
Seconded by: Ryan
VOTED BY ROLL CALL: Unanimously
