Town of Amherst
UNOFFICIAL RECORD OF VOTES OF THE TOWN COUNCIL
Monday, October 7, 2019 6:30 p.m. Town Room, Town Hall
Members Present: Brewer, DuMont, Griesemer, Hanneke, Pam, Ross, Ryan, Schoen, Schreiber, Steinberg,
Swartz
Members Absent: Bahl-Milne and De Angelis
Members Participating Remotely: None
Staff Present: Town Manager Paul Bockelman, Clerk of the Council Athena O’Keeffe
President Griesemer called the meeting to order at 6:30 p.m.
3. Hearings
Public Hearing: Eversource Petition
MOVED: To approve the order for pole locations titled “Eversource Petition 09-05-2019.”
Motion by: Pam
Seconded by: Ross
VOTED: 10-0-0 (Councilors Brewer, DuMont, Griesemer, Hanneke, Pam, Ross, Ryan, Schoen,
Steinberg, and Swartz voted Yes; Councilors Bahl-Milne, De Angelis, and Schreiber were absent)
5. Proclamations and Commemorations
Kanegasaki 40th Anniversary of Life Long Learning Center
MOVED: To approve the Kanegasaki 40th Anniversary Proclamation, as presented.
Motion by: Griesemer
Seconded by: Pam
VOTED: 11-0-0 (Councilors Brewer, DuMont, Griesemer, Hanneke, Pam, Ross, Ryan, Schoen,
Schreiber, Steinberg, Swartz voted Yes; Councilors Bahl-Milne and De Angelis were absent)
7. Action Items
a. Long-Term Closure Public Way Request – 26 Spring Street
MOVED: To authorize the temporary use of the public way at 26 Spring Street, from October 15, 2019
to January 15, 2021.
Motion by: Steinberg
Seconded by: Ross
VOTED: 8-1-2 (Councilors Brewer, DuMont, Hanneke, Ross, Ryan, Schreiber, Steinberg and Swartz
voted Yes; Councilor Pam voted No; Councilors Griesemer and Schoen abstained; Councilors Bahl-
Milne and De Angelis were absent)
MOVED: To approve the application for Archipelago Investments LLC for reservation of four metered
parking spaces on the north side of Spring Street, as reflcted on the map dated October 7, 2019, from
October 15, 2019 to January 15, 2021, 24 hours per day, for a fee of $10.00 per meter per day, for a total
of $14,600.00.
Motion by: Steinberg

c. Zoning Bylaw Amendments Council Hearing Policy
MOVED: To designate the Community Resources Committee (CRC) as the committee to hold public
hearings under MGL Ch. 40A Sec. 5.
Motion by: Ryan
Seconded by: Swartz
VOTED: 11-0-0 (Councilors Brewer, DuMont, Griesemer, Hanneke, Pam, Ross, Ryan, Schoen,
Schreiber, Steinberg, Swartz voted Yes; Councilors Bahl-Milne and De Angelis were absent)
MOVED: To direct the Community Resources Committee (CRC) to hold joint hearings under MGL Ch.
40A Sec. 5 together with the Planning Board whenever feasible.
Motion by: Ryan
Seconded by: Pam
VOTED: 11-0-0 (Councilors Brewer, DuMont, Griesemer, Hanneke, Pam, Ross, Ryan, Schoen,
Schreiber, Steinberg, Swartz voted Yes; Councilors Bahl-Milne and De Angelis were absent)
