Town of Amherst
UNOFFICIAL RECORD OF VOTES OF THE TOWN COUNCIL
Wednesday, December 4, 2019 12:30 p.m. Town Room, Town Hall
Members Present:
Bahl-Milne Brewer, De Angelis, DuMont, Griesemer, Hanneke, Ross, Ryan, Steinberg, Swartz
Members Absent: Pam, Schoen, Schreiber
Members Participating Remotely: None
Staff Present: Town Manager Paul Bockelman, Clerk of the Council Athena O’Keeffe
President Griesemer called the meeting to order at 12:35 p.m.
4. Proclamations and Commemorations
a. Human Rights Day Proclamation
MOVED: To adopt the Human Rights Day Proclamation, as amended.
Motion by: Ross
Seconded by: De Angelis
VOTED: 9-0-0 (Councilors Pam, Schoen, and Schreiber were absent)
b. Resolution in Support of First Congregational, Amherst, United Church of Christ’s Request to
Maintain a Temporary Shelter
MOVED: To adopt the Resolution in Support of First Congregational, Amherst, United Church of
Christ’s Request to Maintain a Temporary Shelter, as amended.
Motion by: Hanneke
Seconded by: De Angelis
VOTED: 9-0-0 (Councilors Pam, Schoen, and Schreiber were absent)
MOVED: To reconsider the Human Rights Day Proclamation.
Motion by: Hanneke
Seconded by: Ryan
VOTED: 9-0-0 (Councilors Pam, Schoen, and Schreiber were absent)
MOVED: To adopt the Human Rights Day Proclamation, as amended by removing the words “since
2018.”
Motion by: Brewer
Seconded by: Hanneke
VOTED: 9-0-0 (Councilors Pam, Schoen, and Schreiber were absent)
5. Annual Charter Requirements
b. Receipt of Town Manager Memo Regarding Topics for Study
MOVED: To accept the receipt of the Town Manager memo regarding topics for study, per Charter Sec.
10.12, as completing the topics for study.
Motion by: Hanneke
Seconded by: De Angelis
