5. Proclamations and Commemorations
a. Reverend Dr. Martin Luther King, Jr. Proclamation
MOVED: To proclaim January 15, 2020 as Rev. Dr. Martin Luther King, Jr. Day, and adopt the REV.
DR. MARTIN LUTHER KING, JR PROCLAMATION, as presented.

VOTED: 12-0 (Councilors Brewer, De Angelis, DuMont, Griesemer, Hanneke, Pam, Ross, Ryan,
Schoen, Schreiber, Steinberg, Swartz voted Yes; Councilor Bahl-Milne was absent)
7. Action Items
a. Community Choice Aggregation
MOVED: To authorize the Town Manager to act jointly with other municipalities to initiate the process
to aggregate the electrical load of interested electricity consumers under Massachusetts General Laws
Chapter 164, Section 134 for the primary purpose of reducing greenhouse gas emissions through energy
efficiency and the development of local renewable distributed energy resources, provided that the Town
Council shall be required to approve the municipal aggregation plan before it is filed with the Department
of Public Utilities (DPU) and after it is developed in consultation with the Massachusetts Department of
Energy Resources (DOER). Further, to authorize the Town Manager to negotiate an intergovernmental
agreement or a joint powers agreement pursuant to Massachusetts General Laws Chapter 40, Section 4A
or Section 4A1/2 to develop and administer the municipal aggregation herein authorized and provide for
additional energy-related products and services, provided that execution of said agreement shall be
subject to authorization by the Town Council at a future meeting.
Motion by: Hanneke
Seconded by: Ross
MOTION: Councilor DuMont moved, second by Councilor Hanneke to amend the motion by (1) deleting the
phrase "provided that the Town Council shall be required to approve the municipal aggregation plan before it is
filed with the Department of Public Utilities (DPU) and after it is developed in consultation with the Department
