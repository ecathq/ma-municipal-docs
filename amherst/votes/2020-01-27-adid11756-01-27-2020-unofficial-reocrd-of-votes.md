MOVED: To add:
 Condominium and Cooperative Conversion, a General Bylaw recommended for deletion, to the
document Bylaws Identified for Future Consideration last edited 12-05-19
 Proposed General Bylaw 3.34 – Regulation of Signs
to the document “Bylaws Identified for Future Consideration last edited 12-05-19” and to refer the
document to the Town Council Governance, Organization & Legislation Committee (GOL) to report a
recommended timeline for developing a work plan to Town Council on March 9, 2020.
Motion by: Hanneke
Seconded by: Steinberg
VOTED: Unanimously
MOVED: To refer Section 3.4 Responsible Employer for Public Construction Projects to the
Government, Organization, and Legislation Committee to make a recommendation whether wording
deleted from the bylaw in three instances should be added back to avoid the risk of weakening the
bylaw. Specifically, add back: 1) the requirement that bidders "agree in writing that they shall comply"
to referenced labor laws; 2) Requirement that contractors/subcontractors shall "certify under oath and in
writing on a weekly basis that they are in compliance with such obligations"; and 3. The penalty of
"liquidated damages payable to the Town in the amount of 5% of the dollar value of the contract." that
was deleted from possible actions in the event of noncompliance.
Motion by: Schoen
Seconded by: Hanneke
VOTED: Unanimously
MOVED: To refer section 3.28 of the General Bylaw, the Single Use Plastic Bag Prohibition, and the
document titled “single use plastic bag bylaw D3 dd 01-23-2020” to the Governance, Organization, and
Legislation Committee, to make a recommendation on changes to the bylaw, with a report back to the
Town Council on March 9, 2020.
Motion by: DuMont
Seconded by: Hanneke
VOTED: Unanimously
MOVED: To refer to the Governance, Organization, and Legislation Committee Section 3.24 - Unlawful
Noise of the General Bylaws to make a recommendation on whether to remove lawn mowers, leaf
blowers, snow blowers, and other similar mechanical devices to the items included as "Loud, disturbing,
injurious, or unnecessary noise," with a report back to the Town Council on March 9, 2020.
Motion by: Hanneke
Seconded by: Ross
VOTED: Unanimously
