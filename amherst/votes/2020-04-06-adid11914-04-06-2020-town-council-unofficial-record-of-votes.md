UNOFFICIAL RECORD OF VOTES OF THE TOWN COUNCIL
Monday, April 6, 2020 6:30 p.m. Virtual Meeting
Members Present: None
Members Absent: None
Others Present: None
Members Participating Remotely: Bahl-Milne, Brewer, De Angelis, DuMont, Griesemer, Hanneke, Pam, Ross,
Ryan, Schoen, Schreiber, Steinberg, Swartz
Others Participating Remotely: Town Manager Paul Bockelman, Interim Finance Director Sonia Aldrich,
Health Director Julie Federman, Clerk of the Council Athena O’Keeffe
Pursuant to Governor Baker’s March 12, 2020 Order Suspending Certain Provisions of the Open Meeting Law,
G.L. c. 30A, §18, the meeting was conducted via remote participation.
President Griesemer called the meeting to order at 6:40 p.m.
5. Proclamations and Commemorations
a. Resolution Adopting an Interim Affordable Housing Policy (Sponsor: CRC)
MOVED: To adopt the Resolution Adopting an Interim Affordable Housing Policy, as
presented/amended.
Motion by: Griesemer
Seconded by: Bahl-Milne
VOTED: Unanimously by roll call
7. Action Items
a. Consent Agenda: The following items were selected because they were considered to be routine and it
was reasonable to expect they would pass with no controversy.

when the President lists the consent agenda items. The request to remove an item from the consent
agenda does not require a second.
MOVED: to move the following items, and the printed motions thereunder and approve those items as a
single unit:
● 7.b. Referral of Bylaw Banning Use of Wild and Exotic Animals to Community Resources
Committee, and automatic referral to Governance, Organization, and Legislation Committee
● 7.d. Acknowledgement of First Discussion of Amendment to Town Council Rules of Procedure rule
10.8k
● 10.a. Approval of March 30, 2020 Special Town Council Meeting minutes, as presented
Motion by: Griesemer
Seconded by: De Angelis
VOTED: Unanimously by roll call

MOVED: To do the following:
1. Repeal the following existing parking restrictions voted by the Select Board on July 27, 2015:
 Parking prohibited as a tow zone, on the east and west side of Lincoln Avenue, 120 feet north of
the intersection of Northampton Road.
