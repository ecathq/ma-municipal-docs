Town of Amherst
UNOFFICIAL RECORD OF VOTES OF THE TOWN COUNCIL
Tuesday, April 14, 2020 6:00 p.m. Virtual Meeting
Councilors Participating Remotely: Bahl-Milne, Brewer, De Angelis, DuMont, Griesemer, Hanneke, Pam,
Ross, Ryan, Schoen, Schreiber, Steinberg, Swartz
Councilors Absent:
School Committee Members Participating Remotely: Demling, Herrington, McDonald, Spitzer
School Committee Members Absent:
Others Participating Remotely: Town Manager Paul Bockelman, Information Technology Director Sean
Hannon, Clerk of the Council Athena O’Keeffe, and School Committee Candidates Gastón de los Reyes, Ryan
Driscoll, Katie Lazdowski, and Heather Lord
Pursuant to Governor Baker’s March 12, 2020 Order Suspending Certain Provisions of the Open Meeting Law,
G.L. c. 30A, §18, this meeting of the Community Resources Committee is being conducted via remote
participation.
President Griesemer called the Council meeting to order at 6:02 p.m.
Chair Spitzer called the School Committee meeting to order at 6:03 p.m.
7. Action Items
a. Joint Meeting with Amherst School Committee to Fill School Committee Vacancy
Joint Council and School Committee Roll Call Vote:
Demling – Heather Lord; Herrington – Katie Lazdowski; McDonald – Heather Lord; Spitzer – Heather
Lord; Bahl-Milne – Heather Lord; Brewer – Heather Lord; De Angelis – Katie Lazdowski; DuMont –
Katie Lazdowski; Griesemer – Heather Lord; Hanneke – Gastón de los Reyes; Pam – Katie Lazdowski;
Ross – Heather Lord; Ryan – Heather Lord; Schoen – Katie Lazdowski; Schreiber – Heather Lord;
Steinberg – Heather Lord; Swartz – Katie Lazdowski
Vote to fill School Committee Vacancy Charter Sec. 4.1(c)
MOVED: To select Heather A. Lord, a registered voter of the Town of Amherst to perform the duties
of a member of the Amherst School Committee until newly elected members of the Amherst School
Committee are sworn in on January 3, 2022.
Motion by: Griesemer
Seconded by: Bahl- Milne
ROLL CALL VOTE: 16-0-1 (McDonald, Demling, Herrington, Spitzer, Bahl-Milne, Brewer, De Angelis,
DuMont, Griesemer, Hanneke, Ross, Ryan, Schoen, Schreiber, Steinberg, and Swartz voted Yes; Councilor Pam
Abstained)
The Council adjourned at 8:11 p.m.
The School Committee adjourned at 8:11 p.m.
