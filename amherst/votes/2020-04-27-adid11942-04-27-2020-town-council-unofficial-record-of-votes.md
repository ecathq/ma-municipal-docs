VOTED VIA CONSENT AGENDA: To grant the following exceptions requested by the Town
Manager on April 23, 2020 due to the extra time needed because of the Governor's declaration of a state
of emergency related to COVID-19:
 In accordance with Charter Section 5.9, to modify the deadline of April 1 established in Charter
Section 5.4(a) for the Amherst School Committee, Regional School Committee, and Library
Trustees to submit their proposed adopted budgets for FY21 to the Town Manager to June 1,
2020.
 In accordance with Charter Section 5.9, to modify the deadline of May 1 established in Charter
Section 5.4(b) for the Town Manager to submit a proposed budget for FY21 to the Town Council
to June 29, 2020.
 In accordance with Charter Section 5.9, to modify the deadline of May 1 established in Charter
Section 5.7(c) for the Town Manager to submit a capital inventory and 5-year capital
improvement program to the Town Council to June 29, 2020.
 To authorize and direct the Town Manager to submit one-month proposed budgets for July 2020
to the Town Council by June 1, 2020.
c. Intermunicipal Agreements
VOTED VIA CONSENT AGENDA: To suspend Town Council Rules of Procedure rule 8.4 for the
current agenda item.
d. Selection of Auditor for Fiscal Year 2021
VOTED VIA CONSENT AGENDA: To suspend Town Council Rules of Procedure rule 8.4 for the
current agenda item.
MOTION: To not engage in an auditor selection process for Fiscal Year 2021 and to continue to use
audit services provided by Melanson Heath, as recommended by the Finance Committee report of April
13, 2020.
Motion by: Griesemer
Seconded by: Hanneke
ROLL CALL VOTE: Unanimous
Bahl-Milne, Brewer, De Angelis, DuMont, Griesemer, Hanneke, Pam, Ross, Ryan, Schoen, Schreiber,
Steinberg, Swartz
