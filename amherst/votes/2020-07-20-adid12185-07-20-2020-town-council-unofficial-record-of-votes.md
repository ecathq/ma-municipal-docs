MOTION: To support and endorse the requests detailed in the Amherst Town Manager's letter dated
July 10, 2020 to Chancellor Subbaswamy of the University of Massachusetts, Amherst, asking for
critical changes to the University's plan for students' return for the fall 2020 semester, and given the
urgency of matter, we call on the University and Chancellor Subbaswamy to partner with the Town to

8. Action Items
a. FY21 Budget
MOTION: That the approval of the FY2021 Operating budget is made with the explicit understanding
with the Town Manager that two upcoming anticipated vacant positions in the Police Department’s
budget not be filled until the Town Manager, in consultation with the Town Council, and Residents of
Amherst, has fully explored alternative options of providing services and presented the results to the
Town Council no later than January 31, 2021.
Motion by: Steinberg
Seconded by: De Angelis
ROLL CALL VOTE: 12-0 (Councilors Bahl-Milne, Brewer, De Angelis, DuMont, Griesemer,
Hanneke, Pam, Ross, Ryan, Schoen, Schreiber, and Steinberg voted Yes; Councilor Swartz was absent)
MOTION: To adopt Appropriation and Transfer Order FY21-04B, “An Order Appropriating the Town
of Amherst FY2021 Operating Budget”, as recommended by the Finance Committee and shown on
pages 7-8 of the document entitled “Town Council Finance Committee Recommendation on Fiscal Year
2021 Budget and additional Orders.” (majority vote)
Motion by: Steinberg
Seconded by: Pam
ROLL CALL VOTE: 12-0 (Councilors Bahl-Milne, Brewer, De Angelis, DuMont, Griesemer,
