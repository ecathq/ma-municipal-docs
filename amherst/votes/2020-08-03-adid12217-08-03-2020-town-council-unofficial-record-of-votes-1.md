8. Action Items
a. Elections
(1) Polling Locations
MOTION: To locate in-person voting on September 1, 2020 and November 3, 2020 for all ten voting
precincts at Amherst-Pelham Regional High School, 21 Mattoon Street, as this will have no disparate
adverse impact on access to the polls on the basis of race, national origin, disability, income or age.
Motion by: Ryan
Seconded by: Hanneke
ROLL CALL VOTE: 6-7 (Councilors Brewer, Hanneke, Ross, Ryan, Schreiber, and Steinberg voted
Yes; Councilors Bahl-Milne, De Angelis, DuMont, Griesemer, Pam, Schoen, and Swartz voted No)
MOTION: To reconsider the last vote under Town Council Rules of Procedure rule 7.5(b).
Motion by: Hanneke
Seconded by: Schreiber
ROLL CALL VOTE: 7-6 (Councilors Brewer, Griesemer, Hanneke, Ross, Ryan, Schreiber, and
Steinberg voted Yes; Councilors Bahl-Milne, De Angelis, DuMont, Pam, Schoen, and Swartz voted No)
