c. Zoning Bylaw 11.250 Amendment – Second Reading, Charter Sec. 2.10(a)
MOTION: To amend Zoning Bylaw 11.250, Planning Board Decision, to delete the phrase “at least two
thirds (2/3), but not fewer than five (5),” and replace it with the words “a majority,” so that the Section
11.250 reads: “The concurring vote of a majority of the members of the Board participating and voting
shall be required for any decision on a site plan application (abstaining members being considered not to
be voting).”
Motion by: Griesemer
Seconded by: Hanneke
ROLL CALL VOTE: 9-4-0 (Councilors Bahl-Milne, Brewer, De Angelis, Griesemer, Hanneke, Ross,
Ryan, Schreiber and Steinberg voted Yes; Councilors DuMont, Pam, Schoen and Swartz voted No)
Bylaw Amendment Passes by required 2/3 of the Full Town Council in favor.
MOTION: Amendment of the original Motion (above) to add the line, “But no fewer than 4 members”
after the words “a majority”.
Motion by: Schoen
Seconded by: Pam
