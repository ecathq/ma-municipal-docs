a. Valley CDC Project at 132 Northampton Road – Local Option
MOTION: To authorize President Griesemer to sign the letter to the Zoning Board of Appeals
regarding the implementation of a local preference titled “Draft Letter re Local Preference at 132

from the Subsidizing Agency to implement a local selection preference with the allowable categories of
