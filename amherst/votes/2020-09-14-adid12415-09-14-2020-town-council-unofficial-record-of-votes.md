Ryan, Schoen, Schreiber, Steinberg, Swartz

G.L. c. 30A, §18, this meeting of the Town Council is being conducted via remote participation.
All votes will be taken by roll call.
Per MGL Chapter 30A Section 20(f), this meeting is being recorded and broadcast by Amherst Media.

MOTION: to amend the letter by removing the second sentence from the sixth paragraph that reads: “In
recent weeks, it has become clear that the administration is seeking to layoff 300 PSU members, 150
USA members, AFSCE members, and temporary workers at the University.”
Motion by: Ryan
Seconded by: De Angelis
ROLL CALL VOTE: 8-3 (Councilors Bahl-Milne, Brewer, De Angelis, Griesemer, Hanneke, Ryan,
Schoen, and Steinberg voted Yes; Councilors DuMont, Pam, and Swartz voted No; Councilors Ross and
Schreiber were absent)
MOTION: to amend the letter by removing the language in strikethrough and adding the language in
bold: “The details -- and how much of tThe impact will be borne in communities adjacent to UMass
Amherst or elsewhere in the Commonwealth – and will depend on the specific details of the
reduction…” so that the sentence reads:
“The impact will be borne in communities adjacent to UMass Amherst or elsewhere in the
Commonwealth – and will depend on the specific details of the reduction in contracting with vendors,
for example, the reduction in wholesale food orders placed by dining services, and on the share of lost
wages and salaries that is offset by Unemployment Insurance and other state and local support
resources.”
Motion by: De Angelis
Seconded by: Schoen
ROLL CALL VOTE: 11-0 (Councilors Bahl-Milne, Brewer, De Angelis, DuMont, Griesemer,
Hanneke, Pam, Ryan, Schoen, Steinberg, and Swartz voted Yes; Councilors Ross and Schreiber were
absent)
MOTION: to amend the letter by removing the following language:
“These employee-initiated proposals have included:
1. 3 or 4 day works weeks with WorKShare, a separation incentive program, and cuts to hours , but not
full furloughs;
2. Sharing the burden and economic pain equally across the unions’ membership with up to two five-
day furloughs – one in October and one in December if the Administration’s cost savings targets are
not met;
3. Eliminating temporary positions, as long established in employee contracts, before imposing any
cuts to permanent positions;
4. Providing more information on the University’s finances and, working with unions to use that data to
carefully structure reductions in hours; and
5. Creatively incorporating University employees in projects like the Ambassador program and in
collaboration with groups like the Amherst Survival Center and the Amherst Mobile Market that
support people in need throughout the Town of Amherst.”
Motion by: Hanneke
Seconded by: Ryan
ROLL CALL VOTE: 10-1 (Councilors Bahl-Milne, Brewer, De Angelis, DuMont, Griesemer,
Hanneke, Ryan, Schoen, Steinberg, and Swartz voted Yes; Councilor Pam voted No; Councilors Ross
and Schreiber were absent)
