MOTION: To acknowledge receipt of Climate Action, Adaptation, and Resilience Plan,

Manger” with the following: “to update the Town Council quarterly regarding achieving
the Climate Action Goals adopted by the Town Council in 2019.”
Motion by: DuMont
Seconded by: Schoen
ROLL CALL VOTE: 10-2, with 1 absent (Bahl-Milne, Brewer, DuMont, Griesemer,
Pam, Ross, Ryan, Schoen, Schreiber, & Steinberg voted Yes; De Angelis & Hanneke
voted No; Swartz was absent)
MOTION: To ask the Town Manager to work with ECAC in the development of their
annual report as described in their charge adopted by the Town Council on February 11,
2019 (revised August 2, 2021) including Progress towards climate action goals; Measures
taken to reduce emissions and build resilience; Evaluation of the effectiveness and
implementation of these measures; Funding needed to enable initiatives recommended by
the ECAC; and Community engagement.
Motion by: Griesemer
Seconded by: De Angelis
