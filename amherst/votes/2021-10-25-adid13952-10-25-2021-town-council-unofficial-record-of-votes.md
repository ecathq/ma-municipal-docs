Town of Amherst
UNOFFICIAL RECORD OF VOTES OF THE TOWN COUNCIL
Monday, October 25, 2021 6:30 p.m. Virtual Meeting
Councilors Participating Remotely: Bahl-Milne, Brewer, De Angelis, DuMont, Griesemer,
Hanneke, Pam, Ross, Ryan, Schoen, and Steinberg
Councilors Absent: Schreiber and Swartz
Others Participating Remotely: Town Manager Paul Bockelman, Clerk of the Council Athena
O’Keeffe
Community Safety Working Group Members Participating Remotely: Russ Vernon-Jones,
Debora Ferreira, Pat Ononibaku, Brianna Owen, Ellisha Walker, Susan Audette, and LEAP
consultant Lisa Tennenbaum
Districting Advisory Board Members Participating Remotely: Dee Shabazz, Peggy Shannon,
Tracy Zafian, Susan Audette, and Irene Dujovne
When remote participation is used, all votes will be taken by roll call.
Per MGL Chapter 30A Section 20(f), this meeting is being recorded and broadcast by Amherst
Media.
President Griesemer called the Town Council meeting to order at 6:32 pm; adjourned at 9:39 pm
CSWG call to order at 6:34 pm; adjourned at 8:58 pm
8. Action Items
a. Community Safety Working Group Final Report
MOTION: To acknowledge receipt of Community Safety Working Group (CSWG)
Report Part B and to thank the Committee, Town Staff and many others who have
participated in this effort for their thoughtful and thorough work.
Motion by: Griesemer
Seconded by: De Angelis
ROLL CALL VOTE: 11-0, with 2 absent (Bahl-Milne, Brewer, De Angelis, DuMont,
Griesemer, Hanneke, Pam, Ross, Ryan, Schoen, Schreiber, and Steinberg voted Yes;
Schreiber and Swartz were absent)
b. Redistricting
MOVED: "To approve the Districting Plan for Five Districts (wards), with two Voting
Precincts in each District, as recommended by the Districting Advisory Board and
represented on a map/plan entitled, “DAB Endorsed Proposed Districts & Precincts Map
Final Version” dated 10/20/2021 and attached to the Motion Sheet on page 2."
Motion by: Griesemer
Seconded by: De Angelis
ROLL CALL VOTE: 10-1, with 2 absent (Bahl-Milne, Brewer, De Angelis, DuMont,
Griesemer, Pam, Ross, Ryan, Schoen, Steinberg voted Yes; Hanneke voted No; Schreiber
and Swartz were absent)
