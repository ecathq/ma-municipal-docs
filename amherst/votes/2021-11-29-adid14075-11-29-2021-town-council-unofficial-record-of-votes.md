(2) Parking Regulations on Kendrick Place
MOVED: In accordance with General Bylaw 3.14 and the Town Council Policy
Regarding the Control and Regulation of the Public Ways, to approve the following
parking regulations on Kendrick Place:
• Parking prohibited as a tow zone, on the east side of Kendrick Place, from
Northampton Road to end of street.
• Parking prohibited as a tow zone, on the west side of Kendrick Place, for a
distance of 30 feet north of Northampton Road.
• Parking permitted, on the west side of Kendrick Place, from a point 30 ft. from
Northampton Road northerly to the end of Kendrick Place.
Motion by: Griesemer
Seconded by: Ross
ROLL CALL VOTE: 12-0, with 1 absent (Brewer, De Angelis, DuMont, Griesemer,
Hanneke, Pam, Ross, Ryan, Schoen, Schreiber, Steinberg, Swartz voted Yes; Bahl-Milne
was absent)
