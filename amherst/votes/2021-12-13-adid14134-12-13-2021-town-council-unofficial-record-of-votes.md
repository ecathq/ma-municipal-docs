b. Public Ways Request: North Pleasant Street between Triangle and Hallock Streets:
Traffic Direction, Parking, and Pedestrian Improvements along Kendrick Park
MOVED: To approve the following changes to parking and permanent changes to the
public way on North Pleasant St from Hallock St to Triangle St:
• Conversion of traffic flow to one-way traffic northbound from McLellan St to
Triangle St
• Creation of back-in angled parking spaces on the eastern side of North Pleasant St
• Parking prohibited as a tow zone on the western side of North Pleasant St
• Removal of the grass belt on the western side
With the following conditions:
• Complete streets approach is applied to the final design to maximize bicycle and
pedestrian safety and use, including consideration of a separate southbound lane
for cyclists either at street or sidewalk level.
• DAAC will be consulted on the number and placement of accessible spaces.
• The Town will return to the Town Council for approval of parking regulation of
new spaces, including a public hearing on the mix of metered and permit parking
and meter regulations.
Motion by: Griesemer
Seconded by: Ross
ROLL CALL VOTE: 10-0, with 3 absent (Bahl-Milne, De Angelis, Griesemer,
Hanneke, Pam, Ross, Ryan, Schoen, Schreiber, and Steinberg voted Yes; Brewer,
DuMont, and Swartz were absent)

Amendment to
Employment Agreement Between
Town of Amherst, Massachusetts
and
Paul Bockelman
This amendment to the employment agreement is made and entered into on this this ___ day of
December 2021 by and between the Town of Amherst, Massachusetts, a municipal corporation,
hereinafter called the “Council” acting by and through its Town Council “Council” and Paul
Bockelman, hereinafter called “Bockelman” or “Town Manager”.
WHEREAS, there is an employment agreement (“Agreement”) between the Council and Town
Manager dated September 9, 2019; and
WHEREAS, the Town seeks to extend the length of the Agreement under the same terms and
conditions of the Agreement; and
WHEREAS, Bockelman is willing to extend the length of the Agreement and is able to perform
the duties described in the Agreement;
NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants contained in the Agreement, the
Council and Town Manager agree to amend the Agreement by revising Sections 2.1 and 2.2 as
follows:
A. Delete Section 2.1 and replace it with the following:
2.1 The term of this employment agreement shall be from August 22, 2019 and ending on
August 31, 2025.
B. Delete Section 2.2 and replace it with the following:
2.2 Should the Town Council decide not to renew this Agreement at the end of the term
(August 31, 2025), the Council shall provide the Town Manager with a written notice of its
intent not to renew no later than June 3, 2025.
