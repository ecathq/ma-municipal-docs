c. African Heritage Reparation Assembly: Request for Proposed Legislation
MOVED: To ask the Town Manager to begin the process of seeking special legislation to
define reparations as a public purpose as described in the K.P. Law memo dated
September 28, 2021 and included in the March 7, 2022 Town Council meeting packet.
The Town Manager will seek guidance, as needed, from the African Heritage Reparation
Assembly (AHRA) while drafting the petition and will provide a copy of the proposed
petition to the AHRA for review prior to submitting it to the Town Council for approval to
file with the State Legislature.
Motion by: Griesemer
Seconded by: Miller
ROLL CALL VOTE: 12-0, with 1 absent (Bahl Milne)

MOVED: To refer the proposed Revision of Fees under General Bylaw 3.50 – Residential

Motion by: Griesemer
Seconded by: Devlin Gauthier

e. Town Council Meeting Format
MOVED: To suspend Town Council Rules of Procedure rule 8.4 for the current agenda
item.
Motion by: Griesemer
Seconded by: Devlin Gauthier
ROLL CALL VOTE: 10-1-1, with 1 absent (De Angelis, Devlin Gauthier, Griesemer,
Lopes, Miller, Pam, Rooney, Schoen, Taub, Walker voted Yes; Hanneke voted No; Steinberg
Abstained; Bahl-Milne was absent)
MOVED: Effective immediately, to hold all Town Council meetings in the in-person
meeting format until July 15, 2022, with the option of virtual meetings if the President
declares 24 hours in advance of a meeting that there will be fewer than 5 Councilors
physically present, and with virtual public access until July 15, 2022.
Motion by: Griesemer
Seconded by: Schoen
MOVED: to postpone the motion until the regular Council meeting on March 21, 2022.
Motion by: Hanneke
Seconded by: Devlin Gauthier
ROLL CALL VOTE: 9-0-3, with 1 absent (De Angelis, Devlin Gauthier, Griesemer,
Hanneke, Lopes, Miller, Rooney, Taub, Walker voted Yes; Pam, Schoen, and Steinberg
Abstained; Bahl-Milne was absent)
