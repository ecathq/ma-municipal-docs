h. Town Council Meeting Format
Motion Postponed on March 7, 2022
MOTION ON THE FLOOR: Effective immediately, to hold all Town Council
meetings in the in-person meeting format until July 15, 2022, with the option of virtual
meetings if the President declares 24 hours in advance of a meeting that there will be
fewer than 5 Councilors physically present, and with virtual public access until July 15,
2022.
Motion by: Griesemer
Seconded by: Schoen
ROLL CALL VOTE: 12-0, with 1 abstention (Bahl-Milne, De Angelis, Devlin
Gauthier, Griesemer, Hanneke, Lopes, Miller, Pam, Rooney, Schoen, Taub, and Walker
voted Yes; Steinberg Abstained)
MOVED: Effective immediately, to hold all Town Council committee meetings in the
virtual meeting format with virtual public access until July 15, 2022.
