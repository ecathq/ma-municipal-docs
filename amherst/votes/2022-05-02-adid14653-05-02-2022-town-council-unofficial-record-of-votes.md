d. Establishment of Rental Registration, Permit, and Inspection Fees in accordance
with General Bylaw 3.50 – Residential Rental Property
MOVED: In accordance with General Bylaw 3.50, to adopt the following fees for
registration, permit application, and inspections:
A. Fee for Registering and Obtaining a Rental Permit in accordance with the Bylaw:
1. For a parcel with a maximum of six units with at least one of the units
Occupied by the Owner as a Principal Residence: $100
2. For all other parcels: $250
B. Fee for Inspections by the Code Official: $150 per inspection
Motion by: Griesemer
Seconded by: Devlin Gauthier
ROLL CALL VOTE: 12-0 with 1 absent (Lopes)
