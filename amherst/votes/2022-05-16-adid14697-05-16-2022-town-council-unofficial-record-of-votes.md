d. Proposed Permanent and Interim Parking Regulations on North Pleasant Street
from Triangle to Hallock Street
MOVED: On an interim basis, to implement one-way northbound traffic on North
Pleasant Street from McClellan Street to Triangle Street, to move parking spaces to the
east side of the street, that twenty spaces near the park be metered parking with a 2-hour
limit from 8 am to 6 pm, Monday through Saturday, at a rate of 50 cents/hour, with three
of those spaces designated handicapped; and to permanently implement the North Pleasant
on-street parking plan as follows:
• 1 parking space for handicapped parking and designed for a van.
• 2 other parking spaces for handicapped parking.
• 7 back-in parking spaces for permit parking enforced Monday through Friday,
September 1 to April 30, from 8:00 a.m. – 5:00 p.m.
• 3 parallel parking spaces for permit parking enforced Monday through Friday from
8:00 a.m. – 5:00 p.m.
• 20 back-in parking spaces for metered parking at 50 cents/hour with a two-hour
parking limit enforced Monday through Saturday from 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m.
Postponed to a future agenda

An Order Approving the Acceptance of Optional Tax Exemptions for FY2023 (majority

tax exemptions to qualifying property owners than the base levels set by the State.

additional exemption of up to 100 percent for taxpayers qualifying for exemption under M.G.L.
