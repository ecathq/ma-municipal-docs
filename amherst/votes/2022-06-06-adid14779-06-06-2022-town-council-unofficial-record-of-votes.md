MOVED: To refer to the Town Services and Outreach Committee consideration of
implementing parking regulations on the east side of Lincoln Avenue between Amity and
McClellan Streets.
Motion by: Devlin Gauthier
Seconded by: Rooney
ROLL CALL VOTE: 10-2-1 abstention (Bahl-Milne, Devlin Gauthier, Griesemer, Miller,
Pam, Rooney, Schoen, Steinberg, Taub, and Walker voted Yes; Hanneke and De Angelis voted
No; Lopes Abstained)
g. Public Way: Proposed Permanent and Interim Parking Regulations on North
Pleasant Street from Triangle to Hallock Street
MOVED: On an interim basis, to implement one-way northbound traffic on North
Pleasant Street from McClellan Street to Triangle Street, to move parking spaces to the
east side of the street, that twenty spaces near the park be metered parking with a 2-hour
limit from 8 am to 6 pm, Monday through Saturday, at a rate of 50 cents/hour, with three
of those spaces designated handicapped; and to permanently implement the North
Pleasant on-street parking plan as follows:
• 1 parking space for handicapped parking and designed for a van.
• 2 other parking spaces for handicapped parking.
• 7 back-in parking spaces for dual use as both permit parking enforced Monday
through Friday, September to May, from 8:00 a.m. – 5:00 p.m. and metered parking
at 50 cents/hour with a two-hour parking limit enforced Monday through Saturday
from 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m.
• 3 parallel parking spaces for dual use as both permit parking enforced Monday
through Friday, September to May, from 8:00 a.m. – 5:00 p.m. and metered parking
at 50 cents/hour with a two-hour parking limit enforced Monday through Saturday
from 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m.
• 20 back-in parking spaces for metered parking at 50 cents/hour with a two-hour
parking limit enforced Monday through Saturday from 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m.
Motion by: Griesemer
Seconded by: Hanneke
ROLL CALL VOTE: 12-1 (Bahl-Milne, De Angelis, Devlin Gauthier, Griesemer, Hanneke,
Lopes, Miller, Pam, Rooney, Schoen, Taub, and Walker voted Yes; Steinberg voted No)
h. Public Way: Pioneer Valley Driving School Request for Long-Term Use of Two
Parallel Parking Spaces on North Pleasant Street
VOTED VIA CONSENT AGENDA: To authorize the long-term, temporary use of the
public way of one parking space on North Pleasant Street near the intersection with
McClellan Street as follows:
• One metered parking space;
• Two Saturdays per month (exact days and dates to be determined with the Town
Manager’s office);
• 8:00 a.m. – 5:00 p.m.;
• June 6, 2022-June 30, 2023 (estimated to be 26 days);
• $10/day/parking space.
