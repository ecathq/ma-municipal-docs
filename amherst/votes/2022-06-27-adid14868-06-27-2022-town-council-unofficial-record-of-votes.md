c. Cannabis Tax Revenue for Reparations
MOVED: That each year upon certification of free cash by the Commonwealth of
Massachusetts (October), and upon review and recommendation by the Town’s Finance
Committee based on the Town’s overall financial health at that time, that the Town
Council add an amount equal to the certified cannabis tax revenue for the previous year
(approximately July) to the Stabilization fund established for the purpose of reparations
until such time as the fund equals $1M.
As amended by changing $1M to $2M, to add the phrase "but not to exceed $205,000"
after the phrase "that the Town Council add an amount equal to the certified cannabis tax
revenue for the previous year (approximately July)," and change the word “fund” to
“Town’s contribution.”
Motion by: Griesemer
Seconded by: Steinberg
ROLL CALL VOTE: 10-2, with 1 absent (Bahl-Milne, De Angelis, Devlin Gauthier,
Griesemer, Lopes, Miller, Pam, Steinberg, Taub, and Walker voted Yes; Hanneke and
Schoen voted No; Rooney was Absent)

CAPITAL IMPROVEMENT PROGRAM APPROPRIATIONS:
APPROPRIATION, TRANSFER and DEDICATION ORDER – FY22 – 05F (2/3rds vote)
An Order Authorizing and Appropriating funds for the Capital Improvement of the North
Common.
To approve the North Common Project for the amount of $1,813,214, to be funded by
Community Preservation Act Funds in the amount of $986,148 as the Town’s grant match to the
project, and accept the Land and Water Conservation Grant (LWCF) in the amount of $827,066.
BE IT ORDERED by the Town Council of the Town of Amherst That:
The Town appropriate the sum of $986,148 for the cost of preserving and rehabilitating the
Project Area of the Town Common and any costs related or incidental thereto, and to meet
such appropriation, transfer $986,148 from Community Preservation Fund Capital funds
previously voted for this purpose as stated in chart below, and to authorize the Town
Manager or his designee to apply for and accept on behalf of the Town, funds granted under
the LWCF Act (P.L. 88-578, 78 Stat 897) for $827,066, and authorize the Treasurer to issue
a Grant Anticipation Note (GAN) under MGL, Ch44, Section 6A, and to accept any other
funds, gifts, grants, under any federal and/or other state program in any way connected with
the scope of this this article, and enter into all agreements and execute any and all
instruments as may be necessary or convenient to effectuate the foregoing project.
