COMMUNITY PRESERVATION ACT APPROPRIATIONS
APPROPRIATION and BORROWING AUTHORIZATION ORDER FY23-08A
Town of Amherst Community Preservation Act
An Order Appropriating and Authorizing Debt for the Rehabilitation of and Improvements to the
Amherst-Pelham Regional School District’s High School Track and Field Facilities under Open
Space/Recreation (2/3 vote required)
The Community Preservation Act Committee has recommended to Authorize Debt by vote of 8-0-0 for
the Rehabilitation of and Improvements to the Amherst-Pelham Regional School District’s High School
Track and Field Facilities, for which future Debt Service will be paid using Community Preservation Act
funds; this order is contingent upon “Option 3,” as presented to the CPAC during its meeting of June 2,
2022, if other planned funding does not reach the “Option 3” target and the track cannot be
reoriented, the borrowing of CPAC funds will be rescinded,
NOW THEREFORE BE IT ORDERED by the Town Council of the Town of Amherst that:
The sum of $800,000 is appropriated under G.L. c. 44B (also known as the Community
Preservation Act), to fund a grant for the purpose of supporting the rehabilitation of and
improvements to the Amherst-Pelham Regional School District’s High School track and field
facilities, including the payment of all costs incidental and related thereto, and to meet such
appropriation the Treasurer, with the approval of the Town Manager, is authorized to borrow said
amount under G.L. c. 44B, §11, or pursuant to any other enabling authority, and to issue bonds or
notes of the Town therefor, and
IT IS FURTHER ORDERED that the Town Manager is authorized to enter into an intermunicipal
agreement as permitted under G.L. c. 40, §4A, between the Town and the Amherst-Pelham
Regional School District as may, in the opinion of bond counsel to the Town, be required in order
to carry out the purposes of this Order

Community Preservation Act Committee (CPAC) Special
Report to the Town Council for FY2023
The Community Preservation Act Committee is pleased to present this report to the Town Council
outlining a special borrowing article we are recommending for Fiscal Year 2023. The Committee has
reviewed the revised High School Track proposal submitted by the Amherst-Pelham Regional School
District this spring and offers its unanimous support (8-0 with one vacancy).
Background and Rationale
The Regional School District (RSD) submitted its original proposal for track replacement last fall. That
application proposed to reconstruct the track in its current orientation. Because an earlier study of the
High School fields and nearby Town-owned fields (not solely the track) envisioned reorienting the track
to allow other improvements, the Committee was unwilling to approve the project at that time and
requested additional information. The RSD then designed funding programs for two options (one that
reorients the track and one that does not), and its plan was approved by the Regional School Committee
with a funding deadline of January 2023. With the authorization of Town Council, the Town has already
provided for a borrowing of $1.5 million, which the Committee was told would be adequate (that is,
without more CPAC funds) to rebuild the track in its current orientation. The borrowing article that we
are recommending is specifically for the option that reorients the track and makes other improvements
to the recreational facilities. If sufficient funds for that project are not secured, CPAC funds will not be
used.
The proposal to reorient and reconstruct the track and associated fields and event spaces meets CPAC
goals for projects that are broadly supported across the community, Town departments, and the public,
addresses documented needs for recreation. Please note that CPA monies cannot be used for artificial
turf fields or most permanent structures such as bleachers.
Project Details- Recreation
Amherst-Pelham Regional School District: Track replacement “Option 3,” as presented to CPAC during
its meeting of June 2, 2022 - borrowing authorization of $800,000. If other planned funding does not
reach the Option 3 target and the track cannot be reoriented, the borrowing of CPAC funds will be
rescinded.
