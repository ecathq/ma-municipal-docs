b. Universal Refuse, Recycling, and Composting
MOVED: To refer the proposed amendment to General Bylaw 3.3, Refuse Collection
and Recyclable Materials, presented by Councilors Bahl-Milne, Steinberg, Taub, and
Walker, dated August 10, 2022, to the Town Services and Outreach Committee, and in
consultation with the Finance Committee to review the proposed revision and make
recommendation of a measure to the Council in 90 days and to refer to the Governance,
Organization, and Legislation Committee for a review of clarity, consistency, and
actionability.
Motion by: Griesemer
Seconded by: Bahl-Milne

c. Public Ways: Proposed Street Lighting Policy
MOVED: To refer the recommended rescission of the current streetlights policy and the
proposed replacement street lighting policy, presented by Councilors Devlin Gauthier and
Hanneke, dated August 10, 2022 to the Town Services and Outreach Committee, and in
consultation with the Finance Committee for review and recommendation to the Council in 90
days, and to refer to the Governance, Organization, and Legislation Committee for a review of
clarity, consistency, and actionability.
Motion by: Griesemer
Seconded by: De Angelis
ROLL CALL VOTE: 9-3 with 1 absent (De Angelis, Devlin Gauthier, Griesemer,
Hanneke, Miller, Pam, Rooney, Steinberg, and Walker voted Yes; Bahl-Milne, Schoen,
