a. Puerto Rican Heritage Day Proclamation

8. Action Items
a. Proposed Special Act on Residential Property Transfer Fees
MOVED: To refer the proposed Special Act on Residential Property Transfer Fees to the
Finance Committee and the Governance, Organization, and Legislation Committee, for
review and recommendation to the Town Council by October 27.
Motion by: Griesemer
Seconded by: Hanneke
ROLL CALL VOTE: Unanimous
b. Acceptance of Public Way: Kestrel Lane and Hop Brook Road
MOVED: To express the Town Council’s intent to accept as public the roadways and
easements of The Meadows at Amherst Fields subdivision, as shown on the Huntley
subdivision plans dated February 24, 1995.
Motion by: Griesemer
Seconded by: Hanneke
ROLL CALL VOTE: Unanimous
MOVED: To refer the proposed acceptance of the roadways and easements of The
Meadows at Amherst Fields subdivision, as shown on the Huntley subdivision plans
dated February 24, 1995, to the Planning Board and Finance Committee, for review and
recommendation to the Town Council by October 27.
Motion by: Griesemer
Seconded by: Hanneke
ROLL CALL VOTE: Unanimous
c. Referral of General Bylaw 3.40: Snow and Ice Removal – Consent Agenda
VOTED VIA CONSENT AGENDA: To refer General Bylaw 3.40: Snow and Ice to the

recommendation on potential revisions by October 27.

e. Proposed Amendments to General Bylaws 3.8, License Fees: Innholders, Common

VOTED VIA CONSENT AGENDA: To amend General Bylaw 3.8, License Fees:
Inholders, Common Victuallers, and Lunch Carts, by inserting the language shown in bold
red font, and removing the language shown in red strikethrough font, as follows:
FEES FOR INNHOLDERS, COMMON VICTUALLERS, AND LUNCH CARTS
Fee for innholder license: $50.00
Fee for common victuallers license: $50.00
Fee for lunch carts license: $100.00
The fees for innholders, common victuallers, and lunch carts are as indicated in the above
data block shall be set by the Board of License Commissioners in accordance with
M.G.L. c. 40 Sec. 22F.
f. Proposed Rescission of General Bylaw 3.23, Peeking or Peering into the Place of
Habitation – Second Reading – Consent Agenda
VOTED VIA CONSENT AGENDA: To rescind General Bylaw 3.23, Peeking or
Peering into the Place of Habitation, in its entirety.
g. Town Council and Council Committee Meeting Format
Options:
MOVED: Effective immediately and until and including the first meeting of the next
Council’s term, to hold all Town Council meetings in the in-person meeting format with
remote participation as permitted by law, with in-person public access and the option of
virtual public access; in the event of inclement weather or at the discretion of the
President, the President may announce a virtual meeting will take place.
Motion by: Griesemer
Seconded by: Hanneke
ROLL CALL VOTE: Unanimous
MOVED: Effective immediately, and until and including the first meeting of the next
Council’s term, unless inconsistent with state law, to hold all Town Council committee
meetings in the virtual meeting format with virtual public access.
Motion by: Griesemer
Seconded by: Rooney
ROLL CALL VOTE: Unanimous
