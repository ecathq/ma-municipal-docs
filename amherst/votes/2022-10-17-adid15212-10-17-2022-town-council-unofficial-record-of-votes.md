APPROPRIATION and TRANSFER ORDER FY23-12A
Town of Amherst Free Cash and Stabilization Fund
An Order Appropriating from Free Cash to the Stabilization funds (Majority Vote)
The Policy on reserves states that if Free Cash exceeds 5% of operating revenue, the excess may
be appropriated and transferred to a Stabilization Fund; the State has certified the Town’s Free
Cash for the fiscal year ending June 30, 2022, at $8,207,716,
BE IT ORDERED by the Town Council of the Town of Amherst that: The Town appropriate and Transfer
the sum of $1,812,450 from Free Cash in the Undesignated Fund Balance of the General Fund to the
Stabilization Funds as follows;
$1,678,120 to be transferred to the Capital Stabilization Fund
$134,330 to be transferred to the Reparations Stabilization Fund
I hereby certify that at a regular meeting of the Town Council for which a quorum was present, the
above Order was adopted by a vote of for, against and present on
, 2022.
