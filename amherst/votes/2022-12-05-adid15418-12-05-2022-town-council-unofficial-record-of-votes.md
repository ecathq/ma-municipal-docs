AMHERST PELHAM REGIONAL HIGH SCHOOL ATHLETIC COMPLEX, as
presented.
Motion by: Griesemer
Seconded by: Miller
Postponed by Bahl-Milne via Charter sec. 2.10(c) to the next regular or special Town
Council meeting.
MOTION: To waive Town Council Rules of Procedure rule 8.6 for the current agenda
item.
Motion by: Hanneke
Seconded by: De Angelis
ROLL CALL VOTE: 11-2
Yea: De Angelis, Devlin Gauthier, Griesemer, Hanneke, Lopes, Miller, Pam, Schoen,

Nay: Bahl-Milne, Rooney
Present (abstain): None
Absent: None
MOTION:
1. That the Town issue a statement to the SC and students indicating our
commitment to addressing the entire athletic complex.
2. Refer this motion immediately to the finance committee to determine:
a). A phased approach to ensure the repair and maintenance of all fields located
at the middle and high school
b). Allocate/identify funding within the FY24 budget to commence the repair of at
least one of the athletic fields located at the high school
c). Funding required for the town to hire someone to oversee the ongoing
maintenance of these and all town (owned) fields. This person would coordinate
with key stakeholders including but not limited to DPW workers, the Athletic
Director and the Rec department.
Motion by: Walker
