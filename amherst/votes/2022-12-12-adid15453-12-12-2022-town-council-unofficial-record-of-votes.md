Amendment to
Employment Agreement Between
Town of Amherst, Massachusetts
and
Paul Bockelman
This amendment to the employment agreement is made and entered into on this 12th day of
December, 2022 by and between the Town of Amherst, Massachusetts, a municipal
corporation, hereinafter called the "Council" acting by and through its Town Council
"Council" and Paul Bockelman, hereinafter called "Bockelman" or "Town Manager"
WHEREAS, there is an employment agreement ("Agreement") between the Council and
Town Manager dated September 9, 2019; and
WHEREAS, based upon a vote of the Town Council on December 13, 2021, with agreement
of the Town Manager, the Town amended the Employment Agreement extending the length
of the Agreement under the same terms and conditions of the Agreement to August 31, 2025;
and
WHEREAS, based upon a vote of the Town Council on December 12, 2022, with agreement
of the Town Manager, the Town is amending the Employment Agreement with respect to
$3,000 presently provided toward disability insurance.
NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants contained in the Agreement,
the Council and Town Manager agree to amend the Agreement by revising Sections 11.1 as
follows:
A. Delete Section 11.1 and replace it with the following:
11.1 The Town shall reimburse the Town Manager annually up to $3,000
toward the cost of a disability or life insurance policy, as selected by the Town
Manager. In filing for this reimbursement, the Town Manger shall provide a
copy of the relevant insurance policy, Coverage Selections Page and/or
Decoration Page, and evidence of his payment for disability or life insurance
premium.
