HICKORY RIDGE
CAPITAL IMPROVEMENT PROGRAM:
CO FY23 – 20A HICKORY RIDGE PROPERTY
AN APPROPRIATION AND TRANSFER ORDER AUTHORIZING THE DEDICATION
OF A PORTION OF THE HICKORY RIDGE PROPERTY FOR PARK AND
RECREATION PURPOSES AND APPROPRIATE FUNDS FOR THE REHABILIATION
AND PRESERVATION THEREOF
Hickory Ridge, located on West Pomeroy Lane is a community-wide asset and the preservation
and improvement of this property is a priority for the Town of Amherst; and the Town wishes to
appropriate funds to defray the cost of the preservation and rehabilitation of Hickory Ridge.
The Town applied to the Commonwealth of Massachusetts, for a grant under the Parkland
Acquisitions and Renovations for Communities (PARC) Grant Program and as a condition of
said grant, the Town wishes to dedicate that portion of Hickory Ridge for park and recreation
purposes in perpetuity and to transfer custody thereof to the Recreation Commission for said
purposes.
NOW THEREFORE BE IT ORDERED by the Town Council of the Town of Amherst that:
(a) the care, custody and control of a portion of Hickory Ridge, consisting of the parcel
of land on West Pomeroy Lane shown on Assessors Map 19D, Parcel 10 and
containing 17.00 acres, more or less, is transferred from the Town Manager to the
Recreation Commission for park and recreational purposes, and to dedicate said
parcel, as shown in the attached plan, in perpetuity to said purposes, under the
provisions of G.L. c. 45, §3 and other Massachusetts statutes related to recreation;
(b) that the Town appropriate and transfer the sum of $400,000, for the purpose of
preserving rehabilitating and preserving Hickory Ridge and any costs related or
incidental thereto, and to meet such appropriation, transfer $120,000 from Council
Order 23-07A, a Community Preservation Act appropriation, and to further authorize
the Town Manager and/or the Recreation Commission or their designee, to apply for
and accept on behalf of the Town funds granted under the PARC Grant Program in
the amount of $280,000, and/or any other funds, gifts, grants, under any federal
and/or other state program in any way connected with the scope of this this article,
and enter into all agreements and execute any and all instruments as may be necessary
or convenient to effectuate the foregoing project, and further authorize the Treasurer,
with the approval of the Town Manager, to borrow said sum under G.L. c. 44, §6A,
Grant Anticipation Note, provided, however, that no funds shall be expended until the
Town has received a grant commitment in the amount of $280,000.00 under the
PARC Grant Program (301 CMR 5.00);
