Surveillance Use Policy
Police Department - In-cruiser Video and Audio
Introduction:
The Police Department maintains a policy (Policy #18: In-cruiser Video and
Audio Recorder) for the use of the In-cruiser Video and Audio system which is on
the Police Department’s website and can be found here: In-cruiser Video and
Audio Recorder. Policy #18 may be updated based on changes to State law or
Accreditation standards. This Surveillance Use Policy shall be reviewed from
time-to-time to ensure this policy is in alignment with the Police Department’s
policy.
A. Purpose:
It is the policy of the Amherst Police Department to video and audio record traffic
stops, enforcement activiteis and special events, whenever a camera is available
and/or the conditions warrant such action {41.3.8a}.
b. Authorized Use:
The use of the In-cruiser Video and Audio system shall comply Amherst Police
Department Policy #18 which can be found here: In-cruiser Video and Audio
c. Data Collection:
For police vehicles that are equipped with an In-cruiser Video and Audio system,
all traffic stops are video and audio recorded whenever a camera is available
and/or the conditions warrant such action.
d. Data Access:
Officers must use their unique password to gain entry into the system at the
beginning of their shifts. Only that officer has access to their individual system as
outlined in Amherst Police Department Policy #18.
e. Data Protection:
The Town’s standard firewall protections and wireless download protections
apply to the handling of In-cruiser Video and Audio recordings.
f. Digital Video Storage and Retention:
Digital Video will be retained on the in-car video computer server located in a
secure area on the third floor of the police department.
Video not requested for evidentiary purposes will be overwritten after seven (7)
calendar days.
If video is requested for evidentiary purposes, officers shall request the Court Liaison Officer to
burn to DVD any recording they wish to save as evidence. Video that is recorded to DVD to be
saved for evidence shall be kept in property and evidence storage. The officer submitting the
DVD for evidence shall completely fill out an evidence tag and submit it in accordance with the

Department’s Property and Evidence policy. DVDs shall be kept securely as evidence until the
criminal case is adjudicated or, if no criminal prosecution occurred, until the records retention
schedule has been met. All recordings will be retained following the schedule outlined in the
Police Department’s Policy #18.
g. Public Access:
All recordings recorded and retained within the timeframe of this policy are
subject to the State’s Public Records laws. Requests for copies will be addressed
under the terms and conditions of the State’s Public Records laws.
h. Third-Party Data-Sharing:
Video and Audio recordings may be shared with the State District Attorney’s or
Attorney General’s offices upon request.
i. Maintenance:
No third-party storage or maintenance.
j. Training:
All officers are trained in the use of the In-cruiser Video and Audio system during
their initial Field Training and refreshers are given as needed.
k. Complaints:
Complaints may be submitted to the Amherst Police Department, Town
Manager’s office, Human Rights Commission, and the Diversity, Equity, and
Inclusion (DEI) Department.
l. Auditing and Oversight:
The In-cruiser Video and Audio system operates in compliance with the Mass
Accreditation Commission Standards. All use, data collection, and trainings must
meet and follow the Amherst Police Policy including annual review by an
Amherst Police Supervisor and every third-year review by the Massachusetts
Accreditation Commission Audit.
