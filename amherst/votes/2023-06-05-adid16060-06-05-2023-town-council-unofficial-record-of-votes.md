d. Council Order FY24-10 An Order Setting the Water and Sewer Rates to be
Effective July 1, 2023; amendment to Water and Sewer Regulations
MOVED: In accordance with General Bylaw 3.62 Water Use Regulations, having heard
public comment specific to the proposed amendments on June 5, 2023, notice of which
was posted on the Town Bulletin Board for at least 10 days on May 17, and notice of
amendments published in the newspaper on May 22, 2023, to amend the Water Use
Regulations Appendix A, as follows:
By replacing “April 18, 2023” with “July 1, 2023”
By replacing “Domestic Water Rate $4.75 per 100 cubic feet” with “Domestic Water Rate
$5.00 per 100 cubic feet”
By replacing “Agricultural Water Rate $4.75 per 100 cubic feet” with Agricultural Water
Rate $5.00 per 100 cubic feet”
Motion by: Griesemer
Seconded by: Hanneke
ROLL CALL VOTE: 9-0, with 4 absent (Bahl-Milne, De Angelis, Miller, Lopes)
MOVED: In accordance with General Bylaw 3.61 Sewer Use Regulations, having heard
public comment specific to the proposed amendments on June 5, 2023, notice of which
was posted on the Town Bulletin Board for at least 10 days on May 17, and amendments
were published in the newspaper on May 22, 2023, to amend the Sewer Use Regulations
Appendix A, as follows:
By replacing “April 18, 2023” with “July 1, 2023”
By replacing “Domestic Sewer Rate $5.20 per 100 cubic feet” with “Domestic Sewer Rate
$5.50 per 100 cubic feet”
Motion by: Griesemer
Seconded by: Hanneke
ROLL CALL VOTE: 9-0, with 4 absent (Bahl-Milne, De Angelis, Miller, Lopes)

An order setting of the Water and Sewer Rates to be effective July 1, 2023. (majority vote)
