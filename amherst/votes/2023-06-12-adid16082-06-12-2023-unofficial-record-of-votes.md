14. Topics not reasonably anticipated 48 hours before the meeting
a. Request to waive Town Council Policy on Making Recommendations for Town
Council Appointments to Multiple-Member Bodies to re-open the Applicant Pool
for the Impending ZBA Vacancies and to waive associated other deadlines
(7 votes)
MOVED: To (1) waive Sections 7 and 9 of the Town Council Policy on Making
Recommendations for Town Council Appointments to Multiple-Member Bodies to (a) re-
open the applicant pool for the impending ZBA vacancies that closed at 10:32 am on
June 8, 2023, so that the applicant pool includes individuals that submitted both a CAF
and a Statement of Interest prior to 11:59 pm on June 11, 2023, (b) permit the late posting
of applicant Statements of Interest and not as a group, and (c) permit less than 7 days’
notice of the adopted interview questions to all applicants; (2) direct the CRC Chair and
Council Clerk to cause the meeting agenda be modified to list any additional applicants
and to post any additional Statements of Interest in the packet and attach them to the
meeting notice by 11:30 am on Tuesday, June 13, 2023, and (3) to direct the CRC Chair
to send any additional applicants the adopted interview questions by 11:30 am on June
13, 2023; provided however, that any additional candidates meeting this criteria that
cannot attend the group interview scheduled for June 15, 2023 at 11:30 am by zoom shall
not be considered applicants for the impending vacancies that the June 15 meeting is
intended to make recommendations to fill.
Motion by: Hanneke
Seconded by: De Angelis
ROLL CALL VOTE: 12-0, with 1 absent (Miller)
