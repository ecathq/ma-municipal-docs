f. Authorization to enter into a Lease Agreement with Ice Pond Woods Condominium
Association for Rooftop Solar: 87-88 Crossbrook
MOVED: To authorize the Town Manager to enter into and execute a lease agreement
with Ice Pond Woods Condominium Association pursuant to which the Town will, for

portions of the premises located at 87-88 Crossbrook, Amherst, for a term of up to fifteen
years, and any and all notices of leases, easements, and other documents related thereto.

Seconded by: Hanneke
ROLL CALL VOTE: 12-0 with 1 absent (Devlin Gauthier)
g. Acquisition of Permanent Public Way Easement: 75 East Pleasant Street

Council President to sign the Order of Taking on behalf of the Town Council.
Motion by: Griesemer
Seconded by: Rooney
ROLL CALL VOTE: 12-0 with 1 absent (Devlin Gauthier)
