6. Proclamations and Resolutions
a. 2023 Puerto Rican Heritage Day Proclamation

as presented.
b. Resolution in Support of Suicide Awareness and Prevention

8. Action Items
a. Councilor Compensation Funding Plan – No action taken
b. Town Manager Evaluation Timeline – No action required
c. Town Manager Goals
VOTED via consent agenda to refer development of the Town Manager Goals to the
Governance, Organization, and Legislation Committee, with a report to the Town
Council by November 20, 2023.
9.a. Town Manager Appointments
1) Design Review Board
VOTED via consent agenda to approve the Town Manager appointments to the Design
Review Board: Historical Commission Representative Patricia Auth and Planning Board
Representative Karin Winter, effective immediately, for terms to expire June 30, 2024.
2) Community Preservation Act Committee
VOTED via consent agenda to approve the Town Manager reappointment to the
Preservation Act Committee: Katie Zobel, effective immediately, for a term to expire
June 30, 2026.
