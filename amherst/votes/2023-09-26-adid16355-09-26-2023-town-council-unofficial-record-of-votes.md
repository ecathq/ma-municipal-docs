Town of Amherst
UNOFFICIAL RECORD OF VOTES OF THE TOWN COUNCIL
Tuesday, September 26, 2023 6:00 p.m.
Councilors present: De Angelis, Devlin Gauthier, Griesemer, Hanneke, Lopes, Steinberg, Taub
Councilors participating remotely: Bahl-Milne, Miller, Pam, Rooney, Schoen, Walker
Councilors absent: None
Amherst School Committee members present: Rhodes, Shiao
Amherst School Committee members participating remotely: None
Amherst School Committee members absent: None
Staff present: Clerk of the Council O’Keeffe
Staff participating remotely: None
When remote participation is used, all votes will be taken by roll call.
Per MGL Chapter 30A Section 20(f), this meeting is being recorded and broadcast by Amherst
Media.
President Griesemer called the Special Town Council Meeting to order at 6:13 p.m.; adjourned at
9:25 p.m.
Election to fill 3 vacancies on the Amherst School Committee, for terms ending
January 2024
MOVED: To elect Roger Wallace, Gabriella Weaver, and Katie Lazdowski, registered
voters in the Town of Amherst, to perform the duties of members of the Amherst School
Committee until newly elected members of the Amherst School Committee are sworn in.
Motion by: Griesemer
Seconded by: De Angelis
ROLL CALL VOTE of the Town Council and remaining School Committee
members: Unanimous
