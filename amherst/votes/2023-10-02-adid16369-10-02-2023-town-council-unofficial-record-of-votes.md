9.a. Town Manager Appointments
1) Board of Assessors
VOTED via consent agenda to approve the Town Manager appointment to the Board of
Assessors: Nick Grabbe, effective immediately, for a term to expire June 30, 2025.
2) Community Preservation Act Committee
VOTED via consent agenda to approve the Town Manager appointments to the
Community Preservation Act Committee: Conservation Commission Representative
Michelle Labbe and Planning Board Representative Doug Marshall, effective
immediately, for terms to expire June 30, 2024.
3) Elementary School Building Committee
VOTED via consent agenda to approve the Town Manager appointments to the
Elementary School Building Committee: Superintendent of Schools Douglas Slaughter
and Finance Director Jennifer LaFountain, effective immediately, for terms to last the
length of the building process.
4) Extension of Appointment of Temporary Police Chief
VOTED via consent agenda to approve the extension of time of the Town Manager
Temporary Appointment to Town Office for Captain Gabriel Ting as Temporary Police
Chief to December 31, 2023.
