h. Request for Waivers of the Town Council Policy on Making Recommendations for
Town Council Appointments to Multiple-Member Bodies

the SOI for the process without resubmission.

the Policy to allow Committee members to ask follow up questions during group
interviews.
VOTED via consent agenda for the current ZBA vacancies, to waive Section 3 of the
Policy to allow the Committee to declare the applicant pool sufficient less than 14 days
after the Bulletin Board notice is posted.
