Town of Amherst
UNOFFICIAL RECORDS OF VOTES OF THE TOWN COUNCIL
Monday, December 11, 2023 6:30 p.m.
Councilors present: De Angelis, Devlin Gauthier, Griesemer, Hanneke, Lopes, Rooney,
Steinberg, Taub
Councilors participating remotely: Bahl-Milne, Miller (joined at 8:37 p.m.), Pam, Schoen,
Walker
Councilors absent: None
Staff present: Town Manager Bockelman, Council Clerk O’Keeffe
When remote participation is used, all votes will be taken by roll call.
Per MGL Chapter 30A Section 20(f), this meeting is being recorded and broadcast by Amherst
Media.
President Griesemer called the special Town Council meeting to order at 6:31 p.m.; adjourned at
10:27 p.m.
a. Town Manager Evaluation
MOVED: To adopt the 2023 Town Manager Performance Evaluation, as presented in
version 5.
Motion by: Griesemer
Seconded by: De Angelis
Unanimous roll call vote
b. Town Manager Goals
d. Town Manager Compensation
MOVED: To grant the Town Manager a 2-year contract extension and, if non-union
employees are granted more than a 2% increase, to match the increase above 2%.
Motion by: Griesemer
Seconded by: Rooney
Unanimous roll call vote
Executive Session
MOVED: To convene in executive session for the following purposes:
In accordance with MGL c.30A, §21(a)2, to conduct strategy sessions in preparation for
negotiations with nonunion personnel, Town Manager Paul Bockelman;
In accordance with MGL c.30A, §21(a)2, to conduct contract negotiations with nonunion
personnel, Town Manager Paul Bockelman.
The Town Council will re-convene in open session following executive session.
Motion by: Griesemer
Seconded by: Devlin Gauthier
Unanimous roll call vote
