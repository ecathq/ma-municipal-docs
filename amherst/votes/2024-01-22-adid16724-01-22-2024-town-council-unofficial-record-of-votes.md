8. Action Items
a. Annual Review of Town Council Rules of Procedure – Rules 4.6, 5, and 6
MOTION: To adopt amendments to the Town Council Rules of Procedure rule 5, with
additions shown in red or green text and deletions shown in red or green strikethrough
text on pages 11-14 of the motions sheet.
Motion by: Griesemer
Seconded by: Rooney
ROLL CALL VOTE: Unanimous with 1 absent (Walker)
MOTION: To adopt amendments to the Town Council Rules of Procedure rule 6, with
additions shown in red or green text and deletions shown in red or green strikethrough
text on pages 15-18 of the motions sheet.
Motion by: Griesemer
Seconded by: Hanneke
ROLL CALL VOTE: Unanimous with 1 absent (Walker)
MOTION: To amend the motion by replacing “Participants” with “Councilors” and by inserting
the words “or communicate with other Councilors or the public by text phone, email, or other
means” in proposed 6.1.f. so that the rule reads, “Councilors shall not hold private conversations
or communicate with other Councilors or the public by text, phone, email, or other means during
Council meetings.”
Motion by: Steinberg
Seconded by: Ryan
ROLL CALL VOTE: 2-10 with 1 absent (Walker)
Aye: Ryan, Steinberg
Nay: De Angelis, Devlin Gauthier, Ette, Griesemer, Hanneke, Hegner, Lord, Rooney, Schoen,
Taub
Present (abstain): None
Absent: Walker
The motion fails.
