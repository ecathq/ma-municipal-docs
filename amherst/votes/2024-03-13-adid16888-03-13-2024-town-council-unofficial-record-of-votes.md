UNOFFICIAL RECORD OF VOTES
Wednesday, March 13, 2024 6:30 p.m.
Councilors participating remotely: De Angelis, Devlin Gauthier, Ette, Griesemer, Hanneke,
Hegner, Lord, Rooney, Ryan, Schoen, Steinberg, Taub, Walker

President Griesemer called the meeting to order at 6:34 p.m.; adjourned at 6:47 p.m.
3. Action Items
a. Response to Offner Open Meeting Law Complaint
MOTION: To determine that the Town Council did not violate the Open Meeting Law
during the last 45 minutes of the March 4, 2024 Town Council meeting; to acknowledge
the Council actions taken during the last 45 minutes of the March 4, 2024 Town Council
Meeting; to confirm the record of the meeting; and to authorize KP Law to respond to the
Open Meeting Law complaint filed by Mike Offner on March 6, 2024, consistent with the
Council’s discussion on March 13, 2024, that the Town Council did not violate the Open
Meeting Law.
Motion by: Griesemer
Seconded by: Devlin Gauthier
ROLL CALL VOTE: Unanimous
b. Response to Steinberg Open Meeting Law Complaint
MOTION: To determine that the Town Council did not violate the Open Meeting Law
during the last 45 minutes of the March 4, 2024 Town Council meeting; to acknowledge
the Council actions taken during the last 45 minutes of the March 4, 2024 Town Council
Meeting; to confirm the record of the meeting; and to authorize KP Law to respond to the
Open Meeting Law complaint filed by Lonna Steinberg on March 6, 2024, consistent
with the Council’s discussion on March 13, 2024, that the Town Council did not violate
the Open Meeting Law.
Motion by: Devlin Gauthier
Seconded by: Lord
ROLL CALL VOTE: Unanimous
5. Adjourn
MOTION: To adjourn the meeting.
Motion by: Griesemer
Seconded by: Ette
ROLL CALL VOTE: Unanimous
