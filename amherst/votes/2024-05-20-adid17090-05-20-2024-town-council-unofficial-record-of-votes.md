c. Requests for Use of the Public Way
1) Mobile Market on East Hadley Road, signs at Groff Park, Mill Lane, and
Belchertown Road
VOTED via consent agenda to approve the requests from the Amherst Mobile Market
during the 2024 Mobile Market season as follows, exact location and duration to be
determined by the Town Manager in consultation with the Amherst Mobile Market:
 Placement of two tents and six tables in the public way near the Valley Bike
Station on East Hadley Road, once per week on Saturdays from 9 a.m. to 3 p.m.;
 Placement of one sandwich-board-style sign advertising the Amherst Mobile
Market permanently during the Mobile Market season at 83 Mill Lane
(advertising the market located at Groff Park Recreation Area);
 Placement of one sandwich-board-style sign advertising the Amherst Mobile
Market permanently during the Mobile Market season at 95 Montague Road
(advertising the market located at Mill River Recreation Area);
 Placement of two sandwich-board-style signs advertising the Amherst Mobile
Market permanently during the Mobile Market season at 81 Belchertown Road
and the South East Street entrance to Colonial Village (advertising the market
located in the Colonial Village Apartments complex).
2) Farm Stand at 822 East Pleasant Street
MOTION: To approve the placement of wooden Farm Stand (5 feet wide by 12 feet
long) with shingled roof and solar panel and wooden sign in the Town Way at 822 East
Pleasant Street.
No action; the motion was withdrawn.

d. Acceptance of a Public Way
1) Amherst Hills
MOTION: Having determined that common convenience and necessity require the
acceptance of Hawthorn Road, Linden Ridge Road (portion), and Concord Way as Town
ways, to hereby lay out Hawthorn Road, Linden Ridge Road (portion), and Concord Way
as public ways, which layouts are shown more particularly on a plan entitled “Plan of
Land in Amherst and Belchertown, Massachusetts Surveyed for Tofino Associates, Inc.”,
dated June 29, 2004, and recorded with the Hampshire County Registry of Deeds at Plan
Book 201, Pages 43-48, which plan was referred to the Planning Board and which plan is
hereby adopted as a part of this order, and all land lying within the layouts of Hawthorn
Road, Linden Ridge Road (portion), and Concord Way are laid out as public ways, and,
further, the Town hereby accepts from Tofino Associates, Inc. deeds conveying said
public ways to the Town and easements related thereto.
Motion by: Griesemer
Seconded by: Devlin Gauthier
ROLL CALL VOTE: Unanimous with 1 absent (Lord)
