d. FY2025 Water and Sewer Rates; Amendments to the Water and Sewer Regulations
MOVED: In accordance with General Bylaw 3.62 Water Use Regulations, having heard
public comment specific to the proposed amendments on July 15, 2024, notice of which
was posted on the Town Bulletin Board for at least 10 days on June 21, 2024, and notice
of amendments published in the newspaper on July 1, 2024, to amend the Water Use
Regulations Appendix A, as follows:
By replacing the effective date of “July 1, 2023” with “July 16, 2024”
By replacing “Domestic Water Rate $5.00 per 100 cubic feet” with “Domestic Water Rate
$5.25 per 100 cubic feet”
By replacing “Agricultural Water Rate $5.00 per 100 cubic feet” with Agricultural Water
Rate $5.25 per 100 cubic feet”
Motion by: Griesemer
Seconded by: Hegner
ROLL CALL VOTE: Unanimous with 1 absent (Devlin Gauthier)
MOVED: In accordance with General Bylaw 3.61 Sewer Use Regulations, having heard
