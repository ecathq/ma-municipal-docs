Town of Amherst
UNOFFICAL RECORD OF VOTES
Monday, August 19, 2024 6:30 p.m.
Councilors present: De Angelis, Ette, Griesemer, Hanneke, Hegner, Rooney, Ryan, Steinberg
Councilors participating remotely: Devlin Gauthier, Lord, Schoen, Walker
Councilors absent: Taub
Staff present: Town Manager Bockelman, Council Clerk O’Keeffe
When remote participation is used, all votes will be taken by roll call.
Per MGL Chapter 30A §20(f), this meeting is being recorded and broadcast by Amherst Media.
President Griesemer called the regular meeting to order at 6:31 p.m.; adjourned at 9:27 p.m.
8. Action Items
a. Proposed Transportation and Parking Commission Charge and Amendments to the
Town Council Policy on the Control and Regulation of the Public Ways
Majority present and voting required to pass.
MOTION: To refer the proposed Transportation and Parking Commission Charge and
Amendments to the Town Council Policy on the Control and Regulation of the Public
Ways to the Town Services and Outreach Committee, with subsequent review for clarity,
consistency, and actionability by the Governance, Organization, and Legislation
Committee, with reports to the Town Council by October 21, 2024.
Motion by: Griesemer
Seconded by: Rooney
ROLL CALL VOTE: Unanimous
9. Appointments
b. Town Council Appointments
1) Finance Committee: Bernie Kubiak for term to expire June 30, 2025 and Thomas
Avery Porter, for term to expire June 30, 2026.
Majority present required to pass.
MOTION: To appoint the following non-voting Finance Committee members: Bernie
Kubiak, for a term to expire June 30th, 2025; Thomas Avery Porter, for a term to expire
June 30th, 2026.
Motion by: Griesemer
Seconded by: De Angelis
ROLL CALL VOTE: Unanimous
2) Motion postponed by Councilor Schoen on July 15, 2024 per Charter Sec. 2.10(c):
To appoint Lawrence Kluttz to the Planning Board for a term beginning
immediately and ending June 30, 2027
Majority present voting in favor required to pass.
