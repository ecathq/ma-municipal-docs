8. Action Items

f. Referral of Proposed University Drive Overlay District – Adoption of Overlay
District, Changes to Official Zoning Map by add the University Drive Overlay
District
Majority present and voting required to pass.
MOTION: To refer the Proposed University Drive Overlay District – Adoption of
Overlay District, Changes to Official Zoning Map by adding the University Drive
Overlay District to the Planning Board and the Community Resources Committee:
 Community Resources Committee and Planning Board public hearings must be
held no later than November 27, 2024 (within 65 days of the referral)
 Written Planning Board recommendation must be sent to the Town Council and
Community Resources Committee no later than 21 days of the close of the
Planning Board public hearing
 Written Community Resources Committee recommendation and all materials
must be sent to the Town Council and Governance, Organization, and Legislation
Committee no later than 30 days of the close of the CRC public hearing
 Written Governance, Organization, and Legislation report on clarity, consistency,
and actionability must be sent to the Town Council within 60 days of the close of
the CRC public hearing
 Town Council action is required within 90 days of the close of the CRC public
hearing
Motion by: Griesemer
Seconded by: Hanneke
ROLL CALL VOTE: Unanimous with 1 absent (Ryan)
