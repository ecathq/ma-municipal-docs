8. Action Items
a. Open Meeting Law Complaint filed by Mr. Ahmed Esfahani, dated Jan 29, 2025:
Acknowledge receipt of complaint, review and discuss Council’s response, and
authorize Council President to work with Town Attorney to finalize submission of
response to Complainant and Attorney General
MOTION: To determine that the Town Council did not violate the Open Meeting Law
as alleged in the Open Meeting Law complaint filed by Ahmad Esfahani dated January
29, 2025 and to authorize KP Law to work with the Council President to respond to the
Open Meeting Law complaint consistent with the Council’s discussion on February 10,
2025, that the Town Council did not violate the Open Meeting Law as alleged;
additionally, in the interests of transparency and the Town Council’s commitment to
compliance with the Open Meeting Law, to review and approve any outstanding meeting
minutes and voluntarily post them to its website as promptly as feasible.
Motion by: Griesemer
Seconded by: De Angelis
ROLL CALL VOTE: Unanimous with 2 absent (Taub and Walker)

e. Request for Long-Term Reservation of Public Way: Amherst Farmer’s Market
MOVED: To approve the Amherst Farmers’ Market Long-Term Event Use reservation
of the South Common and associated parking spaces at no charge, under Town Council
Policy Regarding the Control and Regulation of the Public Ways on Saturdays from 8:30
a.m. to 1:30 p.m. beginning on April 19, 2025 and ending on November 15, 2025:
 Approve exclusive use of the area on the South Common as shown on the plan
entitled “Amherst Farmers’ Market; Proposed Layout 2022 Season: January 27,
2022”;
 Reserve 15 spaces in the Spring Street lot, six spaces on Boltwood Avenue, and
five spaces on South Pleasant Street for all for vendor parking and customer pick-
up to be clearly marked by the Amherst Farmer’s Market.
And to further authorize the Town Manager the ability to modify the approval through
November 15, 2025 in order to schedule concurrent use of the Town Common for short-
term event uses.
Motion by: Griesemer
Seconded by: Ryan
ROLL CALL VOTE: Unanimous with 1 absent (Taub)
