b. Charter Review Committee Appropriation Request and Intergovernmental
Agreement
(Majority present and voting required to pass)
VOTED via consent agenda to refer the $15,000 appropriation request for consultant

(Majority present required to pass)
VOTED via consent agenda in accordance with MGL C.40 s. 4A which allows for joint
operation of public activities among governmental units, which requires that such inter-
governmental agreements be approved by the Town Council and negotiated and signed
by the Town Manager, the Town Council hereby authorizes the Town Manager to enter
into an inter-governmental agreement with the Edward J. Collins, Jr. Center for Public
Management at the University of Massachusetts Boston for consulting services for the
Charter Review Committee.
