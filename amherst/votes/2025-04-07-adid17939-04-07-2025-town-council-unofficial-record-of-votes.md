Article 2 ZONING DISTRICTS
Section 2.04 Special Districts
University Drive Overlay District
The University Drive Overlay District is an overlay district intended to encourage economic
development and expand housing opportunity, by allowing mixed-use buildings, in an area that
is currently zoned Limited Business (BL) and Office Park (OP) along the east and west sides of
University Drive between Northampton Road (Route 9) and Amity Street.
ARTICLE 17 UNIVERSITY DRIVE OVERLAY DISTRICT
SECTION 17.0 GENERAL
The University Drive Overlay District shall be superimposed over certain areas on the east and
west sides of University Drive between Northampton Road (Route 9) and Amity Street.
The Overlay District shall apply only to Mixed-Use Buildings. The Overlay District establishes
its own requirements for Mixed-Use Buildings including dimensional standards, standards &
conditions and design guidelines that shall be used rather than those standards in this Bylaw.
Therefore, the standards and conditions in Section 3.325 of the Zoning Bylaw do not apply in the
Overlay District and any Mixed-Use Building proposed in the overlay shall be permitted using
the University Drive Overlay District contained in Section 17.3.
The definition of a Mixed-Use Building in Section 12 of the Zoning Bylaw remains in effect in
the Overlay District.
SECTION 17.1 PURPOSE
The purpose of the University Drive Overlay District is to encourage economic development and
expand housing opportunity in an area that is currently zoned Limited Business (BL) and Office
Park (OP), and is within close proximity to the University of Massachusetts. The dimensional
standards and design guidelines of the University Drive Overlay District are intended to foster
development with dense residential units, enhance the pedestrian experience along the street,
allow for street trees and stormwater management, provide spaces for commercial and retail
uses, and result in architecture that maintains a scale and character appropriate for an entry into
the Town and University. It is also intended that University Drive Overlay District expand retail
and commercial vitality for year-round use with street-level services, pedestrian activity and
vibrant amenities.

17.36 Height Requirements
Building Height: Minimum: N/A Maximum: 70 ft/6 flrs
17.360 Building height does not include rooftop mechanical equipment,

SECTION 17.4 PERMITTING OF MIXED-USED BUILDINGS
17.41 Within the University Drive Overlay District Mixed-Use Buildings shall be
permitted by Site Plan Review (SPR) pursuant to Section 11.2 with the following
requirements:
17.410 At least 75% of the street facing façade of the first or ground floor on
University Drive and Northampton Road shall be any permitted non-
residential use, other than parking, including incidental uses, to a
minimum depth of 24 feet.
17.411 After accounting for the minimum depth of 24 feet as provided in
17.410, parking shall be allowed on the first or ground floor for the
remaining floor area.
17.412 In the area shown on the Zoning Map that includes properties with
Map 13D and Parcels 2, 3, 5, 59, 60, 62 and 70, the first or ground
floor shall be exclusively occupied by any permitted non-residential
use, other than parking, however, up to 15% of the ground floor may
be used as incidental or common space to support the use on the upper
floors.

17.511 More than one Mixed-Use Building may be permitted on a single lot
using the Overlay District.
17.512 One of more buildings permitted with the underlying zoning may be
located on the same lot as Mixed-Use Building(s) permitted with the
Overlay District.

Building adjacent to the street.
17.52 Project Open Space
17.520 Every project shall provide usable open space for residents and the
public. Project open space on the site shall be provided in areas that
are not required parking areas, driveways, the multi-use path, services
areas or areas with a slope greater than ten percent.
17.521 On the west side of University Drive there shall be a minimum 10ft
wide multi-use path for public access and use between the front of any
building and the property line.
17.522 Outdoor amenities in the project open space such as benches, sitting
walls, and planters should be carefully located to not interfere with the
continuity of the sidewalk and the visual connection to interior uses.
Their use is encouraged to provide benefits to residents of the building
as well as use by the public.
17.523 The planting of street trees shall be required.
17.53 Parking
17.530 The parking standards listed in Article 7 shall apply in the University
Drive Overlay District with the following exceptions:
17.5301 Section 7.0 General Requirements shall not apply.
17.5302 Section 7.2 Shared or Leased Parking shall not apply.
17.531 An applicant shall be required to submit 1) a parking utilization study
and 2) a parking management plan in order for the Permit Granting
Authority to determine that an adequate number of off-street parking
spaces are provided.

17.532 Shared or leased parking is encouraged in the Overlay District. The
Permit Granting Authority shall require the submittal of any shared or
leased parking agreements to determine that those spaces are available.
17.54 Design Guidelines
17.540 Design Review Principles and Standards (Section 3.204) shall apply
and be used by the Permit Granting Authority.
17.541 To the extent feasible, a majority of the street facing façade shall be
built along the width of the lot frontage and be generally located along
the minimum front setback line to reinforce the sidewalk/street edge.
17.542 Overhanging awning and projected canopies are encouraged between
the first and second floors on mixed-use buildings, as is a horizontal
sign band.
17.543 The street facing façade shall have variations in architectural detail,
change in plane or other treatment to break down the scale and mass of
a large flat façade.
17.544 Surface or open parking lots should not face the street and should be
located behind buildings or to the side of buildings a minimum of 30
feet from public ways, and shall be screened from adjacent residential
uses in a residential district.
17.545 To the extent possible, parking lots behind buildings shall be
consolidated across property lines to maximize efficiency of paved
space and minimize the number of driveways and curb cuts to public
streets and associated sidewalk crossings.
17.546 Rooftop mechanical equipment shall be screened from view as
determined by the Permit Granting Authority.
17.547 Loading docks, dumpsters, mechanical equipment and utility meters,
except as required by the public utilities, shall be located in low
visibility locations and screened from view by architectural or
landscape elements compatible with the site and building design.
