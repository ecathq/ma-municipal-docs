Town of Amherst
UNOFFICIAL RECORD OF VOTES OF THE TOWN COUNCIL
Monday, May 5, 2025 6:30 p.m.
Councilors present: De Angelis, Ette, Griesemer, Hanneke, Rooney, Ryan, Schoen, Steinberg,
Taub
Councilors participating remotely: Hegner, Lord, Walker
Councilors absent: Devlin Gauthier
Staff present: Assistant Town Manager Bockelman, Council Clerk O’Keeffe, Finance Director
Zawadzki, Comptroller Drake, Treasurer/Collector LaFountain
Staff participating remotely: Assistant Town Manager Ziomek
When remote participation is used, all votes will be taken by roll call.
Per MGL Chapter 30A Section 20(f), this meeting is being recorded and broadcast by Amherst
Media.
President Griesemer called the meeting to order at 6:31 p.m.; adjourned at 8:05 p.m.
8. Action Items
a. Fiscal Year 2026 Budget, Capital Inventory, and Capital Improvement Program
MOVED: In accordance with Charter Section 5.5(a), to refer the FY26 Budget, Capital
Inventory, and Capital Improvement Program to the Finance Committee for a public
hearing on the FY26 Budget and recommendation on the Budget and Capital
Improvement Program to the Council within 30 days.
Motion by: Griesemer
Seconded by: Schoen
ROLL CALL VOTE: Unanimous with 1 absent (Devlin Gauthier)
b. Acceptance of Roads as Public Ways: Palley Village Place
MOVED: The Town Council hereby declares its intention to lay out Palley Village Place
as a public way and to refer the layout and plan to the Planning Board for its review in
accordance with MGL c.41, s.81G – 81I; with report back to the Town Council within 45
days from this referral.
Motion by: Griesemer
Seconded by: Hegner
ROLL CALL VOTE: Unanimous with 1 absent (Devlin Gauthier)
16. Adjourn
MOVED: To adjourn the meeting.
Motion by: Griesemer
Seconded by: De Angelis
ROLL CALL VOTE: Unanimous with 1 absent (Devlin Gauthier)
