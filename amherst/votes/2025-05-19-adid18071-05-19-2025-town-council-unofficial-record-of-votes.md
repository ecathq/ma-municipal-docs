c. Percent for Art – Elementary School Building Project
Motion postponed on March 10, 2025
MOVED: Schoen moved, second by Walker, to set a limit of $100,000 for the Percent
for Art budget for the Fort River Elementary School. This budget would need to cover
commissioning the art works, installation, insurance (if needed), and other Town taxpayer
costs related to soliciting proposals, public engagement, and contracting for the art
projects.
