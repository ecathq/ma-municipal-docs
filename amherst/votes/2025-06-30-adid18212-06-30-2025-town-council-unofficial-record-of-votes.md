5. Consent Agenda
The following items were selected because they were considered to be routine and it

consent agenda does not require a second.

MOVED: To move the following items, and the printed motions thereunder and approve

expire June 30, 2026
o Recreation Commission Reappointments: Jonas Cox and Andrew MacDougall
for terms to expire June 30, 2028
 11.a-c. Approval of the following meeting minutes
o April 14, 2025 Special Meeting Minutes
o April 28, 2025 Regular Meeting Minutes
o May 5, 2025 Regular Meeting Minutes
