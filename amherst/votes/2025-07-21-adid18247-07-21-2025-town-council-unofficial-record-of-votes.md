5. Consent Agenda
The following items were selected because they were considered to be routine and it
was reasonable to expect they would pass with no controversy. To remove an item from
the consent agenda for discussion later in the meeting, ask that it be removed when the
President lists the consent agenda items. The request to remove an item from the
consent agenda does not require a second.

o May 12, 2025 Special Meeting Minutes

o June 2, 2025 Regular Meeting Minutes
