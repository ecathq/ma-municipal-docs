b. Proposed Zoning Amendment – Accessory Dwelling Units Section 5.011 – First Reading
c. Proposed Zoning Amendment - Inclusionary Zoning Bylaw Section 15.171 payment in
lieu of affordable units
(2/3 vote required to adopt)
MOVED: To amend Article 15, Section 15.171 by striking out “four (4)” and replacing
with “five (5) times and no more than eight (8) and adding the sentence “Precise
payment amounts shall be determined by the Permit Granting Authority with
recommendation provided from the Housing Trust, and may account for market
conditions, total development costs, unit sizes, project location, cost of postponement
in the delivery of affordable units, and similar factors.”, as shown below:
15.171Payment of fees-in-lieu of affordable units may be allowed, payable prior to the
granting of a Certificate of Occupancy, for any dwelling units associated with the
development, to the Town of Amherst Municipal Affordable Housing Trust. The
fee-in-lieu value for each affordable unit not provided shall be four (4) at least
five (5) times and no more than eight (8) times the current Median Family
Income for Amherst as determined by the U.S. Department of Housing and
Urban Development (HUD) or successor agency. Precise payment amounts shall
be determined by the Permit Granting Authority with recommendation
provided from the Housing Trust, and may account for market conditions, total
development costs, unit sizes, project location, cost of postponement in the

d. Proposed Zoning Amendment – Zoning Map Change: Main Street B-N District
(Majority present and voting)
MOVED: In accordance with MGL Ch. 40A §5, to refer the proposed change to the
Zoning Map, rezoning parcels 14B-29, 14B-30, and 14B-31 from the General Residence
District (R-G) to the Neighborhood Business District (B-N) to the Planning Board, for
public hearing within 65 days and report to the Town Council; to Community Resources
Committee, for public hearing and recommendation to the Town Council; and to
Governance, Organization, and Legislation for a final review of clarity, consistency, and
actionability.
Motion by: Griesemer
Seconded by: Hanneke
ROLL CALL VOTE: Unanimous 11-0, with 2 absent (Rooney, Walker)
e. Request for Removal of One Parking Space Adjacent to 151 Amity Street
(Majority present required)
MOVED: To eliminate one parking place immediately to the east of the driveway for 151
Amity Street, and request that the Department of Public Works investigate adjusting
parking places in an appropriate manner to address the safety issues that have been
raised by the residents.
Motion by: Griesemer
Seconded by: De Angelis
ROLL CALL VOTE: Unanimous, 12-0 with 1 absent (Rooney)
