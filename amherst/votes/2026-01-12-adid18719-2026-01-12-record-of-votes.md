8. Action Items
a. Intermunicipal Agreement: Towns of Amherst, Leverett, Pelham, Shutesbury, and the
Amherst Regional School District regarding Pelham payment for Middle School Roof
Project
(Majority present required)
VOTED via consent agenda in accordance with MGL C.40 s. 4A which allows for joint
operation of public activities among governmental units, which requires that such inter-
governmental agreements be approved by the Town Council and negotiated and signed
by the Town Manager, the Town Council hereby authorizes the Town Manager to enter
into an inter-governmental agreement with the Amherst-Pelham Regional School
District and the Towns of Leverett, Pelham, and Shutesbury to allow the Town of
Pelham make a one-time cash payment and not participate in the District’s borrowing
for the Middle School roof project.
