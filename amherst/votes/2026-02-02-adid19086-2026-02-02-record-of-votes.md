8. Action Items
a. Acceptance of Public Way: Palley Village Place
(Majority present required)
VOTED via consent agenda that the Town Council, having determined that common
convenience and necessity require the acceptance of Palley Village Place as a Town way,
hereby lays out Palley Village Place as a public way, which layout is shown more
particularly on a plan entitled “’Palley Village’ ‘Definitive Subdivision Plan’ Plan of Land
in Amherst, Massachusetts Prepared for Alandev, LLC,” dated May 24, 2004, prepared
by Harold L. Eaton & Associates, Inc., and recorded with the Hampshire County Registry
of Deeds at Plan Book 203, Page 28, which plan was referred to the Planning Board and
is hereby adopted as a part of this order, and all land lying within the layout of Palley
Village Place is laid out as a public way, and hereby takes by eminent domain a
permanent easement in Palley Village Place for all purposes for which public ways are
used in the Town of Amherst, including, without limitation, for utility purpose, and
drainage and hydrant easements on abutting lots, and, further, awards no damages for
said taking, it being acknowledged that the property owners have also waived damages
for said taking.
(Majority present required)
VOTED via consent agenda to approve the Order of Taking, as shown on pages 11-14 of
the Motions Sheet.

COMMONWEALTH OF MASSACHUSETTS
HAMPSHIRE, SS. TOWN OF AMHERST
ORDER OF TAKING
At a regularly convened meeting of the Town Council of the Town of Amherst held this
_____ day of ____________, 2026, it was voted and ordered:
The Town Council of the Town of Amherst, duly elected, qualified, and acting as such on
behalf of the Town of Amherst by virtue of and in accordance with the Order adopted by the
Town Council on February _____________, 2026, a certified copy of which is attached hereto
and incorporated herein, and the authority of the provisions of Chapter 82, Sections 21-24, and
Chapter 79 of the General Laws, as amended, and any and every other power and authority that
is hereunto enabling, does hereby take, on behalf of the Town, the following interests in land for
public way purposes:
1. A permanent easement in, on, under, along, over, across and along the roadway
shown as “Palley Village Place” (the “Roadway Premises”) on a plan entitled “‘Palley
Village’ ‘Definitive Subdivision Plan’ Plan of Land in Amherst, Massachusetts Prepared for
Alandev, LLC,” dated May 24, 2004, prepared by Harold L. Eaton & Associates, Inc., and
recorded with the Hampshire County Registry of Deeds at Plan Book 203, Page 28 (the
“Subdivision Plan”), for all purposes for which public ways are used in the Town of
Amherst, including but not limited to, constructing, operating, improving, maintaining,
repairing, replacing, relocating, realigning and/or reconstructing rights of ways, sidewalks,
driveways, walkways, wheelchair ramps, guardrails, pavement markings, slopes of
excavation and/or embankment, retaining walls, stone walls, drainage structures and facilities
and other utilities, traffic signals, bicycle racks, and landscaping, and for any and all purposes
and uses incidental or related thereto. The Roadway Premises are bounded and described
more particularly in the Subdivision Plan and also shown on a plan entitled “‘As-Built Palley
Village Place’ Plan of Land in Amherst, Massachusetts Prepared for Alandev, LLC,” dated
July 31, 2023, revised through January 15, 2026, prepared by Harold L. Eaton & Associates,
Inc., and recorded with the Hampshire County Registry of Deeds herewith (the “As-Built
Plans”). The herein taking includes all roadway improvements, any drainage structures
and/or facilities serving the public ways, and municipal utilities, if any, located within the
Roadway Premises;
2. A permanent drainage easement in, on, under, along, over, across and along the
parcels of land shown on the Subdivision Plan as “Drainage Easement” on Lot 7 and Lot 12
(together, the “Drainage Premises”) for the purpose of flowing stormwater onto and for the
purpose of constructing, installing, connecting to, inspecting, maintaining, operating,
repairing, removing, replacing, relocating, and/or abandoning in place stormwater drain or
drains, manholes, outlet control structures and such other related facilities that the Town
deems appropriate for the collection, channeling, and disposal of stormwater, regardless of
origin. Included in this taking are any and all drainage structures and/or facilities for the
drainage of stormwater from the Roadway Premises, provided, however, that the Town shall

have no responsibility for the maintenance of any detention and/or retention ponds located
within the Drainage Premises; said detention/retention ponds shall be the responsibility of the
homeowners association and/or the lot owners; and
3. A permanent easement in, on, under, along, over, across and along the parcels of
land shown as “Fire Hydrant Easement” on Lot 8 and Lot 11 on the As-Built Plan (the
“Utility Premises”) for the purpose of installing, maintaining, repairing, replacing and
abandoning in place hydrants and any structures and/or facilities related thereto.
The easements taken herein shall further include the right of the Town and/or its agents,
employees, representatives, contractors and invitees to enter upon the Roadway Premises, the
Drainage Premises, and the Utility Premises (collectively, the “Easement Premises”) from time
to time by foot, vehicle or heavy equipment for all purposes stated herein and uses incidental
thereto, including, without limitation, grading and excavating land. No temporary or permanent
buildings, structures or other objects shall be constructed, installed or placed upon the Easement
Premises.
The rights taken herein do not include the taking of any structures, utilities and other
objects presently situated within the Easement Premises, but does include the right of the Town
to remove such structures, utilities and/or other objects, as well as any vegetation (including
trees and shrubs) located within the Easement Premises whenever their removal shall be
necessary or convenient to exercise the rights taken hereunder.
Structures and facilities located within the Easement Premises that are owned by private
utility companies, and easements presently encumbering the Easement Premises, including,
without limitation, easements held by private utility companies, are not included in this taking.
Said Easement Premises are owned or supposed to be owned and/or formerly owned by
the parties listed in Exhibit A, attached hereto and incorporated herein, which parties are
hereinafter collectively referred to as Owners. If in any instance the name of any Owner is not
correctly stated in this Order of Taking, it is understood that in such instance said parcel of land
is owned by an Owner or Owners unknown to us.
The Owners have waived damages and a right of appraisal of said damages, and we award no
damages to the Owners for the herein taking.
No betterments are to be assessed under this taking.
