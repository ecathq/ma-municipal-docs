c. Requests for use of the Public Way
1) Amherst Farmers Market
VOTED via consent agenda to approve the Amherst Farmers’ Market Long-Term Event Use
reservation of the South Common and associated parking spaces at no charge, under Town
Council Policy Regarding the Control and Regulation of the Public Ways on Saturdays from 5:30
a.m. to 1:30 p.m. beginning on April 18, 2026 and ending on November 21, 2026. (Note, market
vendors arrive and set-up as early as 5:30 a.m. The Market is open from 8:00 a.m. to 1:30 p.m.)
 Approve continued exclusive use of the area on the South Common as shown on the
plan entitled “Amherst Farmers’ Market; Proposed Layout 2022 Season: January 27,
2022”;
 Reserve fifteen spaces in the Spring Street parking lot, six spaces on Boltwood Avenue,
and five spaces on South Pleasant Street all for vendor parking and customer pick-up all
to be clearly marked by the Amherst Farmers’ Market.
And to further authorize the Town Manager the authority to modify the approval through
November 21, 2026 in order to schedule concurrent use of the Town Common for short-term
event uses.
2) Amherst Feminist Generation
VOTED via consent agenda to approve the Amherst Feminist Generation request to place no
more than 25 total signs in the public way adjacent to the PVTA bus stops identified by the
applicant from late March – late May and late August – early November, 2026.

Presentation:
 Regional School Committee Member Heard
o Auditorium roof not old enough to qualify for MSBA grant
o MSBA funding may be available in a few years
o Condition of the roof currently is poor
o Advocated for Town funding for roof repair
 Town Manager Bockelman
o Competing request for funding
o Schools have resources at their disposal that they can utilize rather than ask the
Town to fund the entire repair
Discussion:
 Schoen spoke about the need for repair and cost of deferred maintenance; advocated for
repairs to be funded by the Town and wrapped into planned school building repairs; value
of the building
 Concerns about leaks, air quality
 Desire to include auditorium roof repairs in planned repairs to other parts of the building
 Building owned by four towns, not only Amherst; other towns are not being asked to
contribute to repair
 Suggestion to allow the Town to use the auditorium for free if repairs are paid by the Town
 Lack of funds available from other Towns
 Debate regarding inclusion of a clause in the referral to explore intermunicipal agreements
Action:
MOTION: To request that the Town Manager resubmit Financial Order FY26-12C, An Order
Appropriating from Free Cash for a Portion of the Town of Amherst Capital Program – Road and
Sidewalk Repairs, to the Town Council, reducing the amount of the appropriation to $2,700,000,
and submit a new Appropriation and Transfer Order to the Council, appropriating $1,600,000
from Free Cash for a gift to the Amherst Pelham Regional School District for use to repair and
replace the Amherst Regional Middle School Auditorium Roof concurrently with the MSBA
Accelerated repair program for the rest of the Amherst Regional Middle School roof with
consideration of an intermunicipal agreement that would require the Amherst Pelham Regional
School District to reimburse the Town of Amherst for the excess contribution to the work on the
Amherst Regional Middle School Auditorium Roof above its required capital costs under the
regional agreement through an appropriation from the Amherst Pelham Regional School Excess
& Deficiency or Capital Stabilization Accounts or garner other financial benefits to the Town of
Amherst in light of its gift.
Motion by: Hanneke
Seconded by: Ryan
ROLL CALL VOTE: Unanimous
