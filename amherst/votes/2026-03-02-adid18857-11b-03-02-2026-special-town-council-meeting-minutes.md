Town of Amherst
Special Town Council Meeting

7:00 p.m.

Minutes
Councilors present: Brevik, Cano-Martin, Churchill, Devlin Gauthier, Griesemer, Hanneke, MacLeod,
Rooney, Ryan, Schoen
Councilors participating remotely: Lord, Taub, Walker
Councilors absent: None
Staff present: Town Manager Bockelman, Council Clerk O’Keeffe
1. Call to Order
President Hanneke declared the presence of a quorum, called the meeting to order at 6:00 p.m.,
confirmed members could hear and be heard, and announced audio and video recording.
2. Executive Session
a. In accordance with M.G.L. c. 30A, § 21(a)(3) To discuss strategy with respect to
collective bargaining or litigation, as an open meeting may have a detrimental effect
on the bargaining or litigating position of the public body (Unions: DPW Association
and DPW-S) the Council will not return to open session
MOTION: In accordance with M.G.L. c. 30A, § 21(a)(3) To discuss strategy with respect to collective
bargaining or litigation, as an open meeting may have a detrimental effect on the bargaining or litigating
position of the public body (Unions: DPW Association and DPW-S) the Council will not return to open
session.
Motion by: Hanneke
Seconded by: Griesemer
Councilors Brevik and Cano-Martin stated they read the materials provided and do not believe the
Council requires an executive session to hear comments from DPW employees.
ROLL CALL VOTE: 6-6, 1 abstention
Aye: Churchill, Devlin Gauthier, Griesemer, Hanneke, MacLeod, Ryan
Nay: Brevik, Cano-Martin, Lord, Rooney, Taub, Walker
Abstain: Schoen
Absent: None
The motion failed.
