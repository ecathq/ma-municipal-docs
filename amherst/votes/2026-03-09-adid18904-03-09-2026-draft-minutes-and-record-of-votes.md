8. Action Items
a. Financial Orders
1) Council Order FY26 – 12F An Order Appropriating from Free Cash for a Portion of the Town of
Amherst Capital Program – Replacement of Middle School Auditorium Roof
Discussion:
 Schoen gave the Finance Committee report, which centered on the issue of giving the funds to
the regional schools as a gift, timing of the Massachusetts School Building Authority grant
process to seek funds for the repairs, reduction to roads and sidewalks
 Request did not follow the usual funding practice/pathway
 Coordination in the future between the Town and Regional Schools on matters of funding for
repairs and major expenses
 Desire to advocate on behalf of the Town to the MSBA to address the funding schedule
 Town Manager spoke in support of the original funding amount for roads and sidewalks and
seek an alternative funding plan for the auditorium roof
 Sense of urgency to address the roof while the rest of the school roof is being repaired
 Urgent competing needs of roads and sidewalks and auditorium roof
MOTION: Having held a public forum on March 9, 2026, with a recommendation by the Finance
Committee on March 3, 2026, to adopt Appropriation and Transfer Order FY26 – 12F An Order
Appropriating from Free Cash for a Portion of the Town of Amherst Capital Program – Replacement of
Middle School Auditorium Roof, as shown on page 11 of the Motions Sheet.
Motion by: Hanneke
Seconded by: Devlin Gauthier
ROLL CALL VOTE: 12-1 (Hanneke nay)
Aye: Brevik, Cano-Martin, Churchill, Devlin Gauthier, Griesemer, Lord, MacLeod, Rooney, Ryan, Schoen,
Taub, Walker
Nay: Hanneke
Present (abstain): None
Absent: None
MOTION: To postpone the vote to March 23, 2026.
Motion by: Hanneke
Seconded by: Griesemer
Discussion:
 The bids for the project will be open before the next Council meeting and if the Council waits to
the next meeting, the Council will be able to vote the exact amount of the roof and allocate all
remaining funds to road repairs
 Schoen spoke in opposition to the motion, stating the Council should signal that there is funding
for the project
 The School District cannot sign a contract until financing is secured, but that funds do not need
to be secured when bids are opened
 The bid is out and they need funding approved before awarding a contract
 Desire among Councilors to signal that the funding will be available for the project
