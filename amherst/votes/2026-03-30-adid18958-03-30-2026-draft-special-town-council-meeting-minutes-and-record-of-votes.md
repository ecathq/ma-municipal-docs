Town of Amherst
Special Town Council Meeting pursuant to Charter Section 2.6(d)(iii)
Monday, March 30, 2026
6:30 p.m.
Town Room, Town Hall
4 Boltwood Ave. Amherst, MA 01002
Minutes
Councilors present: Cano-Martin, Churchill, Devlin Gauthier, Griesemer, Hanneke, Lord, MacLeod,
Rooney, Ryan, Schoen, Taub, Walker
Councilors participating remotely: Brevik
Councilors absent: None
Staff present: Town Manager Bockelman, Council Clerk O’Keeffe
1. Call to Order
Hanneke declared the presence of a quorum, called the meeting to order at 6:30 p.m., confirmed
Councilors could hear and be heard, and announced audio and video recording.
2. Special Public Comment Period to hear from Department of Public Works Employees about
the plethora of concerning issues they are facing
All DPW staff who wished to speak were given an opportunity to do so.
 Andrew Brace: staffing, turnover, training, bargaining process
 Mike Perez: poor building conditions
 Jon Foster: bargaining process, road conditions, poor building conditions
 Patrick McNeill: poor building conditions, road conditions, water infrastructure, cost of living in
Amherst
 Alan Snow: appreciation for Council, Town Manager, and staff; underfunded and deferred
maintenance; funding for staff and maintenance versus funding for schools, fields
3. General Public Comment
All members of the public who wished to speak were given an opportunity to do so.
 Michael Brown: spoke about the Town’s bargaining strategies, building conditions
 Maria Kopicki: spoke in support of DPW employees
 Vincent O’Connor: poor DPW building conditions; spoke critically about the Town Manager’s
executive order
 Jerry Levinsky: spoke critically about the Town’s bargaining process, building conditions
4. Presentation and Discussion
a. To explore possible solutions that are within the purview of the Council to enact
Council discussion:
 Delay in forming the DPW building committee; lack of sense of urgency in moving forward with
the DPW building project

 DPW operating budget versus water and sewer pipes, miles of roads and sidewalks maintained,
acres of fields maintained
 Swing space for DPW building, use of the space as quickly as possible, available options for
immediate use
 Water and sewer rates; increase to generate additional revenue for wages
 Settling current contract; concern about use of consultant
 Comments regarding 2016 report on DPW building conditions
 Need to budget for repairs
 Suggestion to ask higher education institutions for financial support
 Suggestion to condemn the DPW building
 Suggestion to create a regular process to hear from Town staff
 Frustration around scheduling the meeting and inability to take action at the meeting
 Walker spoke about a motion she intends to make at a future meeting regarding requesting
information from the Town Manager with the proposed FY27 water and sewer rates
 How to generate additional revenue; economic development
 Prioritizing health and safety for employees
 Turnover rate
 Expectation that new building in Town will generate revenue that will allow better maintenance
 Departments and programming added
 No state funding entity for funding municipal buildings; advocacy for state municipal and public
safety building authority
 Amherst College planned to donate property to the Town for a DPW building but the plan was
abandoned due to complaints from neighbors
 Swing space options; what would need done prior to staff occupation
 Concerns about DPW Building committee work
 Cano-Martin urged the Town Manager not to use a consultant during collective bargaining

Rooney reported on the committee’s process:
 New building site
 Committee’s discussion on alternative sites

 Request for qualifications for design services under work
 Swing space is an early need
 Upcoming meeting dates
Discussion:
 Committee time spent discussing alternative sites

 Building budget
