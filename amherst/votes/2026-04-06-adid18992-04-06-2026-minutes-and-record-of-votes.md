c. Charter Sec. 2.8 Request for Information – Water & Sewer Revenues and Expenditures
Discussion:
 Clarification of what additional information is sought
 Desire for analysis of
 Consideration of five-year road and sidewalk plan in regards to water and sewer
 Differential rates for high users
 Increasing rates in order to raise compensation
 Salary comparison
 Some employees are paid via enterprise funds; cannot use water and sewer income as a source
of revenue to raise all DPW salaries
MOTION: Pursuant to Charter Section 2.8(c), to request the Town Manager provide the following
information with the proposed FY27 water and sewer rates:
 Current and projected revenues and expenditures within the water and sewer enterprise funds
 An assessment of whether current rate structures adequately support:
o System maintenance and infrastructure needs,
o Workforce sustainability within enterprise-funded operations, including recruitment and
retention challenges within the Department of Public Works,
o Long-term financial stability of the enterprise funds;
 Consideration of the relationship between enterprise fund capacity and broader Department of
Public Works staffing challenges.
 Consideration of whether adjustments to water and sewer rates, or alternative structural
changes, may be warranted to ensure the enterprise funds are adequately resourced in an
equitable and sustainable manner.
Motion by: Walker
Seconded by: Taub
ROLL CALL VOTE: Unanimous

d. Council Actions in Response to concerns around Department of Public Works building
conditions and worker safety
1) Plan for relocating DPW employees
Discussion:
 Desire for data to ground decisions in facts
 Concern about timeframe and adequate time to prepare a plan
 Rooney stated the DPW Building Committee will discuss swing space at its next meeting
 Concern re: moving complex operations on short timeline
 Feasibility of building a structure at the Ruskin site
 Feasibility of moving staff and operations to a new site
 Complications of moving operations
 Lack of a suitable town-owned space
 Need for staff to be in proximity to one another; cannot easily divide staff between locations
 Suggestion of using mobile buildings as a temporary space
 Questions regarding costs
 Some Councilors advocated for a plan to move all employees to a temporary space
MOTION: That the Town Manager present a plan by May 4, 2026 to the Town Council that provides for
the following:
a. The relocation of at least 50% all town personnel currently assigned to the DPW building to
suitable temporary workspaces by August 1, 2026.
b. The relocation of 100% of all town personnel currently assigned to the DPW building to suitable
temporary workspaces by October 1, 2026.
c. A mechanism for current DPW staff to provide input on the relocation process and the
identification of adequate temporary facilities.
d. Regular updates to the Town Council on progress towards the goals of the plan.
That the Town Manager present a plan by June 29, 2026 to the Town Council that provides for the
following:
a. The relocation of all town personnel currently assigned to the DPW building to suitable
temporary workspaces as soon as feasible, with a goal of October 1, 2026.
b. A mechanism for current DPW staff to provide input on the relocation process and the
identification of adequate temporary facilities.
c. Provide a cost estimate and funding sources for relocation.
d. Regular updates to the Town Council on progress towards the goals of the plan.
As amended.
Motion by: Cano-Martin
Seconded by: Brevik
ROLL CALL VOTE: Unanimous

e. The relocation of all town personnel currently assigned to the DPW building to suitable

f. A mechanism for current DPW staff to provide input on the relocation process and the
