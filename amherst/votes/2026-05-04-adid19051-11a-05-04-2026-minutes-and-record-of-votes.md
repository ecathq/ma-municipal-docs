e. Proposed Acceptance of MGL Chapter 40, §71, School Bus Violation Detection Monitoring
System
Hanneke spoke to the proposal; the state adopted legislation that requires local acceptance of a school
bus monitoring law in order for school districts to implement school bus monitoring systems.
Acceptance of the MGL does not mandate use; the school committee could then choose to pursue the
option or not.
Discussion:
 Preference that the request come from the school committee
 Accepting the statute does not have a financial implication
 Community members have expressed concern with vehicles dangerously passing school busses
as students are entering or exiting busses
 Implications for the Town’s Surveillance Use Policy
 Proposed previously in the last Council term and motion to refer failed
 Concern about the ticket being issued to the vehicle owner and not necessarily the driver
 Adherence to Surveillance Bylaw
 Would require funding if the School Committee chose to pursue
 Sentiment that the request should be initiated by the School Committee
 TSO Chair unclear what the committee would discuss
 Suggestion to vote against referral in order to adopt the general law
MOTION: To refer the proposed adoption of Acceptance of MGL Chapter 40, §71, School Bus Violation
Detection Monitoring System to the Town Services and Outreach Committee, for a report and
recommendation to the Town Council by June 15, 2026.
Motion by: Hanneke
Seconded by: Ryan
ROLL CALL VOTE: 2-10, 1 abstention
Aye: Hanneke, MacLeod
Nay: Brevik, Cano-Martin, Churchill, Devlin Gauthier, Griesemer, Rooney, Ryan, Schoen, Taub, Walker
Present (abstain): Lord
Absent: None
The motion failed.

MOTION: That the City known as the Town of Amherst hereby accepts MGL Chapter 40, Section 71
enabling the city to install, operate, and maintain school bus violation detection monitoring systems on
school buses to enforce violations pursuant to sections 14 and 14C of Massachusetts General Law
Chapter 90. Any system put in place shall be limited to monitoring and detecting violations of motor
vehicle operators who fail to stop for a school bus and shall be in accordance with the Town of Amherst
Surveillance Use Bylaw.
