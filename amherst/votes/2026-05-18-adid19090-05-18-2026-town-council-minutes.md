d. Charter Review Committee Beyond the Charter Recommendations
Discussion:
 How changes could be made; 4 of the 5 remaining items would require a charter commission or
43B charter change process
 Charter commission process
 Lack of consensus on changes to form of government
 Need for public input prior to major structural changes
 Suggestion to hold public forums or a dialogue session
 Charter Review Committee recommendation to hold a public forum to hear from residents and
potentially raise energy to form a charter commission if voters wished to
Discussion on the motion:
 Support for the motion; need for conversations about democracy in general
 Desire for the session not to be restrictive to allow broad discussion
 Discussion of the structure of a public dialogue session; who would chair the meeting, how to
manage discussion
 Concern about holding a dialogue session when there is not Council support or authority to
make the changes discussed
 Holding a session over the summer when half the population is not generally in Town
 Concern that one meeting will not provide an accurate measure of community support of any of
the changes proposed in the report
 Value of holding a meeting of the full Council hosted by the Town; could be a public forum
rather than a public dialogue; create an opportunity for residents to express themselves before
making a decision or determining next steps
 Support for discussions at district meetings
 Making it clear that some changes are outside the Council power to make
MOTION: In accordance with the Charter Review Committee's recommendations, to schedule a
Listening Session within the next 6 months for the purposes of discussing the first six major structural
changes listed in section 4.a of the committee's final report.
Motion by: Brevik
Seconded by: Ryan
