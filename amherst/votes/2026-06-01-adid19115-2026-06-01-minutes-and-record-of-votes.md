8. Action Items
a. Hampshire College Property: Land Use Planning Update; authorization for Town Manager to
enter agreements with MassDevelopment; Proposed Council statement identifying key
priorities for the next phase of Hampshire College land use
Town Manager Bockelman, Planning and Economic Development Director Bagg, and MassDevelopment
Senior Vice President of Real Estate Planning Claire O’Neill gave a presentation on the ongoing work of
Planning Department staff and partnership with MassDevelopment, with the goal of creating a process
to collect information and community input, create a vision for the developable portions of the campus,
and enact zoning to implement the vision over time, and enable the property to generate much needed
new tax revenue, allow for various kinds of housing to be implemented over time, and balance
redevelopment with preservation of important resource areas while embracing the existing cultural
institutions.
https://www.amherstma.gov/DocumentCenter/View/86931/Town-Council-Land-Use-Planning-Update-
06012026
Discussion:
 Impact of Hampshire College selling their property
 Speed of public engagement process and future zoning changes
 Importance of public engagement through the planning process
 Dates of public engagement meetings
 Ability of folks to make comments remotely or asynchronously
MOTION: To authorize the Town Manager to enter into any Memorandums of Agreement with the
Massachusetts Development Finance Agency, “MassDevelopement,” and the Town of Amherst for the
purpose of assisting the Town in developing a community-informed redevelopment plan for the former
Hampshire College campus, incorporating site analysis, stakeholder engagement, redevelopment
scenarios, and recommended zoning changes to promote job creation and economic development (the
“Project”) within the Town at 893 West Street, Amherst, MA 01002 (the “Site”).
Motion by: Hanneke
Seconded by: Schoen
ROLL CALL VOTE: Unanimous
MOTION: The future uses of Hampshire College land are of critical importance to the Town of Amherst,
and comprehensive, consultative, and inclusive land planning efforts are being undertaken by staff. As
this effort continues, and as Hampshire College decides what to do with its land, the Town Council
identifies the following key priorities and stands ready to provide zoning and regulatory support for
them:
 Buildable land should be prioritized for housing, small business development, and other
appropriate tax revenue-generating uses.
 Sensitive habitat, farming, or resource areas should be prioritized for preservation, creating a
balance between potential redevelopment and preservation.
 Housing development should prioritize a wide range of housing types, as reflected in the Town’s
2021 Comprehensive Housing Policy and 2025 Housing Production Plan.
