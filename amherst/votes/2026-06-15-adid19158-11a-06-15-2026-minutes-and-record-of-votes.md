2) Adoption of FY2027 Capital Improvement Program: FY27-05A Capital Improvement Program
Schoen spoke about the Finance Committee’s review, deliberation, and recommendation to the Council
on the Capital Improvement Program and full operating budget, including extensive discussion about
funding for the elementary schools. Ultimately, the Finance Committee unanimously recommended a
reduction of $120,000 in the Capital Improvement Program in order to increase the Elementary Schools
budget by that amount.
Discussion:
 Finance Director Mangano stated he cannot firmly state which projects would be cut if the
Capital Improvement Program were reduced, however the program would be evaluated to
determine what projects are most essential and what might be deferred to next year if not
funded this year.
 Clarification of vote quantum for the motion and following motion, the need to decrease an
area of the budget to fund another area since the Town is at the levy limit

b. Adoption of Proposed Fiscal Year 2027 Water and Sewer Rates, Order FY27-10, and
Amendments to Water and Sewer Regulations – Second Read and Vote
VOTED via consent agenda serving as the Water Commission and the Sewer Commission as set by
Section 2.14 of the Amherst Home Rule Charter, to adopt Approval Order FY27-10 increasing the water
rate from $6.45/100 cubic feet to $6.75/100 cubic feet and increasing the sewer rate from $6.95/100
cubic feet to $7.30/100 cubic feet, effective July 1, 2026, and as shown on page 19 of the motions sheet.
VOTED via consent agenda in accordance with General Bylaw 3.62 Water Use Regulations, having heard
public comment specific to the proposed amendments on June 1, 2026, notice of which was posted on
the Town Bulletin Board for at least 10 days on May 28, 2026, and notice of amendments published in
the newspaper on May 29, 2026, to amend the Water Use Regulations Appendix A, as follows: By
replacing the effective date of “July 1, 2025” with “July 1, 2026”; by replacing “Domestic Water Rate
$6.45 per 100 cubic feet” with “Domestic Water Rate $6.75 per 100 cubic feet”; and by replacing
“Agricultural Water Rate $6.45 per 100 cubic feet” with “Agricultural Water Rate $6.75 per 100 cubic
feet”.
