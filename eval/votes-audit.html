votes.json — manual audit worksheet

A stratified sample of 27 motions across every year and every extraction format. Check each against its official source PDF.

How to review each card:
  1. Click Open official record to see the real PDF on amherstma.gov.
  2. Find this motion and compare the extracted votes below to what the PDF actually says.
  3. Mark it Correct, Issue, or Unsure, and jot a note for anything wrong.
  4. Your marks save in this browser. Use Copy results at the top to export them.
For the not-attributed cards, the goal is different: check whether the motion should have been attributable (i.e. the PDF clearly lists each vote) — those are extraction misses worth logging.

Sample is reproducible (seed 42). Re-generate from build_votes_dataset.py output to refresh.
