What’s in this tool

What do these words mean? (plain-English glossary)
Motion
A formal proposal the council votes on — for example, to adopt a budget, make an appointment, or change a bylaw.
Proposed it (also “moved” or “motion by”)
The councilor who put the motion forward. Every motion is started by one councilor — that’s what we call “motion sponsorship.”
Seconded it
A second councilor who formally backed putting the motion to a vote. A motion usually needs a “second” before the council can vote on it.
Voted yes / no
How each councilor voted on the motion.
Abstained
The councilor was present but chose not to take a side — often because of a conflict of interest or to step back from a particular decision.
Absent
The councilor was not present for that vote.
Unanimous / not unanimous
“Unanimous” means everyone present voted the same way. “Not unanimous” (also called contested) means at least one councilor voted no or abstained — a sign the council was divided.
Roll call
A vote where each councilor’s choice is read out and recorded by name, rather than a quick show of hands.
Sources & how to check this (where the numbers come from)
  • Where it comes from. Every figure is drawn from the Town of Amherst’s own Unofficial Record of Votes, published on amherstma.gov. Nothing here is estimated or editorialised.
  • Check any number yourself. Click a bar, slice or point on any chart to open the exact motions behind it. Each row has a Record ↗ link that opens the official meeting PDF on amherstma.gov.
  • How we make sure it’s right. A councilor’s vote is only shown when our count matches the official printed tally for that motion exactly. Motions we can’t match are left out of the per-person figures rather than guessed.
  • How fresh it is. The records are re-collected from amherstma.gov automatically, so new meetings appear shortly after they’re posted.
  • What to treat with care. Topic labels (library, budget, zoning…) are added automatically by keyword and are approximate. Some older meetings used wording our reader couldn’t match, so they aren’t counted.
Citing a record: “Amherst Town Council — Unofficial Record of Votes, [meeting date], amherstma.gov.” The Record ↗ link is the permanent address for that document.
